# Wonder Man

Sources: [official rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc58_rulesheet-web_1.pdf), [FFG mirror](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/08/67/08678ae5-1d4b-454e-a2dc-33bd607dbe58/mc58_rulesheet-web_1.pdf), Rules Reference v1.8, and [official rulings reproduced by Hall of Heroes](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/).

## Rulesheet clarifications

Ionic Physiology cannot hold more than three cards. Its tuck/heal response cannot resolve while it already holds three. Its alternate card back distinguishes this permanent card from ordinary deck cards; it begins outside the forty-card starter deck.

Cameo searches the player's collection outside the current game for a signature ally belonging to an identity absent from this game. It does not search the encounter Collection zone.

When overpaying, the player assigns generated resources between the card's effective resource cost and the excess resources. Energy icons need not pay the ordinary cost first. Wild resources count as the resource types their payer declares.

## Rules and rulings applied, 2026-11-02

The user authorized consulting official rules and recent rulings without asking for decisions.

- **Ionic Physiology erratum:** RR v1.8, Appendix V, replaces the cost arrow with “Then.” Wonder Man can tuck an eligible event while undamaged. Healing occurs only after successful tucking.
- Tucked cards are faceup and out of play. They are not attached, do not count as controlled cards, and leave their former zone. Discarding all cards after a basic attack does not occur after an event attack.
- Power Recycling counts physical cards currently tucked under Ionic Physiology. Printed energy means the icon in the card's resource area; a printed wild icon does not qualify.
- Energy Siphon's additional icons require actual damage taken as its cost. Toughness, prevention, and reduction cannot generate unpaid bonus resources. Its printed energy resource remains independent of the optional interrupt.
- “What Are You?” replaces defeat and sets remaining hit points to four; it does not heal four damage. Its form change can trigger form-change responses. Resolve optional tucking before removing the upgrade.
- Avengers Compound permits paying for its tucked ally as though in hand; it does not put that ally directly into play without paying.
