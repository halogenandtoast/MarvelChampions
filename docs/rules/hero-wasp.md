## Foldable Cards

Nadia Van Dyne/Wasp's identity card is a foldable, "three-sided" card. One side is her alter-ego form, one side is her Tiny hero form, and the inside of the card is her Giant hero form. Changing form with a three-sided card follows the standard rules for changing form found in the Rules Reference.

## New Keyword: Team-Up

The team-up keyword names two characters. To include a card with the team-up keyword in your deck, your chosen identity must match one of the named characters. Additionally, a card with the team-up keyword cannot be played unless both of the named characters (hero or ally) are in play.

## New Keyword: Piercing

An attack with the piercing keyword discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack before dealing damage.

## Rules Clarifications

**Q: How does changing between three forms work?** A: Nadia Van Dyne/Wasp can change from alter-ego form to either hero form, from either hero form to alter-ego form, or from one hero form to the other hero form.

**Q: What happens when a basic thwart power or thwart action divides threat removal among schemes?** A: Each scheme is considered to have been thwarted. In these instances, follow the standard rules for thwarting schemes.

**Q: What happens when a basic attack power or attack action divides damage among enemies?** A: Each enemy is considered to have been attacked. In these instances, follow the standard rules for attacking enemies.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-26)

- All for One: the hero is an Avenger character you control, and may be exhausted for it.
- Beetle's Forced Interrupt: the player who defeated him chooses, and spends the [physical] resource; with none to spend, Beetle is shuffled into the encounter deck.
- Rapid Growth in Giant form: no form change, the power still gets +2 (resolve as much as possible).
- Small but Mighty: "Wasp" is her basic powers and her hero's own abilities, plus the events she plays; not her upgrades, supports or allies.
- Pym Particles (13007) has 12006's text and is the same card.

## Readings taken in implementation (not yet ruled on)

- Bio-Synthetic Wings: "Wasp gains the Aerial trait" is the hero, in either form; Nadia Van Dyne does not.
- G.I.R.L.: "up to 2" allows none, and then the deck is not shuffled.
- Athletic Conditioning, Into the Fray, Running Interference may be played when they would do nothing (no status card, no main scheme threat removable); nothing in the RR forbids it.
- Wasp (Janet Van Dyne): what was paid beyond her cost is the overpay, energy resources (a wild one declared as energy) counted first.
- Beetle shuffled into the encounter deck is not defeated: no "after defeated" window opens for him.
- A per-instance bonus (Embiggen!, Shrink) applies to each enemy's or scheme's share of a divided effect.
