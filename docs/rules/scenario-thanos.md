## Summary (non-normative)

```
Villain deck: Thanos (I), (II); expert (II), (III). Main schemes: The Infinity Stones (1A/1B), Balance the Scales
  (2A/2B). Encounter deck: Thanos, Infinity Gauntlet, Black Order, Children of Thanos, Standard (+ Expert).
Infinity Gauntlet (any scenario with exactly one villain at the start): attached to the villain at setup; the six
  Infinity Stone environments are its own facedown deck with its own discard pile, reshuffled without penalty
  when empty. After the attached villain activates against you, each Infinity Stone in play resolves its
  Special (and goes to the stone discard pile); with none in play, the top stone is put into play instead.
1B When Revealed: the top stone into play; Sanctuary revealed. While Sanctuary is in play, Thanos cannot take
  damage from player cards; defeating it lets each player spend up to 3 [physical] from hand for 2 damage each,
  ignoring tough.
Thanos: stalwart (II, III toughness, and they fetch Thanos's Helmet); each time the stone deck runs out he gets a
  facedown boost card. Thanos's Armor takes 1 off every damage; Master of the Stones puts a stone into play as he
  activates and is discarded at the end of that activation.

  completed: the players lose. Thanos's last stage defeated: the players win.
```

## Scenario
<!-- MTS rulebook p.16 -->

**SCENARIO #3 - THANOS**

*The Black Order's assault on Avengers Tower was brutal, but the Avengers stood firm in the defense. The tide of
battle turned when the Guardians of the Galaxy arrived to lift the seige. The combined forces of the Guardians and
the Avengers was enough to drive back the armies of Thanos. Yet in the midst of that chaos, the Mad Titan used the
Space Stone to teleport directly into Avengers Tower. Once inside, he seized the Reality Stone and teleported back
to his enormous space ship, Sanctuary.*

*With all six Infinity Stones in his possession, it is only a matter of time before Thanos masters the power of
the Infinity Gauntlet and wreaks havoc on the universe. You only have one shot at stopping him before it's too
late!*

**Villain Deck**: Thanos (I), Thanos (II)

*Remove Thanos (I) and add Thanos (III) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: The Infinity Stones, Balance the Scales

**Encounter Deck**: Thanos, Infinity Gauntlet, Black Order, Children of Thanos, and Standard sets. (The Standard set
can be found in the *Marvel Champions* core set.)

*The Black Order and Children of Thanos sets can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when
using the scenario customization rules. The Infinity Gauntlet set may be used in other scenarios, but it is
required when playing Thanos.*

## The Infinity Gauntlet Encounter Set
<!-- MTS rulebook p.16 -->

The Infinity Gauntlet is one of the most powerful artifacts in the Marvel universe. To represent its unique and
unprecedented power, the Infinity Gauntlet has its own encounter set for *Marvel Champions: The Card Game*. This
special modular set can be used in most *Marvel Champions: The Card Game* scenarios by using the following rules:

When using the Infinity Gauntlet set in a scenario, attach the Infinity Gauntlet attachment card to the villain
during setup. If there is more than one villain (or no villain) in play at the start of the game, The Infinity
Gauntlet set cannot be used.

After attaching the Infinity Gauntlet to the villain, shuffle the six **Infinity Stone** environment cards together
and set them aside, facedown. This is the "**Infinity Stone** deck." The **Infinity Stone** deck has its own discard
pile. When an **Infinity Stone** environment is discarded, it is placed in the **Infinity Stone** deck discard pile.
If the **Infinity Stone** deck is ever empty, shuffle the **Infinity Stone** deck discard pile back into the
**Infinity Stone** deck. There is no built-in penalty for doing this.

Each **Infinity Stone** environment has a unique **Special** effect that is triggered by the Infinity Gauntlet when
the attached villain activates. If there are no **Infinity Stone** environment cards in play when the villain
activates, the Infinity Gauntlet's ability places the top card of the **Infinity Stone** deck into play instead.

## Campaign Instructions
<!-- MTS rulebook p.17 -->

**SETUP:**

- Put the "Hack Sanctuary's Computer" side scheme into play.
- If Cosmo is in the campaign pool, put him into play under the first player's control.
- If Security Breach is in the campaign pool, shuffle it into the encounter deck.
- If Shawarma is in the campaign pool, each player shuffles one copy of Shawarma into their deck.
- If Black Swan is in the campaign pool, put her into play engaged with the first player.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the
  campaign log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may place one acceleration token on the main scheme to heal their identity
  to its full hit point value.
  - If Avengers Tower had the ***Damaged*** trait, deal three damage to each identity.

**VICTORY:**

- If Defensive Protocols is NOT in the victory display, add System Shock to the campaign pool.
- If The Infinity Stones 1B was completed, record that in the campaign log.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points in the campaign log.

## Important Keywords (excerpt)
<!-- MTS rulebook p.13 -->

The keywords of the box that these cards use, from its "Important Keywords" list:

**Hinder X**: When a card with the hinder X keyword is revealed, place X threat on that card.

**Incite X**: When a card with the incite X keyword is revealed, place X threat on the main scheme.

**Permanent**: A card with the permanent keyword cannot leave play.

**Piercing**: An attack with the piercing keyword discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack
before dealing damage.

**Setup**: A card with the setup keyword begins the game in play.

**Stalwart**: A character with the stalwart keyword cannot be stunned or confused.

**Villainous**: When a minion with the villainous keyword activates, give it a facedown boost card from the top of
the encounter deck. When you resolve that minion's activation, turn the boost card faceup and apply its boost icons
to the minion's stats for the activation. If the boost card has a boost ability, resolve its effects. Discard the
boost card after the activation.

**Victory X**: When a card with the victory X keyword is defeated, place it in the victory display instead of its
owner's discard pile. The victory display is a named, out-of-play game area shared by all players. Cards in the
victory display follow the standard rules for out-of-play cards.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **The Infinity Stone deck** is a deck of the scenario's own, beside the encounter deck, with its own discard pile:
  a stone discarded from play, or from the deck's top, goes to that pile. Emptied, it is made again from its
  discard pile at once, with no acceleration token. It is shown on the board facedown, with its size and its
  discard pile.
- **"The Infinity Stone deck runs out"**: its last card leaves it (put into play, or discarded by "I Am
  Inevitable"). That happens once each time the deck empties, before it is made again; an empty deck with an empty
  discard pile (every stone in play) does not run out again until it has had a card.
- **"Activates against you"**: the villain's attack against you, or his scheme in your alter-ego's activation (in
  the villain phase or by a card such as Avatar of Death). The Gauntlet's response comes after the activation has
  fully resolved, its boost cards discarded. A stalwart villain is never stunned or confused, so for Thanos every
  activation counts; a stunned or confused villain of another scenario does not activate.
- **Specials** resolve for "you", the player the activation was against, one stone after the other in the order
  they entered play, each going to the stone discard pile as its Special ends.
- **Master of the Stones**: as Thanos's activation begins, the top stone is put into play (so the Gauntlet resolves
  it at the end of that activation); as the activation ends it is discarded, before the Gauntlet responds.
- **Sanctuary**: damage to Thanos from a player card (a hero, alter-ego, ally, event, upgrade or support, or an
  effect one of them created) is not taken while it is in play. Its own When Defeated damage is the side scheme's,
  dealt as one amount (2 for each resource), still reduced by Thanos's Armor.
- **Thanos's Armor** reduces every damage, of any source, by 1. A tough status card comes first (RR Damage): Thanos
  with tough loses only the tough status card, whatever the amount.
- **"I Am Inevitable"**'s boost: the discarded stone's boost icons count toward the activation as a boost card's
  would (amplify included); the stone goes to the stone discard pile.
- **Ebony Maw**'s boost: "this enemy" is the one activating, Ebony Maw or the villain.

## Open rulings

- **Sanctuary, "from their hand"**: the engine's payment also offers resources from cards in play unless the cost
  can be limited to the hand; the printed restriction is to the hand.
- **Sanctuary's damage**: one amount of 2 per resource (reduced once by Thanos's Armor), "for each" multiplying it.
- **"Player cards"**: hero or alter-ego, ally, event, upgrade, support, and effects they created; obligations and
  encounter cards are not.
- **Several Specials**: resolved in the order the stones entered play; the card does not set an order.
- **Armor and Helmet's Hero Response**: offered to the player whose hero made the basic attack.
- **The Titan's Throne**: "an Infinity Stone" in play is an Infinity Stone environment (the Galaxy's Most Wanted
  Power Stone is a permanent attachment and could not be discarded anyway).
- **Balance the Scales**: MarvelCDB places its When Revealed on 2B and the loss on 2A; both sides are revealed back
  to back, so it resolves as stage 2 is revealed, and completing stage 2 loses the game.
- **The Infinity Gauntlet with no villain at setup** (Loki's scenario, which requires the set): it attaches to the
  villain revealed during setup. With more than one villain at the start (The Wrecking Crew) the Gauntlet and the
  stones are put out of play.
- **"Runs out" by I Am Inevitable**: the discarded last card empties the deck: Thanos gets his boost card, then the
  discard pile (that stone included) is shuffled back.
- **The Mad Titan's boost**: an ally defeated by the attack's own damage counts.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-28)

Orchestrator decisions that apply here, and the plan's readings adopted:

- **"Spend … from their hand"** (Sanctuary): paid only with cards from the hand; no resource ability of a card in
  play is offered (`ResourcesFromHandCost`).
- **Simultaneous forced abilities**: the Infinity Stones' Specials are ordered by the Gauntlet, in the order the
  stones entered play; none is asked.
- **Two main schemes** (Tower Defense, which has two villains, so the Gauntlet is not used there): "the main
  scheme" on Deviant Syndrome and the Time Stone places its threat on each main scheme.
- **Sanctuary's damage** is one amount of 2 per resource spent, the side scheme's, reduced once by Thanos's Armor.
- **"Player cards"**: hero or alter-ego, ally, event, upgrade, support, and effects they created.
- **Thanos's Armor** reduces every amount of damage by 1, after the tough status card (same result as RR Damage).
- **Armor and Helmet's Hero Response**: offered to the player whose hero made the basic attack.
- **The Titan's Throne**: an Infinity Stone environment.
- **Balance the Scales**: its When Revealed resolves as stage 2 is revealed; completing it loses the game.
- **The Infinity Gauntlet**: with exactly one villain at the start it is attached to him; with none (Loki), it
  attaches to the villain as he enters play; with more than one (The Wrecking Crew, Tower Defense) it and its
  stones are put out of play.
- **"Runs out"**: once each time the last card leaves the stone deck (put into play, or discarded by "I Am
  Inevitable"), before its discard pile is shuffled back.
- **The Mad Titan's boost**: an ally the attack's own damage defeats counts.
- **Activation**: an attack against you (or your ally) or a scheme in your alter-ego's activation, Avatar of Death's
  included; Master of the Stones discards and the Gauntlet responds after it, in either order.
- **Gauntlet with no stone in play and an empty stone deck**: nothing happens.
- **Ebony Maw (minion)'s boost**: "this enemy" is the enemy activating.
- **Corvus Glaive, Proxima Midnight, Reality Stone** with nothing to discard: nothing is asked; a permanent card is
  not offered.
