## Scenario #5 — Ronan the Accuser
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.18 -->

*With a sigh of relief, you kick back in your pilot seat and set course for Knowhere. You have the Power Stone and
you've captured Nebula. After a discussion with the other Guardians, you decide it best to hand both over to the
authorities... because it's the right thing to do.*

*The reward money may also be an incentive.*

*As Knowhere comes into sight, a massive Kree warship suddenly drops out of hyperspace in front of you, intercepting
your course. Ronan the Accuser. He sends a brief transmission through your comms relay—something about standing
accused? Yeah, that seems appropriate.*

*Then, he attacks.*

**Villain Deck**: Ronan the Accuser (I), Ronan the Accuser (II)

*Remove Ronan the Accuser (I) and add Ronan the Accuser (III) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: Interception Imminent (1A/1B), "Take What Is Mine" (2A/2B)

**Encounter Deck**: Ronan the Accuser, Kree Militants, Power Stone, Ship Command, and Standard encounter sets.

*The Kree Militants modular set can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when using
scenario customization rules.*

### Campaign Instructions

**Setup:**

- (Optional) Reveal the Kree Supremacy (182A) side scheme (use the reverse side for expert mode).
- If a player's identity was recorded in the "Power Stone Control" section of the campaign log, search the encounter
  deck and discard pile for one copy of the "You Stand Accused!" (116) treachery, then deal that card to that player
  as a facedown encounter card.
- Search the encounter deck and discard pile for one copy of the Pincer Maneuver (112) side scheme and reveal it.
  Place X[per_hero] additional threat on Pincer Maneuver, where X is equal to 3 minus the recorded number in the
  Evasion Counters section of the campaign log. *(For example, if 2 evasion counters were recorded in the campaign
  log, X will be 1, as 3 minus 2 is 1.)*
- Shuffle the Badoon Headhunter (183) minion into the encounter deck. Then, if the number of marks in the
  "Headhunter Defeated?" section in the campaign log is equal to or greater than:
  - (1) Shuffle the On the Hunt (184) treachery into the encounter deck.
  - (2) Shuffle the Dead to Rights (185) treachery into the encounter deck.
  - (3) Shuffle the Headhunter's Henchman (186) minion into the encounter deck.
  - (4) Shuffle the Fugitive Recovery (187) side scheme into the encounter deck.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign
  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may subtract 1 unit from their respective "Unspent Units" box in the campaign
  log to heal their identity to its printed hit point value.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Place an additional 1[per_hero] threat on the main scheme.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: If the players lose this game, Ronan the Accuser claims the Power Stone and the players
  lose the campaign.

**Victory:**

- Ronan the Accuser is defeated and the players win the campaign! Read the conclusion on the next page!

## How the engine reads these rules

- **The scenario** is set up by Ronan (I)'s code, `16103`, Kree Militants its recommended modular set; expert starts
  at Ronan (II) and adds the expert set. Ronan (I) defeated, he advances to (II); in standard (II) defeated wins, in
  expert (II) advances to (III), whose defeat wins.
- **Setup (1A)**: the Milano (setup keyword) is in play already and the Power Stone on Ronan; 1A's setup puts the Kree
  Command Ship into play from the encounter deck, puts the Universal Weapon into play (it attaches itself to Ronan),
  and then attaches the Power Stone to the first player. A villain stage's "When Revealed" resolves as setup ends,
  so expert Ronan (II) reveals Cut the Power then.
- **Toughness** on a villain: each stage with it enters play with a tough status card (it is set up, or the villain
  advances to it); one tough already stays one.
- **Ronan's Forced Interrupt**: as he begins an attack against a player (an ally of theirs included), or schemes in
  the active player's activation, he is dealt one more boost card if that player controls the Power Stone. An attack
  "that does not get a boost card" is dealt no additional one.
- **"Take What Is Mine" (2B)**: while the Power Stone is on a villain the scheme has `ThreatCannotBeRemoved`: no
  thwart of it is offered and no threat comes off it, by thwart or otherwise. Completed, the players lose.
- **First Player Action / Interrupt** (1B, Pincer Maneuver, Kree Command Ship): only the first player may use them,
  the Milano exhausted as the cost (whoever controls it); the Kree Command Ship's resource is any other one. Its
  cancel is the engine's "cancel the When Revealed": surge (a When Revealed ability) and the rest of the card are not
  resolved, and the card is discarded.
- **Universal Weapon**'s cost is paid in full (2 damage, then the facedown encounter card); the card goes to the
  encounter deck, not to the discard pile, and Ronan loses stalwart with it. Turned up as a boost card it enters play
  attached to Ronan.
- **Fanaticism**'s fury counters are placed as it attaches: 1 + 1 per player. The counter comes off at the end of the
  attack it armed; with the last one gone, it is discarded.
- **Cut the Power**'s boost is chosen by the player the activation is against; the Milano only if it is in play and
  ready (RR Choose).
- **Superior Tactics** gives the Power Stone, while it is on the villain, `CannotBeUnattached`: an attachment with it
  is not moved to another character, the Power Stone's own Forced Response included.
- **Single-Minded Fury**: with no player holding the Power Stone (it is on Ronan), or with Ronan's stun removed in
  place of the attack, no attack is made and the card surges.
- **Kree Combat Armor** attaches to the enemy, villain or minion, with the highest ATK as it is revealed, the player
  revealing it choosing among those tied (RR Choose: the player resolving the ability chooses). "Spend 3 resources of
  the same type" is one Hero Action for each type (physical, mental, energy), [wild] paying for any.
- **Kree Lieutenant**'s boost adds 3 boost icons to the activation only while it is an attack; Kree Commando's and
  Kree Private's boosts likewise give only an attack their keyword.

## Campaign hooks (the campaign implements its steps)

- Main scheme stage: the main scheme's card code (`16106` / `16107`), as for every scenario.
- The Power Stone: `getPowerStoneHolder` (who holds it, for "Power Stone Control").
- The campaign's searches find the cards by their definitions: `Marvel.SideScheme.Cards.pincerManeuver` (reveal it
  with `searchForAndRevealScheme`, then place the extra threat on it) and `Marvel.Treachery.Cards.youStandAccused`.

## Open rulings

- **An attack "that does not get a boost card"** (`VillainAttacksWithoutBoost`) is not given Ronan's additional boost
  card: "additional" is read as one more than the attack gets.
- **Kree Lieutenant's +3 boost icons and amplify**: an amplify icon adds one icon to each boost card turned up, not to
  icons a card's ability adds; the +3 are not amplified further.
- **"Activates against you" when Ronan schemes**: the player whose activation it is (the active player).
