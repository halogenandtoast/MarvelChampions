# Nightcrawler Hero Pack

Sources: [official rules insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/e3/7b/e37bce25-fb98-4d80-91cd-64f13284972c/mc48_nightcrawler_rules_insert.pdf), local Rules Reference v1.8 and [FAQ/errata](faq-errata.md), [official pack announcement](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2024/5/23/rapid-teleportation/).

## Rules insert

Player side schemes enter beside the main scheme with their starting threat. One may be in play at tables starting with one or two players; two at tables starting with three or four. They otherwise work as side schemes. If the limit is exceeded, the first player selects schemes to discard until it is satisfied.

Alliance allows other players to help pay card costs. Tucked cards sit faceup underneath their host, out of play, and are discarded when the host leaves. Victory X sends a defeated card to the victory display, where it is worth X points.

The pack combines Bamf! defenses, Tally Ho! damage and recovery of the discarded Bamf!, and Protection rewards for defending without damage. The included bonus encounter set is the Crazy Gang.

## Rules and sourced rulings (2026-11-01)

The project owner delegated rules decisions to published rules and rulings while asleep.

- **Bamf!** declares a basic defense, applies Nightcrawler's DEF, and can be used while exhausted. It does not ready or exhaust him. Defense restrictions still apply. The exact discarded physical Bamf! is retained for Tally Ho!'s response; multiple responses may share a trigger, but cannot retrieve a card no longer in the discard pile.
- **Rogue** uses the other character's **base** THW/ATK, following v1.8 errata, rather than the obsolete MarvelCDB wording “printed”. The chosen character remains a live reference through the round: traits and base powers track changes, and the bonus disappears if that character leaves play (FFG ruling #40, November 19, 2024).
- **Tweedledope** has one boost icon and no boost star, following v1.8 errata.
- **Kurt's Cutlasses** consumes two restricted slots. Prehensile Tail increases the restricted limit by one. Its resource pays only an event's play cost, not an action printed on an event.
- **Scout Ahead** must select a different scheme for its optional second removal, even if the first remains in play.
- **Alliance** permits contributions to both resource and non-resource costs. The two exhausted characters are distinct; Gunboat Diplomacy uses their combined current THW.
- **Riposte** damage is a delayed effect after the attack and shares timing priority with retaliate, so its controller chooses their order.
- **Brimstone Strike** does not retrigger Quickstrike when Azazel is already engaged with its revealer (FFG #47, November 23, 2024).
- **Find Azazel** uses the find rules across all ordinary zones and may relocate Azazel already in play. Brimstone Strike's alter-ego clause schemes against the revealing player after the reveal and any Quickstrike resolves as applicable.

Bamf!, Tail, Rogue, Azazel and Riposte clarifications are recorded in the [FFG rulings archive](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) (September 26, November 19 and November 23, 2024). Local v1.8 is authoritative where later errata differ.

## Starter deck

Nightcrawler's 15 signature cards, the Protection cards numbered 12–20 in their printed quantities, Gambit, Moira MacTaggert, Energy, Genius, and Strength form the 40-card Protection starter. Combine Forces, Gunboat Diplomacy and the Crazy Gang are extras.
