# Fear No Evil — player cards

Source: [Fear No Evil rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc60_rulebook-web.pdf), featured keywords p. 3, starter decks pp. 24–25, Sense deck and FAQ p. 26; Rules Reference v1.8. Expansion scenarios, campaign rules, obligations and nemesis cards are excluded.

## Starting and discount

A deck can contain at most one Starting card. Before drawing an opening hand, its owner may find it and add it as the first card of that hand. It counts toward the opening hand size and can be mulliganed normally.

Discount X (trait) reduces a card's resource cost when its player's identity has a specified trait. Multiple listed traits are alternatives; satisfying more than one does not multiply the discount.

## Daredevil's Sense deck

Shuffle the five Sense upgrades at setup and put their separate deck faceup beside the identity. Its owner may inspect it but may not reorder it. Daredevil's hero action plays only its top card, paying its cost; other cards explicitly permit selecting any Sense upgrade. Searching this deck does not shuffle it. A Sense upgrade leaving play goes to the bottom of this deck. This replacement is printed on both identity sides; MarvelCDB's hero text omits it.

Only the controller of a Sense upgrade may trigger its abilities. Removing a scheme's last threat occurs before its defeat, so an Acute Tactility attached to Focus the Senses can return to the Sense deck before Focus the Senses resolves.

## Echo and Photographic Reflexes

Tucked events are faceup, out of play and retain their physical identities and owners. Echo may tuck an eligible event played by another player, from that player's discard pile. When she exceeds three tucked cards, her player chooses which to discard until only three remain.

Photographic Reflexes can replay a tucked event at that event's ordinary action, interrupt or response opportunity. It discards one actual Reflexes card from hand, reduces the replayed event's cost by two, and prevents Watch and Learn for that event only.

Temporary upgrades are discarded at the end of the round. The Best Offense replaces the values of ATK and THW with modified DEF, including for card effects that read those stats.

## Printed card checks

Cloak has zero consequential damage on both THW and ATK, verified against the printed card image (MarvelCDB 60020). His acceleration icon supplies his drawback. Dagger has two consequential damage on both powers, reduced to one while Cloak is in play. Focus the Senses and De-escalation each enter with a fixed four threat, independently of player count.

### Ally defenses and basic powers

Allies defend by exhausting, but do not make a basic defense: they have no DEF power. Thus Daredevil (60039) does not trigger after defending, and Stick does not interrupt an ally defending. The Rules Reference v1.8 Basic Power and Defend entries distinguish these cases. FFG's Alex confirmed this in the MACH-IV ruling dated May 22, 2025: “allies cannot make basic defenses.” Source: [FFG rulings collected by Hall of Heroes](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/).
