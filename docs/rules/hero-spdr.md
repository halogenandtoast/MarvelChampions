# SP//dr Hero Pack (MC31)

Sources: [official MC31 rulesheet, p.1](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc31_spdr_rulesheet.pdf#page=1)
(p.2 is blank); Rules Reference v1.8; local MarvelCDB data, codes 31001–31037;
[printed starter-deck card](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/z1.jpg).
This document summarizes the rules for implementation. Card text is in
`frontend/public/data/cards.json`.

## Separated identity

<!-- MC31 rulesheet p.1 -->

Begin with Peni Parker's alter-ego face in play. Her setup puts the Suit's
Inactive support face into play. Changing to hero flips Peni to the SP//dr
Interface upgrade and the Suit to its Active hero face. Changing to alter-ego
reverses both flips. They share one hit point dial; damage persists across forms.
Defeating either identity defeats both and eliminates that player.

The two physical cards have independent ready/exhausted states. Exhausting the
Inactive Suit for Maintenance does not exhaust Peni. Exhausting Peni for recovery
means the SP//dr Interface remains exhausted after changing to hero. Likewise,
exhausting the hero Suit does not exhaust the SP//dr Interface. Flipping alone
does not ready a card (RR “Ready”, “Exhaust”, “Flip”).

## Permanent, patrol and steady

<!-- MC31 rulesheet p.1; RR v1.8 pp.32,41 -->

Patrol prevents its engaged player from thwarting the main scheme. Steady needs
two matching status cards to stun or confuse; when they replace an activation,
remove both matching cards. The current [permanent rule](glossary-p-r.md)
protects against defeat, leaving play and blanking by cards outside the same set,
and excludes permanent cards from deck size. The explicit printed blanking
protection on both permanent SP//dr faces also applies.

## Return to Base errata

<!-- RR v1.8 Appendix V, SP//dr Hero Pack, p.68 -->

The [current errata](faq-errata.md) changes Return to Base so that counters and
attached cards move **from the Suit to Peni Parker** when changing to alter-ego.
Suit Up moves them in the opposite direction when changing to hero. These
explicit instructions override the normal different-card-type flip rule that
discards attached cards, tucked cards, status cards and tokens (RR “Flip”).

An [archived FFG ruling](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/)
confirms that Sync Ratio may be used repeatedly in the same payment window,
exhausting a different ready Interface each time, and that status cards move
with attached cards. That archive predates RR v1.5; current errata takes
precedence over its original Return to Base interpretation.

## Spider-Man Noir FAQ

<!-- RR v1.8 Appendix IV, SP//dr Hero Pack, p.63 -->

[The FAQ](faq-errata.md) requires some part of the treachery to resolve before
Noir can attach it. Canceling its When Revealed effects prevents this response
if nothing else resolves. Resolving a keyword such as surge or incite still
permits the response. Attach at most three treacheries facedown; X equals their
number. Another controlled Web-Warrior is required when triggering the response.

## Card and timing notes

Printed consequential damage differs significantly among the allies: Daredevil
has THW 2 with two consequential damage and ATK 1 with one; Noir has one for
each power; Spider-Ham has zero for both powers; Otto has THW 1 with one and
ATK 2 with two. VEN#m has THW 1 and ATK 1, each with one consequential damage.

Rapid Deployment's second thwart may choose the same scheme or another eligible
scheme; it is a separate threat-removal instance. Web-Trap's stun depends on
Sync Ratio payment, rather than on dealing damage. Rapid Deployment and Web-Trap
check resources actually paid, excluding surplus generation (RR “Cost”). If
identical resource types were generated by Sync Ratio and another source, the
payer chooses which sources pay the cost. VEN#m counts resources
produced by Sync Ratio toward her payment; ordinary Interface cards discarded
from hand do not qualify. This counts generated resources, including surplus
generation: RR “Resources”, p.37, permits generating at least the cost and calls
the surplus resources generated toward that cost, then loses them. The card
counts generation rather than resources actually spent.

Repurpose uses the discarded upgrade's printed cost. Its modifier ends at the
end of the round. Giant Monster Attack charges its extra energy for a thwart,
including an ally's thwart; non-thwart threat removal has no such cost.
Bombshell divides attack damage directly among the attacked player's characters;
this is distinct from indirect damage, which has its own allocation restrictions
(RR “Indirect Damage”).

The Pilot remains one of the two separated identity cards. RR “Identity”, p.23,
prohibits discarding identity cards from play, so Inherited Burden cannot discard
the Pilot even though the current Permanent rule has a same-set exception.

## Starter deck

The [printed deck card](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/z1.jpg)
lists fifteen shuffled signature cards: VEN#m; All Systems Go! ×3; Rapid
Deployment ×2; Web-Trap ×2; Aunt May & Uncle Ben; Ejection Protocol; SP//dr Command;
Host Spider; Psychic Link; Speed-Metal Alloy; and Web-Fluid Compressor.

The seventeen Protection cards are Daredevil, Spider-Man Noir, Repurpose ×3,
Thwip Thwip! ×3, Energy Barrier ×3, Forcefield Generator ×3 and Spider-Tingle ×3.
The eight basic cards are Spider-Ham, Spider-Man (Otto Octavius), Limitless
Stamina ×3 and Unshakable ×3. These forty cards are codes 31003–31024 at their
printed quantities. There are no core double-resource cards. The two separated
identity cards begin outside the shuffled deck. Inherited Burden is the obligation;
the nemesis set contains Giant Monster Attack, M.O.R.B.I.U.S. and Energy Drain ×3.

Clarity of Purpose is an additional deck-building card. Iron Spider's Sinister
Six is a separate eight-card modular encounter set: Grand Larceny, Bombshell,
Electro, Hobgoblin, Iron Spider, Sandman, Spot and Surge in Crime.

## Bombshell attack calculation

Under [enemy attack steps 4–5](round-sequence.md), calculate attack damage and
subtract a basic defending hero's DEF before dealing damage. Bombshell's printed
ability then divides that calculated damage as evenly as possible among the
attacked player's characters, replacing the normal single recipient at step 5.
The attacked player is the defending player's controller if another player took
over the defense (step 2). After division, each recipient resolves its own tough
and damage-prevention effects; those effects modify damage taken, not the damage
already calculated and divided (RR “Damage”, p.14). This is direct attack damage,
so the indirect-damage assignment cap at remaining hit points does not apply.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-30)

- Use the official rules and current errata.

### Surplus Sync Ratio resources

The owner ruled: “Count generated resources, including surplus (matches VEN#m’s
wording).” VEN#m therefore counts every Sync Ratio resource generated toward her
payment, including lost surplus. Rapid Deployment and Web-Trap instead require
at least one Sync Ratio resource among the resources actually paid. Identical
resource types from different sources may be allocated by the payer.
