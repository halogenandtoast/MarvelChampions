## New Keyword: Team-Up

The team-up keyword names two characters. To include a card with the team-up keyword in your deck, your chosen identity must match one of the named characters. Additionally, a card with the team-up keyword cannot be played unless both of the named characters (hero or ally) are in play.

## Rules Clarification

**Q: Scarlet Witch's Chaos Control ability and her Scarlet Witch's Crest upgrade "count" boost icons. What does it mean to count boost icons?** A: Boost icons are counted whenever a game step or card ability determines the exact number of boost icons on an encounter card.

## FAQ (RR v1.8 p.61)

**Hex Bolt (#4) — Q: For the second sentence of Hex Bolt, do the bulleted abilities resolve each time a card is discarded from the encounter deck, or do the bulleted abilities resolve after all 3 cards have been discarded?** A: As Hex Bolt does not contain an alteration effect, resolve the first sentence of Hex Bolt entirely, without interruption. Then, determine and resolve the appropriate bulleted abilities based off of what was discarded.

**Slipping Sanity (#23) — Q: Is it intentional that Scarlet Witch has two obligation cards?** A: Yes. Scarlet Witch's powers are chaotic, often working in her favor, though also sometimes working against her. While she has many cards within her personal set that represent the positive facets of her powers, having multiple obligations represents the negative facets.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-26)

- Chaos Control and Scarlet Witch's Crest on the same count: the last one used wins. Chaos Control replaces the whole count (a ±1 from the Crest used before it is lost); the Crest used after Chaos Control adjusts the new count.

## Readings from the rules (not asked)

- A count is "the exact number of boost icons on an encounter card" (rulesheet): a boost card's icons added to an activation, and every "for each boost icon discarded" / "equal to the number of boost icons". Whether a card has boost icons at all (Attacrobatics' condition) is no count; it still sees The Next Evolution's +1.
- The Next Evolution's +1 applies to every encounter card, wherever it is, including the card Chaos Control discards. Stars are not boost icons (Slipping Sanity counts stars, untouched by it).
- Chaos Magic plays the card "ignoring its resource cost": no resources are paid, Magical Suspension's +1 included (RR Ignore). It discards as many encounter cards as the card's printed cost.
- Superpowered Siblings: "Pietro Maximoff is in play" is the Quicksilver ally (his subtitle), or an identity whose face-up title is Pietro Maximoff (as the Quicksilver ruling for Wanda).
- Slipping Sanity's first option needs Wanda Maximoff in play and ready (RR Target, Choose (Option)); both copies go into the encounter deck at setup (FAQ).
- Hex Bolt resolves its bullets in the order written, each discarded card once.
- Molecular Decay's 5 and its additional damage are one instance of damage.
- Wiccan: each boost icon deals 1 damage to an enemy the player chooses, one at a time.
- Warp Reality cancels everything the revealed card would do, surge and incite included; it does not enter play, and no replacement card is revealed.
- Turn the Tide answers any thwart by your hero (basic, event, ability, each scheme of a divided thwart) that leaves the scheme with no threat.
- Magical Suspension's Hero Action is the attached player's only (RR Ability: an attachment saying "your").
- The Next Evolution starts with 2 [per player] threat.
- Chaos Magic needs a card in hand that could be played ignoring its cost (RR Target); attack events need an enemy that can be attacked (Browbeat and Swift Retribution, the villain); Recuperation needs damage to heal.
- I See You's "+1 boost icon" is added to the activation, not to an encounter card, so it is no count Chaos Control or the Crest can answer.
- Overkill from an attack on an ally is damage its controller "would take": Magic Shield (and other such interrupts) answer it.
