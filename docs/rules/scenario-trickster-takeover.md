# Trickster Takeover

Source: [FFG MC55 rulebook, pp. 2–23](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc55_rulebook-web.pdf).
The implementation uses Single Group Mode (1–4 players).

## Enchantress

Forced Actions may be performed whenever the player could normally perform an action.
The player phase cannot end while a possible Forced Action remains. Exhausted cards
whose Forced Actions require exhausting cannot perform them again; discarded cards
no longer require their actions.

Each player receives a random Hypnotic Gaze. Its reverse face stays hidden until it
flips. Flipping resolves its new face's When Revealed ability. Permanent cards cannot
leave play or have their text boxes blanked except by abilities in their own set.

Future of Despair enters with 2 threat per player. Enchantress II's standard-mode
reveal adds 3 per player (5 total); III adds 4 (6 total). Temptation Forced Actions
must be used even when their benefit cannot help. Spell Blast can attack alter-egos.

## God of Lies, Single Group Mode

Resolve Worlds Collide setup before Mischief and Mayhem setup. True Loki and Worlds
Collide occupy a neutral area. Only effects naming them may affect them; ordinary
villain/main scheme effects and acceleration tokens cannot. A per-group value counts
one group. References to a group in your pod refer to this group.

Swaps preserve damage, attachments, statuses, counters and tokens; swapping is neither
leaving play, entering play nor being revealed. Shatter counters persist until shattering.

At 10 remaining hit points per player, true Loki flips and resolves his reveal. When
Worlds Collide reaches 2 threat, finish the current player phase if in one; otherwise
finish resolving abilities and lose. Defeating true Loki first wins.

### Shatter the Illusion

Source: [printed rules card scan](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/shattertheillusion.jpg).
Resolve each step fully before the next, before the Fading Figment's synergy reward:

1. Remove all shatter counters; deal that much damage to true Loki.
2. Swap in a random other avatar, avatar side up; reset its dial to printed hit points.
3. Deal each player a facedown encounter card.

The first player resolves unspecified choices. Stories and Lies can attack alter-egos.
Epic Multiplayer Mode requires separate groups. Single Group Mode applies pod effects
within the current game.

## Trickster Magic

The four linked allies start set aside whenever their parent minions are included.
Linked cards never enter decks. Defeating a linked minion places it in the victory
display and puts its corresponding ally into play under the defeating player's control.
These allies do not count toward the ally limit and have Victory 0.
