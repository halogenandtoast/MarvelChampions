# Domino — NeXt Evolution

Sources: [FFG NeXt Evolution rulebook](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/73/e2/73e23a2b-3f2a-4173-8fee-cf0073ab11c8/mc40_next_evolution_rulebook-web.pdf), FAQ p.21 and starter decks p.22; Rules Reference v1.8, particularly Ability, Discard, Empty Deck, For Each, Resource, Swap, and Triggering Condition.

## Resource icons and discarding

Domino's hero constant counts each printed wild resource icon twice when resolving a card's resource-counting effect after a top-deck discard. Both icons remain wild. Other resources are counted once, including the three different icons on Jackpot. This constant is absent in alter ego and suppressed when the identity's text box is blank. It does not modify resource payments.

Finish a bulk discard before responding to its individual discarded cards. Immediate empty-deck resetting precedes these responses. Preserve exact card identity so Digging Deep or White Fox can find the original card after that reset; a response cannot claim a card another response has already moved. The Painted Lady may hold at most three cards; its action retrieves one, and its held cards are discarded if it leaves play.

Both identity actions swap cards directly. The swap never counts as drawing or discarding from the deck. Each face has a separate once-per-round action limit.

## Official clarifications

The rulebook FAQ confirms that Diamondback completes her action when the self-damage paying its cost defeats her. It also confirms that the discard resetting an empty deck includes a last-card Jackpot, before Jackpot's optional response.

Published correspondence from FFG Game Rules Specialist Alex Werner is preserved in the [post-1.5 ruling collection](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/):

- Ruling 82, September 7, 2023: a constant does not resolve, so Domino's wild-counting constant does not trigger Superpower Feedback.
- Ruling 83, September 7, 2023: pay Domino's Pistol costs first; resolve the discarded Jackpot's response; then resolve Sharpshooter's interrupt and its additional discard; finally resolve attack damage.
- Ruling 92, September 21, 2023: top-deck discard responses wait for the complete discard operation, including any immediate reset. Digging Deep can retrieve its exact copy after that shuffle.
- Ruling 98, October 12, 2023: Probability Field can enhance ATK when ATK substitutes for THW on an Assault scheme.

The newer [post-1.6 collection](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/), ruling 9 dated September 5, 2024, preserves Luck Be a Lady's ability to choose different targets as its individual resources resolve. A search of that collection found no later Domino, Jackpot, Painted Lady, or Sharpshooter correction.

## Starter deck

The printed Domino/Justice deck has 40 cards: fifteen signature cards, twelve Justice cards, and thirteen Basic cards. Her obligation is Memories of Armageddon; her nemesis set consists of Topaz, Not My Lucky Day, Prototype, and two Superpower Feedback attachments.

Use the official clarifications above; the additional Wolfsbane ruling below is an explicit project-owner decision.

## Additional damage and threat removal

The printed [A Good Workout](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/40040.jpg) says “that enemy”; MarvelCDB's “an enemy” transcription is incorrect. Choose one enemy, discard and count the card, then deal one combined instance of 4 plus that count. Tough prevents that whole instance, and attack damage reduction applies once.

The printed [Right Place, Right Time](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/40042.jpg) likewise adds the discarded count to its original removal: choose one scheme and remove 3 plus that count in one instance after discarding. The extra amount cannot move to another scheme.

## Discard responses and zone changes

All responses to a bulk discard share a window, so the player can resolve the individual discarded cards in any order. The Painted Lady can store each eligible discarded card once, up to its three-card capacity. Resolving one card’s response does not consume another card’s response.

An automatic empty-deck reset can put the original discarded card back into the deck before its response. Jackpot's subsequent optional shuffle must reshuffle that same physical card, not add a second copy. Digging Deep, White Fox, and The Painted Lady likewise move the original card; they cannot claim a card already taken by a competing response. Resource-counting effects still use the printed icons of the discarded card even after a response moves it.

**Project-owner ruling (2026-11-09):** When Wolfsbane discards Jackpot and Jackpot's response deliberately shuffles it into the deck, Wolfsbane cannot subsequently retrieve it. This is distinct from an automatic empty-deck reset, after which Wolfsbane can still retrieve the exact discarded card if Jackpot's optional response is declined. No direct FFG ruling on the deliberate-shuffle/Wolfsbane interaction was found; the owner explicitly selected this behavior.
