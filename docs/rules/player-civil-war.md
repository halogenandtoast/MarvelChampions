# Civil War — player cards

Source: [Civil War rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc56_rulebook-web_1.pdf), player decks pp. 24–25, and Rules Reference v1.8. Scenario leaders, team-versus-team cards, campaign rules and encounter cards are outside this implementation.

## Tigra and Hulkling

Tigra's On the Hunt draws once at the beginning of the player phase, with a maximum of three cards. Hunted's additional cost puts a searched minion into play, bypassing its When Revealed ability. The upgrade attaches to that particular minion; playing Hunted does not let its controller choose an existing minion instead.

Hulkling responds after a Shapeshift upgrade enters play: discard his other Shapeshift upgrades, then ready Hulkling. Teddy Altman's restriction prevents playing or putting a second Shapeshift upgrade into play; he does not discard down after playing one.

Armored Defense grants its tough status card after the attack even if its damage defeated the attacking minion. A defense event declares a defense without automatically applying the identity's basic DEF.

## Recorded rulings, consulted 2026-11-02

The project owner authorized consulting the rules and current FFG rulings instead of requesting decisions.

- FFG, 2026-01-11: Teddy cannot play a second Shapeshift in alter-ego form; Armored Defense still gives tough if its damage defeats the attacker.
- FFG, 2026-10-12: Coup de Grâce counts as exactly defeating its target, despite not dealing damage.

[FFG rulings collected by Hall of Heroes](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/).
