# Angel Hero Pack (MC42)

Sources: [FFG MC42 rules insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/81/27/8127f9a1-f792-4963-b016-fdb05d84abfd/mc42_angel_rules_insert-web.pdf), p.1; Rules Reference v1.8; the [printed starter-deck card](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/photo-oct-07-2023-4-42-19-pm.jpg).

## Insert rules

These are nonverbatim summaries of the insert; it contains no separate Q&A.

Warren's foldable identity has three faces: Warren Worthington III, Angel, and Archangel. Normal form-change rules apply. While the identity is in play, in any form, an Angel or Archangel character with Warren Worthington III as subtitle cannot enter play.

Player side schemes enter play with starting threat after their resource cost is paid during a player's turn. Their shared limit is one when one or two players started the game, otherwise two. If exceeded, the first player chooses schemes to discard until within the limit. Defeated victory schemes enter the victory display.

Alliance permits players to contribute payment together. Piercing removes the attacked target's tough cards before damage. Team-up requires a matching chosen identity for deck construction and both named characters in play when playing the card. Victory X sends a defeated card to the victory display instead of its owner's discard pile.

## Published rulings

These summaries report published designer correspondence, not project-owner rulings.

The [post-RR 1.5 correspondence](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/) establishes:

- Ruling 33, June 27, 2023: Archangel has an acceleration icon; Venom Goblin applies its extra threat to the scheme with the glider counter.
- Ruling 60, August 10, 2023: Mutant Protectors can be played during Taunt's attack, but the ally cannot defend and enters exhausted.
- Ruling 103, October 26, 2023: Aerial Agility allows a subsequent basic defense. Avian Anatomy returns it once its lasting effect is created, before boost cards flip.
- Ruling 219, July 11, 2024: post-attack forced responses precede Warpath's response and the event it plays. Pestilence can therefore blank Archangel before the event resolves.

The [post-RR 1.6 correspondence](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/), July 25, 2025, says Soaring Hearts requires the Angel face; Archangel and Warren do not satisfy its named character requirement.

## Rules Reference interactions

These implementation notes apply the local RR v1.8 transcription:

- [Form changes](glossary-d-h.md): a card-induced change does not consume the normal voluntary form change. Changing away from a face and back does not reset its phase limit.
- [Indirect damage](round-sequence.md): assign all damage from a source before resolving it simultaneously. An indirect attack still permits defense first. Only its defender, or the identity for an undefended attack, counts as attacked; other assigned characters merely take attack damage. A character that cannot take damage is ineligible for assignment.
- [Basic powers](glossary-a-c.md): Soaring Acrobatics applies to basic recovery as well as attack, thwart, and defense. Removing stunned or confused instead of using a power does not count as using that power.
- [Consequential damage](glossary-a-c.md): Cannonball checks the Aerial cards currently in the controller's hand when reducing consequential damage.

## Printed-card checks

Archangel's printed acceleration icon must be represented even though `pack.py` omits icons from its displayed hero text. Its additional threat applies only while that face is showing. The printed starter list calls card 17 “Triage”; the released card with that number is Render Medical Aid (42017).
