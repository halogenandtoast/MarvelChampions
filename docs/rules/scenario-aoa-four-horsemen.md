# Four Horsemen — Age of Apocalypse scenario 2

Sources: official MC45 *Age of Apocalypse* rulebook, pp.11–12, and card text 45081–45100; Rules Reference v1.8 entries Activation, Attack, Costs, Damage, Defeat, Find, Healing, Surge, Tough, and Villain. The official rulebook PDF is listed in the Age of Apocalypse campaign reference.

## Setup and villain row

Use War, Famine, Pestilence, and Death on their A sides for standard play and their B sides for expert play. Reveal all four in a random row and place the active counter on the leftmost villain. Randomness comes from the game generator; the ordered row is persisted in scenario state so saving, reloading, and undoing preserve it.

The main scheme is The Horsemen of Apocalypse. The Four Horsemen encounter set is required. Hounds and Dystopian Nightmare are the recommended customizable modular sets, alongside the selected Standard set. Each player reveals one randomly selected Four Horsemen side scheme during setup, without choosing the same physical scheme twice.

## Active villain

Only the active villain activates during the usual villain-phase activation. Encounter cards and constant abilities that say “the villain” refer to that active villain. A player-card ability that refers to “the villain” lets its player choose among the villains. Players can attack any villain.

After **any** villain activation, including a named inactive villain's activation caused by a card, move the active counter one place right from its current position, wrapping from the rightmost to the leftmost villain. A stunned attack or confused scheme that cancels the activation does not complete an activation. A card that explicitly moves the active counter does not itself cause an activation.

## Hit points and defeat

A Horseman cannot be defeated while another villain has at least one hit point. Such a Horseman can remain in play at zero actual hit points, still activating normally. The printed Forced Response after attacking checks whether that Horseman has at least one hit point; its response does not apply at zero.

Golden Horse and Metal Wings make their attached villain count as having at least one hit point. This is an effective value for those checks; it does not heal or move the physical hit-point dial. The protected villain cannot be defeated while its attachment maintains that effective minimum, and the other Horsemen cannot be defeated while it does. Discarding the final effective-minimum attachment when all actual dials are zero completes the players' victory.

Golden Horse attaches to a non-Aerial villain with the fewest remaining hit points, with the revealing player choosing ties. It gives Aerial, +1 ATK and +1 SCH. Metal Wings attaches to Death, moves the counter to him, and gives retaliate 1, +1 ATK and +1 SCH. Their Hero Responses resolve the attached villain's Forced Response against the attacking player before discarding the attachment.

## Horseman effects

War discards an upgrade or support controlled by the attacked player. Famine discards the top ten cards of that player's deck. Pestilence blanks that player's identity text box except Traits until the next villain phase begins. Death deals one nonattack damage to every character that player controls; identity damage uses the normal damage interrupt windows.

The corresponding side scheme applies its penalty to the **player who defeated it**, including when an ally or event supplied the thwart. Non-thwart threat movement does not itself identify a new defeating player.

Each Horseman treachery heals its named villain by two, gives them tough, then activates them against the revealing player. Its boost ability schedules that named villain's activation **after the current activation**, without an ordinary boost card. This includes schemes against alter ego. The main scheme moves the counter following each activation independently.

Rough Riders resolves the current active villain's Forced Response as though it had a hit point and attacked the revealing player, moves the counter once, then resolves the new active villain's response the same way. These are response effects, not attacks or activations, so they do not cause another counter move.

## Hounds modular set

Ahab has toughness. His reveal places three threat on Release the Hounds if present; otherwise it finds and reveals that side scheme. Hound attacks a hero player when revealed, or changes an alter-ego player to hero form instead.

Ahab's Energy Spear attaches to Ahab if he is in play; otherwise to the active villain. It adds two ATK, gives the attached enemy's next attack overkill and piercing, and is discarded at the end of that attack. Release the Hounds gives the first Hound revealed each phase surge. Defeating it searches the encounter deck and discard pile for a Hound and deals that card facedown to the defeating player.
