# Civil War Standard PvP

The three treachery families are implemented in all four printings: Righteous Cause, Whatever It Takes, and Targeted Strike. Only the canonical copies contribute to encounter-set quantities, giving four physical treacheries. Choosing Sides and its Now It's Personal back remain pending, so Standard PvP is not yet a complete selectable encounter set.

Righteous Cause makes the enemy leader scheme, with an additional boost card when the revealing team has more than one remaining player. Opponents and eliminated teammates do not count for that conditional; printed per-player values elsewhere still use the original team size. A deferred activation receives its extra boost immediately before that activation begins.

Whatever It Takes attacks the revealing hero, or that player's friendly leader when they are in alter-ego. The latter uses native leader combat, with no player defender and with boost-card resolution owned by the defending leader's team.

Targeted Strike gives the opponent a private search of the receiving team's top five encounter cards. The chosen card is dealt facedown and that team's deck is shuffled. Its boost ability gains icons for the players on the defending team. Gained icons are counted on that physical card, so ordinary boost-icon cancellation still works. Canceling its boost ability prevents those icons from being gained. Two physical copies do not count each other's icons.

Rules checked: the Civil War team and leader-attack rules, and v1.8 Player Elimination, Search, Boost, and Boost Icon entries. A search for a newer Righteous Cause elimination exception found none; remaining players are used for its textual conditional.

Verification: 26 behavior checks cover the printing aliases, team sizes, elimination, hero and leader attacks, private searches on independent decks, duplicate boost cards, Target Acquired, and Northstar. The focused Civil War suite passes 316 examples.

Mighty Avengers modular minions: Yellow Jacket (56073), Wasp (56074), and U.S. Agent (56075) use the same printed effect when revealed and when resolved as a boost. Yellow Jacket gives the enemy leader tough; Wasp places one threat on the main scheme; U.S. Agent deals one ability damage to the resolving player through the ordinary damage interrupt windows. Their printed Toughness, Teamwork (Avenger), and Retaliate keywords use the native minion rules. Resolving their boost abilities does not put the minion into play. Earth's Mightiest Heroes (56077) shares only the printed keywords of the other Avenger minions on its board; gained keywords never propagate recursively. Its base threat is three, plus Hinder 1 per player. Mighty Avengers (56076 and 56081) repeats each unblanked minion's When Revealed abilities (including Surge, which RR 1.8 defines as a When Revealed ability) on the resolving player, who chooses their order, and gains surge when there are none. Repeating an ability does not reveal or re-enter the minion, so entry keywords such as Toughness and Quickstrike do not trigger again. The five-card Mighty Avengers set is available as a modular encounter set.

The Initiative: Doc Samson (56078) confuses the revealing player, or places two threat if they were already confused. Hellcat (56079) stuns the revealing player, or deals two indirect damage if they were already stunned. Their boost abilities only give the printed status; they never use the reveal ability's alternate effect. Guard and Quickstrike use the ordinary minion rules.

Drafted (56080) lets the opposing team choose an eligible minion engaged with them, attaches before moving it to the resolving player, and does not reveal that minion again. Cooperative play discards until a minion is found, reveals it normally, and attaches afterward. Drafted is discarded if it cannot attach. Its host gains two hit points and the Avenger trait. The competitive test creates two distinct encounter decks and verifies the defeated minion and Drafted each return to their own original discard pile, as specified in the Civil War FAQ (page 17).

The Fifty State Initiative (56082) starts with three threat plus Hinder 1 per player. Its defeat shuffles only printed Avenger minions from its encounter discard into its encounter deck; Avenger gained from Drafted does not persist in the discard pile. The complete five-card Initiative set is available as a modular encounter set.
