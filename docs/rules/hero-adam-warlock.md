# Adam Warlock (The Mad Titan's Shadow, MC21)

<!-- MTS rulebook p.3 (starter decks); RR v1.8 p.62 (FAQ) -->

Adam Warlock's hero set, his obligation and nemesis set, and the box's Aggression, Justice,
Leadership (21053–21058), Protection and basic (Martinex) cards in his starter deck
(MarvelCDB 21041–21065).

## Deck-building: Avatar of Life

<!-- printed on Adam Warlock 21031b -->

During deck-building, your deck must include an equal number of cards from all 4 aspects. You
cannot include more than 1 copy of any non-Adam Warlock card. (MarvelCDB records this as
`deckRequirements: [{aspects: 4, limit: 1}]`.)

## Starter Deck: Adam Warlock / All Four Aspects

<!-- MTS rulebook p.3 -->

Adam Warlock is the avatar of life and a cosmic sorcerer. Use his *Battle Mage* ability each round
to turn unwanted aspect cards into powerful abilities. With Warlock's Cape and Mystic Senses on the
table, you will ready him and draw a card when you do.

Warlock has access to all four aspects, but he cannot include more than one copy of any aspect card
in his deck. As a result, his pre-built deck includes many powerful cards that are unique or "Max 1
per deck." Shuffle the ***Cosmic Entity*** events, such as The Gardener and Eternity, into the
encounter deck to potentially replace your encounter card reveal with a positive effect!

- **Adam Warlock cards**: Pip the Troll, Soul World, Karmic Staff, Warlock's Cape, Cosmic Ward (x2),
  Mystic Senses (x2), Karmic Blast (x3), Cosmic Awareness (x2), Quantum Magic (x2)
- **Aggression cards**: Marvel Boy, In-Betweener, Magic Attack, Uppercut, Combat Training, Audacity
- **Justice cards**: Quasar, Living Tribunal, For Justice!, Zone of Silence, Heroic Intuition,
  Determination
- **Leadership cards**: Major Victory, Eternity, Summoning Spell, Make the Call, Inspired, Innovation
- **Protection cards**: Charlie-27, The Gardener, Shield Spell, Counter-Punch, Armored Vest,
  Preservation
- **Basic cards**: Martinex
- **Nemesis cards**: The Magus, Universal Church of Truth, Zealot of Truth (x2), Cosmic Inquisition
- **Obligation**: Regeneration Cycle

(40 cards: 15 of his own, 6 of each aspect, 1 basic.)

## FAQ and errata

<!-- RR v1.8 p.62 -->

### In-Betweener (#42), Living Tribunal (#48), Eternity (#54), The Gardener (#60)

**Q: Where does a Cosmic Entity event go after it is resolved as a boost card?** A: That event is
placed in the encounter deck discard pile.

### Rules Reference (v1.8), Player Deck (deck runs out / resets)

<!-- RR v1.8 p.33 -->

If a player deck empties, the player shuffles their discard pile to make a new deck. That player
immediately deals themself one facedown encounter card from the top of the encounter deck.

- If the player's deck empties and reshuffles while the player was drawing cards, the player
  continues to draw cards up to the specified number.
- If the player's deck empties while the player was discarding cards from their deck, no further
  cards are discarded from the newly shuffled deck.
- If a player deck empties and the player has no cards in their discard pile, the deck does not
  reset until there is at least one card in the player's discard pile, then the player deals themself
  one facedown encounter card.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-28)

Orchestrator decisions from the rules, and the implementation plan's recommended readings, adopted:

- "Spend … from their hand": paid only with resource cards from hand (no resource abilities in play).
- Simultaneous forced abilities with the same timing: the first player orders them (RR Simultaneous
  Resolution), asked only when there are two or more.
- **R7** Karmic Blast / Cosmic Awareness: 4 (3) + X is one instance, one attack (a tough status eats all of it).
- **R8** A Cosmic Entity's "you" is the player revealing it, and it is a player card: an effect that
  stops damage from player cards (Thanos's Sanctuary) stops In-Betweener. "The villain" is chosen as
  for any player card (`withTheVillain`).
- **R9** A Cosmic Entity's When Revealed resolves once, even under "resolve When Revealed again"; it
  then leaves the game. It cannot be cancelled, so no "when revealed" window opens for it.
- **R10** A Cosmic Entity shuffled in stays in the encounter deck after its owner is eliminated.
- FAQ: a Cosmic Entity turned faceup as a boost card adds nothing and goes to the encounter discard pile.
- **R17** Shield Spell is not playable with fewer cards in the deck than the damage.
- **R18** Pip the Troll answers any enemy attack against a player's identity, before defenders are
  declared, so he may defend it; he enters play under the attacked player, still owned by his owner,
  and is not paid for with himself.
- **R19** Soul World ("after your deck runs out") and the Universal Church of Truth ("after a player
  resets their deck") both answer the reset: the empty deck made again from the discard pile, the
  facedown encounter card dealt.
- **R20** Regeneration Cycle: the flip to alter-ego is optional and comes first; "exhaust your
  alter-ego" is offered only to a ready alter-ego.
- **R21** Battle Mage discarding a basic or Adam Warlock card does nothing, but Warlock's Cape and
  Mystic Senses still answer "after you resolve Battle Mage". A basic power or changing form is not
  resolving Battle Mage.
- **R22** Battle Mage's Protection heals any friendly ally, any player's.
- **R23** Avatar of Life: at least one card of each aspect and the same number of each; copies of a
  card not his own counted by the card across its printings. Other heroes' "Max 1 per deck" stays
  unchecked (no deck validation exists for any such limit).
- **R27** Read Marvel Boy's "[physics]" as [physical] and Pip's "player's controller" as "player's control".
