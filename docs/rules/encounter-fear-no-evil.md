# Fear No Evil encounter implementation

Sources: [Fear No Evil rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc60_rulebook-web.pdf), supplied MC60 PDF, printed card scans bundled with this repository, and Rules Reference v1.8 under this directory. Ordinary player cards and existing hero nemesis sets are outside this work.

All 171 encounter and campaign faces now have runtime builders, including the five interchangeable underlings, Kingpin, six scenarios, six modular sets, campaign environments, and the campaign Mary Walker ally. The campaign and board UI are integrated. Final behavioral verification and random-play coverage remain in progress; runtime-builder coverage alone does not establish correct implementation.

Printed statistics missing from the imported card data are recorded in `data/card-text/street-encounter-stats.json`. The enrichment and definition generators read this overlay, so rebuilding the data preserves the corrections. Runtime handlers use the same printed statistics.

Disasters begin with two civilian counters and leave play after the last is rescued. Cops replace their scheming with attacks against the villain. Police Cordon's guard and patrol apply to Police minions. Wanted remains in play as an obligation until its action resolves.

Mister Fish changes the normal entry engagement to the identity with the fewest remaining hit points. Flight Serum and the two Tombstone attachments retain their printed numerical bonuses if their host changes type; abilities referring specifically to the attached enemy or minion require that type. Beetle chooses a minion-compatible attachment and attaches it specifically to himself. Under Rules Reference “Attach To,” an ability naming the specific host does not resolve the attachment's original host-selection instruction. Thus Beetle does not resolve that instruction's separate tough-status effect.

Tracksuit Mafia tucks actual encounter cards faceup, preserving physical card IDs and keeping them out of all encounter piles. Cards tucked under it are visible in the public board, survive saving, and are discarded when the side scheme leaves play. Revealing a tucked minion uses a distinct reveal source, so it does not recursively trigger the side scheme's response to revealing a Tracksuit from the encounter deck. Ivan's acceleration tokens belong to his minion entity and leave play with him.

Behavior tests are in `test/Marvel/Encounter/FearNoEvilSpec.hs`. Remaining content and broader verification are required before declaring the expansion complete.

Before implementing Kingpin, apply the [FFG ruling dated September 10, 2026](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/): The King's Gambit 1B hero action has no per-phase limit and resolves as much as possible. Shuffling an empty discard pile still shuffles the encounter deck and can satisfy that part of its instruction. Do not require the full printed threat-removal amount or a nonempty discard pile before offering the action. This specific official clarification governs its “then” handling.

The same official ruling collection (September 14, 2026) confirms that Tracksuit Mafia's tucked-card reveal is outside the encounter deck and cannot chain into further tucked-card reveals. The September 22 clarification also says to preserve Change of Venue's intended non-surge boost resolution; FFG announced an erratum distinguishing its non-surge When Revealed ability. Apply that exception when implementing Protection Racket, rather than granting a surge from its boost.

Drive is selectable as a modular set. Its three vehicles replace a complete damage packet with damage placed on the attachment; excess does not spill to the host, and this replacement can apply to damage that cannot be prevented. Carjacking preserves the discarded vehicle’s physical card ID when it is paid for and attached to an identity.

Traffic Jam retains the acting character through threat placement and removal. Its forced interrupt changes the destination before the scheme’s normal threat window, retains villain scheming receipts, and closes the replaced character window without chaining another copy. A removal that is blocked on the original scheme, or that would remove no threat, does not trigger it. The implementation interprets the amount replaced as the threat the character would actually remove from the original scheme; this is an inference from the printed replacement and RR v1.8 Interrupt, Replacement Effect, and Thwart entries. Ten focused Traffic Jam behavior tests pass, including Dark Phoenix’s separate scheming destination. The full unit regression run passed 9,135 examples, and Drive’s random-play modular smoke case passed with two seeds. Broader expansion validation remains required.

Cold as Ice and Hard as Marble compare base hit points, including a printed per-player value, while ignoring temporary maximum-HP increases. They use a separate base-HP matcher so effects that explicitly compare printed values retain their existing behavior. Tests cover Garm in a two-player game and a lower-base-HP minion with a large temporary HP bonus.

Bullseye retains latent attack keyword grants when a continuous effect removes that keyword. Attack resolution recalculates active keywords from the current modifiers. Deadly Sai's discard response occurs after the defended attack ends, including retaliation (RR Defend and FAQ Black Panther); it does not undo that attack's damage or retaliation. Its numerical ATK bonus and piercing likewise last through that attack.

Protection Racket tracks each business's owner and preserves the physical business card through swaps. The villain, outside player play areas, schemes onto every business. Minions and cards in a player's area affect that player's business when referring to “the main scheme”; “a scheme” can select any business. Attached upgrades and encounter attachments occupy their host's area. Pawn Shop's first-upgrade discount follows the destination area even when another player pays for the upgrade.

Black Cat retains villainous when she becomes an ally. Her basic attack and thwart receive and resolve actual physical boost cards, including extra boost cards and boost abilities; the activation context is serializable. An ally's printed dash remains unavailable when converted into a minion and is displayed as a dash in the public board.

Integration verification checkpoint: the frontend production build and backend library, test executable, and API executable build successfully. The first full non-smoke regression run completed 9,363 examples with one Pawn Shop test failure. Correcting its test interaction then exposed a real first-upgrade consumption timing bug: the upgrade was not yet in play when the old handler inspected its controller. The destination area is now recorded as the paid card is played, before its play interrupts, and put-into-play effects do not consume the discount. Subsequent focused runs pass, including 323 examples after save/reload, conversion, type-change cleanup, and campaign removal checks. All six scenarios passed normal/expert setup checks and every Fear No Evil face passed runtime-builder coverage. The 60 scenario, modular-combination and campaign smoke cases pass with two seeds each across the initial run and targeted reruns after fixing Art Museum retrieval. All six standalone modular smoke cases also pass with two seeds each. Final broad regressions and the remaining per-card rules/behavior audit are still required.

Baron Zemo conversion preserves an original ally snapshot and its physical card. This keeps a recruited Black Cat blank while Deceived, restores her printed action when Zemo leaves, and sends her physical encounter card to the encounter discard if defeated. Save/reload tests cover this state. Establish Trust discards attached upgrades and tucked cards when changing type; Mary Walker flips back with fresh threat and no carried counters. Tests also verify Mary replenishes her hit points twice and wins on the third Psyche token.

Campaign title tracking excludes temporary removals into held-card or identity storage zones. It classifies the physical card in its out-of-play state: under RR Leaves Play, temporary type changes do not persist after leaving play. Thus a removed printed ally counts even if it had been treated as a minion, while a printed encounter minion briefly controlled as an ally does not become a banned player ally. This is an application of RR Card Types and Leaves Play to the campaign Victory instruction; no specific contrary FFG campaign ruling was found in the latest ruling collection.

Outstanding completion audit includes the remaining newer card/boost behaviors, expert campaign health/retry cases, and final full/Shadowland regressions after the shared engine changes. Do not mark the expansion complete from this checkpoint.

Additional audit: Shadowland’s latest scenario/modular/campaign run passed 129 examples with zero failures. The full non-smoke regression reached 9,379 examples with one controlled-minion definition failure. Restoring the original encounter minion’s printed definition and powers, while preserving the explicit blanking of an ally treated as a minion, passes all 20 Phoenix control regressions and the recruited Black Cat save/reload cases. Campaign removal tests also cover unique supports and title bans across different printings.

Expanded behavior audit: 368 Fear No Evil checks pass after adding direct Electro, Hammerhead, Purple Man, Mary, art/chase, Daily Bugle, and campaign-health cases. Explicit defeats now open the same defeat windows as damage-based defeats, preserving their source. Consolidate Power handles its After timing by retrieving the physical defeated card from its destination; the earlier When Defeated path remains available for effects that replace the discard with a boost.

The metadata generator no longer treats linked opposite faces as interchangeable printings. In this engine `cdAlternateCardCodes` identifies reprints, so populating it from MarvelCDB `linkedTo` made face-specific matchers treat Out Front and Alongside as the same face. The Getaway collision behavior now verifies the transition and damage after its threat reaches zero. The correction applies to both street expansions while preserving the frontend's linked-face metadata.

Expert campaign healing prompts now state each expansion's actual cost and benefit. Fear No Evil heals REC; Shadowland restores full hit points. A defeated hero selected for the next game uses the existing automatic rejoin workflow and pays its encounter-card cost. Surviving heroes can decline healing. Treating nonparticipation as an in-game defeat was rejected because it fires scenario elimination conditions, including Protection Racket's loss condition; the app currently continues campaigns with their registered party and automatically rejoins its defeated heroes.

The full non-smoke regression passed 9,423 examples with zero failures after the shared defeat-window and face-metadata corrections. Both two-seed expansion smoke runs are being refreshed. Their current logs are `/tmp/mc-street-regression-after-defeat-faces.log`, `/tmp/mc-shadowland-face-final-smoke.log`, and `/tmp/mc-fne-face-final-smoke.log`. The smoke results remain pending at this checkpoint.
