# Gamora Hero Pack (MC18)

<!-- Gamora rulesheet, https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc18_gamora_rulesheet.pdf -->

For your first game with Gamora, take all of the cards preceding the divider card and follow the Setup instructions in the Learn to Play. There is a complete decklist on the reverse side of the divider card for your reference.

The remaining cards included after the divider card can be used when creating your own custom player deck. The rules for deck customization can be found in Appendix I of the Rules Reference.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do the 6 Attack and/or Thwart events that Gamora may include in her deck count toward her minimum and maximum deck sizes?** A: Yes. While Gamora may include events in her deck outside of her chosen aspect, these events are still counted as part of both her minimum and maximum deck sizes.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-27)

- Sibling Rivalry: "players other than Gamora" are the other players. Anything the Gamora player controls (her identity, allies, events, upgrades) may remove its threat.
- Unfulfilled Destiny with neither option fully possible: the second option resolves as far as it can (discard the events you have, up to 2), then the obligation is discarded.
- In a Bind blanks Gamora's printed text box: Finesse, Precision and the alter-ego's Action. Her basic powers (thwart, attack, defense, recover) and changing form are game rules, not text, and stay.

## Readings from the rules (not asked)

- The starter deck ("Aggression+") is #2–#23: the hero set, Aggression and basic cards, and 3 Impede and 3 First Hit taken with Skilled Tactician. Pivotal Moment, Comms Implant, True Grit and Enhanced Reflexes (#29–#32) are after the divider.
- Skilled Tactician is a deck-building rule; the engine checks no deck's aspects, so it needs nothing.
- An "attack event" / "thwart event" is an event with the Attack / Thwart trait. Crosscounter and Hit and Run count as both.
- Both of Gamora's faces are titled Gamora, so "Gamora" (In a Bind, Waylay, Sibling Rivalry, Conditioning Room) is her identity in either form (RR Identity: a title refers to the identity with that title).
- "This turn" (Decisive Blow, Forward Momentum) is the current player's turn; "this round" (Clobber, Impede) counts cards you played since the round began, the one resolving included.
- Villains are unique, so Godslayer answers a basic attack against the villain.
- Unfulfilled Destiny's first option needs a ready alter-ego (RR Choose (Option)).
- First Hit is two abilities on one card: played as a Hero Action it attacks the villain; played in the window "when a minion initiates an attack" (any player's minion) it deals 2 to that minion.
- Crosscounter and Hit and Run carry every label they print (RR Labeled Ability): a stun or confusion cancels the whole card and each such status is removed.
- Sibling Rivalry is not a thwart target for other players, and a source no player controls (an encounter effect) still removes its threat ("players other than Gamora" only).
- The Nebula ally cannot be played or put into play while the Nebula minion is in play (RR Unique Icon: matching unique cards); the minion discards the ally as she would enter play.
