# Black Panther (Shuri)

Sources: [official rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc51_rulesheet-web.pdf), [FFG mirror](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/01/1c/011c0590-aba8-4803-a1ff-d61229ba8a5a/mc51_rulesheet-web.pdf), Rules Reference v1.8 (local glossary and FAQ/errata).

## Featured rules

**Special.** A Special is resolved only when another effect instructs a player to resolve it. Shuri's upgrades each have their own Special; they do not use T'Challa's last-Special bonus. The two identities can resolve the other identity's BLACK PANTHER upgrades if they control them.

**Linked.** Redemption is linked to Show of Empathy, starts set aside, and is excluded from the minimum deck size. Show of Empathy redirects removed threat to a non-Elite minion. That threat remains on the minion. Once it meets the minion's remaining hit points, the side scheme enters the victory display and Redemption converts the minion to a blank Redeemed ally with its printed SCH as THW and one consequential damage per basic power. Changing the card type preserves its physical card, damage, statuses, and attachments.

**Team-Up.** Heart of the Panther requires both Black Panther incarnations (T'Challa and Shuri) in play, represented by their identity or ally forms. It is limited to one copy in a deck.

**Tuck.** Tucked cards are faceup, out of play, and not attached. Leaving play discards cards tucked under the host. The Raft tucks the actual discarded minion, retaining its card ID. Random selection uses the stored game generator.

**Victory and Uses.** T'Challa's Shadow starts with four doubt counters. Its cost increase remains while it is in play. After each qualifying thwart, attack, or defense, remove one. RR v1.8's Victory X definition explicitly sends a card with uses to the victory display when its final counter is removed.

**Villainous.** Each activation gives the minion a boost card. Additional boost cards from Joystick or Playing for Keeps are cumulative.

## Rules decisions (autonomously researched, 2026-11-02)

- T'Challa's Shadow goes to the victory display on its final doubt counter, under RR v1.8 pp.46, Uses and Victory X.
- A discarded Panther Claws Special still resolves as an attack from its source. Its five damage is one attack with piercing, rather than two damage instances.
- Vibranium Suit moves one damage: the hero heals only the amount actually moved. Damage dealt is attack damage and uses attack restrictions.
- Redemption goes to victory when its host is defeated; when its host leaves without defeat, discard it. This agrees with FFG's Alex Werner ruling dated 2025-04-08, indexed in [FFG rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/official-ffg-rulings/), and the RR v1.8 Victory X entry.
- A card cannot be tucked from the encounter discard pile when another replacement has placed it elsewhere (for example, The Collection or victory display).

### Later published FFG rulings checked

[Alex Werner, 2025-11-21](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) confirms that the slashes in Heart of the Panther identify alternatives: Shuri's Black Panther identity and her T'Challa ally satisfy the team-up. The same ruling collection confirms Show of Empathy redirects a threat token moved by Beat Cop to the minion, so Beat Cop does not retain that token. An earlier 2025-04-08 ruling confirms Target Spotter changes who engages a minion; effects whose costs require the original player to engage it fail if someone else engages it instead. Infiltration's removal does not depend on which player engages its minion.

Printed Going Undercover (51016) has 4 starting threat, confirmed against its printed card image. An ally using a basic power includes defending, so T’Challa and the Dora Milaje allies can respond after a basic defense. Moving damage with Vibranium Suit requires a hero who can be healed; the optional discard-for-tough clause can still resolve separately.
