# Wolverine

Source: [official Wolverine Hero Pack rules insert (MC35)](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc35_wolverine_rules_insert.pdf), p.1. Rules Reference v1.8 supersedes the original keyword reminders. Card text is from the local MarvelCDB dump, with current errata in `faq-errata.md`.

## Rules insert

<!-- MC35 insert p.1; implementation summary -->

The insert introduces Permanent, Piercing, Steady and Team-Up. Their current definitions are in `glossary-p-r.md` and `glossary-s-z.md`. Permanent cards are set aside during setup and cannot leave play, be defeated or be blanked except by cards in their own set. Piercing removes all tough cards before an attack deals positive damage. Steady requires two stunned or confused cards for the respective status to apply. Team-Up requires the identity to match one named character for deck construction and both named characters in play to play the event.

Wolverine begins with his Claws in play. Claws pays for an Attack event by taking its printed cost as damage, ignoring its resource cost; Lunging Strike gains overkill when played this way. Healing Factor is an optional response at the beginning of the player phase. Warrior Skill modifies an attack; Aggressive Energy modifies the event's damage, so their bonuses can combine.

## Current errata and timing

<!-- RR v1.8 pp.18, 31, 34, 68; implementation summary -->

Logan's setup puts Wolverine's Claws into play, without searching. Berserker Barrage takes 2 damage **to** repeat its ability; it does not initiate a new paid ability using a cost arrow. A repeat is a separate attack, including its attack windows and retaliation. Slice and Dice makes two separate attacks, so stun replaces one attack and retaliation happens between them. Event damage modifiers apply to every instance; a modifier to one attack applies only to that attack.

Taking damage as a cost requires taking the full amount. Tough or prevention cannot pay it. Printed cost is unaffected by discounts. Additional non-resource play costs and play restrictions still apply. A basic recovery requires damage on the identity; Death Factor replaces its healing, not its exhaustion cost.

Elimination ends the ongoing attack. Further attack steps require Wolverine to remain in play; the event's remaining resolution and cleanup still complete. An activation initiated during another activation waits until the current activation and its responses finish.

Lady Deathstrike's discard uses the damaged character's owner. Scenario-owned story allies have no player hand; changing a player card's controller does not change its owner.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-30)

Wolverine's Claws grants piercing only to the first actual attack of Slice and Dice. If stun replaces the first listed attack, the second attack gains piercing. Repeated Berserker Barrage attacks lose the piercing granted by Claws.
