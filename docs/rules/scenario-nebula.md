## Summary (non-normative)

```
Villain deck: Nebula (I), (II); expert (II), (III). Main schemes: The Art of Evasion (1A/1B), Warp Drive
  Initiated (2A/2B). Encounter deck: Nebula, Power Stone, Ship Command, Space Pirates, Standard (+ Expert).
1A Setup: Nebula's Ship and the Milano into play, the Power Stone on Nebula; discard the top 2 per player encounter
  cards and attach each Technique discarded to Nebula.
Evasion counters on Nebula's Ship: +1 as each villain phase begins; Barrel Roll and Ruthless add more; the first
  player removes them with Shoot the Thrusters! Both main schemes accelerate by X per player, X the counters.
2A: 2 more counters, then 2 cards of each player deck and the encounter deck discarded for each counter. 2B completed:
  the players lose. Nebula's last stage defeated: the players win.
Nebula: the first Technique revealed each round surges; when she initiates an activation against you, each
  Technique's Special resolves, then (I) all of them are discarded, (II) you discard 1, (III) you may remove your
  deck's top card from the game to discard 1.
```

## Scenario
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.14 -->

**SCENARIO #4 - NEBULA**

*As you get out of range of the museum, the Milano's sensors pick up another speeding ship within your vicinity.
Assuming it must be the thief who stole the Stone, you crank up the hyper thrusters and ride the ship's tail. When
you get close, you realize you're chasing Nebula, daughter of Thanos, and Gamora's dear sister.*

*It would be best if she didn't have the Power Stone, either.*

**Villain Deck**: Nebula (I), Nebula (II)
*Remove Nebula (I) and add Nebula (III) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: The Art of Evasion (1A/1B), Warp Drive Initiated (2A/2B)

**Encounter Deck**: Nebula, Power Stone, Ship Command, Space Pirates, and Standard encounter sets.

*The Space Pirates modular set can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when using
scenario customization rules.*

## Campaign Instructions
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.14 -->

**SETUP:**

- Reveal the Guerrilla Tactics (181A) side scheme (use the reverse side for expert mode).
- Shuffle each Galactic Artifacts side scheme recorded in the "Galactic Artifacts Side Schemes in the Victory
  Display" section of the campaign log into the encounter deck. Perform the following for each specified side scheme
  shuffled in this way:
  - **Hujadarian Monarch Egg**: Place 1 evasion counter on Nebula's Ship.
  - **Magical Teapot**: Deal the first player 1 facedown encounter card.
  - **Philosopher's Stone**: Give Nebula 1 facedown boost card.
  - **Crystal Ball**: Give Nebula a tough status card.
- Shuffle the Badoon Headhunter (183) minion into the encounter deck. Then, if the number of marks in the
  "Headhunter Defeated?" section of the campaign log is equal to or greater than:
  - (1) Shuffle the On the Hunt (184) treachery into the encounter deck.
  - (2) Shuffle the Dead to Rights (185) treachery into the encounter deck.
  - (3) Shuffle the Headhunter's Henchman (186) minion into the encounter deck.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign
  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may subtract 1 unit from their respective "Unspent Units" box in the
  campaign log to heal their identity to its printed hit point value.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Discard cards from the top of the encounter deck until a **Technique** attachment is
  discarded this way, then attach that card to Nebula.

<!-- TGMW rulebook p.15 -->

**VICTORY:**

In the campaign log:

- Record units in each player's respective "Unspent Units" box *(add these to units you already have)*:
  - Record 1 unit for each player.
  - Record a number of units for each player (to a maximum of 3 units) equal to the victory values on encounter
    cards in the victory display.
  - Record 1 unit for each player if there are 1 or fewer evasion counters on the Nebula's Ship environment.
  - Record 1 unit for each player if the main scheme is on stage 1B (The Art of Evasion).
- If Badoon Headhunter is in the victory display, mark the box beside "Scenario #4 - Nebula" in the "Headhunter
  Defeated?" section.
- If the Power Stone is attached to an identity, record that identity's name in the "Power Stone Control" section.
- Record the number of evasion counters on Nebula's Ship in the "Evasion Counters" section.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **Evasion counters** are the counters on Nebula's Ship (`placeEvasionCounters`, `getEvasionCounters` in
  `Marvel.Environment.Environments.NebulasShip`; none without the Ship in play), shown on the Ship on the board. The
  Ship is set aside from the encounter deck and put into play by 1A's setup, not revealed. The Milano and the Power
  Stone are already in play then, by their setup keyword.
- **Acceleration X[per player]** on 1B and 2B is counted as the main scheme accelerates in step one of the villain
  phase. The Ship's counter is placed as the phase begins, so it counts that phase.
- **Shoot the Thrusters!** is offered as two First Player Actions, one spending 1 resource and one spending 2;
  spending none would remove nothing. The Milano's own resource cannot pay for it (it is exhausted by the cost).
- **The first Technique revealed each round**: revealed from anywhere (the encounter deck, Lethal Intent, Combat
  Ready), it surges after it has resolved. A Technique attached without being revealed (1A's setup, its own boost)
  is not revealed and does not count.
- **"When Nebula initiates an activation against you"**: her attack against you (the villain phase, Ruthless, any
  card), or her scheme in your alter-ego's activation. A stunned Nebula's attack, or a confused Nebula's scheme, is not
  initiated: its status card is removed instead (RR Stun, Confuse), and nothing resolves. The Specials resolve before
  the activation's boost card is dealt; with no Technique in play, nothing is asked. Discarding "those attachments"
  is of the Techniques whose Special resolved, still in play.
- **Special** abilities are resolved for "you": the player Nebula's activation is against, the player revealing
  Combat Ready, or the player a boosted activation was against. A boost card given to Nebula outside an activation
  stays with her for her next one.
- **A Technique's boost**: the card is discarded with the other boost cards as the activation ends, then attached to
  Nebula from the discard pile and its Special resolved.
- **Cutthroat Ambition**'s cap applies to damage from a hero's or an ally's attack, after Wide Stance's reduction:
  an 8-damage attack with both deals 5.
- **Ruthless**: threat placed by the scheme's own placement (not a Special resolved by Nebula's interrupt) and damage
  dealt to any character by the attack (a tough status card's prevention deals none) count.
- **Warp Drive Initiated**: 2 cards per evasion counter, counted after its 2 are placed; a deck that runs out is
  reset as usual (the encounter deck's discard stops there, RR Encounter Deck).
- **Pirates**: "remove from the game" is not a discard. A pirate's boost turned up for a minion's activation gives
  nothing (no villain activation to give it to).
- **Honor Among Thieves**: with no Criminal minion found, nothing else happens ("then").
- **Lethal Intent** reveals the Technique for the player revealing it (the active player).

## Campaign hooks

The campaign's Victory steps read: `getEvasionCounters` (evasion counters), the main scheme in play
(`getMainSchemeCode` / `gameMainSchemes`: The Art of Evasion is stage 1, code 16091), `getPowerStoneHolder`
(Power Stone control) and `getVictoryDisplay` / `getVictoryPoints`. The campaign Setup and Victory are not
implemented by this scenario.

## Open rulings

- **Cutthroat Ambition with Wide Stance**: the order of "reduce by 1" and "no more than 5" is not stated; the engine
  reduces first, then caps (8 → 7 → 5), reading the cap as a limit on what she takes.
- **"Nebula initiates an activation" while stunned or confused**: the RR's status cards are replacement effects
  ("instead"), so the activation is not initiated and the Specials do not resolve.
- **Lethal Intent's "reveal that card"** is for the active player, the one who revealed Lethal Intent in every way
  it can be revealed in this scenario.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-27)

- A stunned Nebula's attack, or a confused Nebula's scheme, does not resolve her Techniques' Specials: the status card replaces the activation.
- Ruthless counts only threat Nebula's scheme itself places (SCH and boost icons), not threat a boost card's ability places.
