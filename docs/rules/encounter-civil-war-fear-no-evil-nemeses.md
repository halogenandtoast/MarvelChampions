# Civil War and Fear No Evil hero encounter cards

Sources: the printed encounter cards accompanying Tigra, Hulkling, Daredevil and
Echo; the Civil War and Fear No Evil rulebooks; Rules Reference v1.8 (especially
You/Your, Enemy Activation, Facedown, and Resource).

- [Civil War rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc56_rulebook-web_1.pdf)
- [Fear No Evil rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc60_rulebook-web.pdf)

Referential abilities use the same-identity priority in RR v1.8: these nemesis
abilities refer to their associated nemesis card, rather than another version
of Bullseye or Kingpin from a different encounter set.

Each hero's obligation belongs in the encounter deck during ordinary setup.
Nemesis cards remain outside the game until instructed to enter it. The five-card
nemesis sets count physical copies, including both copies of a repeated treachery
or minion.

## Tigra

The Hood is steady and villainous. His attack places one threat on Criminal
Underworld afterward. Jigsaw's quickstrike attack also triggers his response if
it damages a character. Criminal Underworld prevents all damage to Criminal
minions; it does not prevent attacks themselves. Madame Masque finds and reveals
Criminal Underworld if absent, otherwise places two threat on it. Her boost
places two threat if the scheme is present, otherwise adds two boost icons.
The Hood's Mantle attaches to the Hood when possible, otherwise the villain.
Its resource payment response follows a basic hero attack against its host.

In Too Deep permits the optional change to alter-ego before offering the exhaust
and remove option. Its other option activates every engaged minion in the
player's current form, in the order that player chooses. With no minion activation,
including when every activation is prevented by status cards, it gains surge.

## Hulkling

Super Skrull checks each distinct printed resource type on the discarded card;
a double-resource card can trigger multiple different effects. A wild icon is
its own printed type, not a choice of another type for this ability. The attack
bonus lasts only for that attack. Fantastic Powers finds and puts Super Skrull
into play, attaches, then makes him attack if the resolving identity is in hero
form. Its hit point bonus is three, not three per player.

Skrull Business triggers after player deck discards and holds those cards
facedown. They cannot also remain available in the discard pile. On leaving
play, the held cards return to their owners' discard piles. Their identities
remain hidden in the public game view.

You're Coming With Me! counts distinct printed resource types among all three
discarded cards, for a maximum of four. Damage in hero form opens the ordinary
would-take-damage windows; threat in alter-ego opens would-place-threat windows.
Complicated Lineage discards all controlled Shapeshift upgrades on its second
option.

## Daredevil

Bullseye's reveal attack can attack an alter-ego. Discarding a controlled Persona
support is an alternative, not an additional cost. Deadliest Man Alive gives
Bullseye an additional boost card when he attacks; its amplification icon also
applies normally. Stolen Sai attaches to an enemy tied for highest ATK, grants
piercing, and can move to Elektra after its hero action payment. Eye on the Target
removes the selected controlled ally or Persona support from the game; its other
option attacks with Bullseye or finds and reveals him if absent.

Sensory Overload remains in its owner's play area. Each Sense upgrade entering
play triggers one damage. Its owner can discard it after using recovery in
alter-ego form.

## Echo

Kingpin replaces an attack against Maya Lopez's identity with a scheme
activation, including an attack against her hero form. Henchmen and Master
Manipulator independently prevent damage to Kingpin. Master Manipulator's
initial threat is two per player; its boost reveals it only if Kingpin is in
play.

Raised by the Kingpin prevents its owner's identity from damaging Kingpin.
Under RR v1.8 You/Your, this includes events and upgrades that extend the
identity, but does not include allies. The same distinction applies to its
response after its owner thwarts. Count the threat actually removed, not the
attempted thwart amount. Accumulate that threat on the obligation and remove it
from the game at four or more. Its reveal instruction puts Kingpin into play.
Pawn of the Kingpin's hero instruction deals non-attack damage equal to the
chosen hero's modified ATK; in alter-ego Kingpin schemes, or the card surges if
he is absent.

A search for subsequent card-specific rulings on 2026-11-02 did not identify an
applicable official clarification superseding these printed rules.
