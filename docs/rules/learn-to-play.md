# Learn to Play (Core Set): Summary

> Source: *Marvel Champions: The Card Game* core set **Learn to Play** booklet (FFG, 24 pages; PDF dated Oct 2019,
> file `mvc01_learn_to_play_eng-compressed.pdf`). This is a **summary**, not a full transcription: it keeps what the
> Rules Reference (RR) does not cover. Lists of cards, sets and scenario contents are transcribed exactly.
> Golden rule: where the LTP and the RR disagree, the RR wins (RR "The Golden Rules"). Known differences are listed at the end.

## Components (LTP p.3)

- 343 cards: 199 player cards, 137 encounter cards, 7 reference cards
- 30 status cards (stunned, confused, tough)
- 62 damage tokens (values 1, 3, 5)
- 33 threat tokens (values 1, 3, 5)
- 16 all-purpose counters (values 1, 3)
- 5 acceleration tokens (+1)
- 4 player hit point dials, 1 villain hit point dial
- 1 first player token

The core set contains five identities (Spider-Man, Captain Marvel, Iron Man, She-Hulk, Black Panther), four
aspects (Aggression, Justice, Leadership, Protection), basic cards, and three scenarios (Rhino, Klaw, Ultron).

## Card anatomy (as described in the LTP)

The LTP itself defers card anatomies to the RR (see [appendices.md](appendices.md), Appendix III). Positions the LTP
calls out in its examples:

- **Cost**: top-left of a player card; the **card type** (ally, upgrade, support, event) is printed beneath the cost.
- **Resources** a card generates when discarded: icons in the **bottom-left corner** (mental, physical, energy, wild).
- **Identity cards**: hero side has THW / ATK / DEF; alter-ego side has REC. **Hand size** and **hit points** are
  printed at the bottom ("HAND SIZE 6 / HIT POINTS 10"; hand size near the bottom-left).
- **Allies**: THW and ATK with **consequential damage** icons beneath each; hit points in the lower right of the art.
- **Villain**: SCH and ATK on the left, **stage number** in the upper-right corner, hit points at the bottom,
  printed per player (e.g. Rhino (I) "HIT POINTS 14[per player]").
- **Main scheme**: **target threat** top-left (e.g. The Break-In! "7[per player]"), **acceleration field** and
  **starting threat** at the bottom-right/bottom ("+1[per player]" / "0"), stage (e.g. "1B") top-right.
- **Side scheme**: starting threat at the bottom; side scheme icons (crisis, acceleration, hazard) in the text area.
- **Encounter cards**: **boost icons** (or a star icon) at the **bottom-right**.
- **Nemesis set** cards share a common label; starter **decklists** are printed on the back of each deck's title card
  (do not shuffle the title card into the deck).

Abbreviations: ATK – Attack, DEF – Defense, REC – Recover, SCH – Scheme, THW – Thwart.

## Tutorial setup (LTP pp.4-7)

The tutorial uses Spider-Man (and, for a second player, Captain Marvel) against Rhino. The LTP lists 14 steps; the
current RR (Appendix II, in [round-sequence.md](round-sequence.md)) has 16 steps and is authoritative. LTP steps with
the tutorial-specific instructions:

1. **Select Heroes.** Alter-ego side up. (Spider-Man/Peter Parker; Captain Marvel/Carol Danvers.)
2. **Set Hit Points.** Spider-Man 10, Captain Marvel 12.
3. **Select First Player.**
4. **Set Aside Obligations.** Eviction Notice (Spider-Man), Family Emergency (Captain Marvel).
5. **Set Aside Nemesis Sets.** All Spider-Man / Captain Marvel nemesis cards set aside, out of play.
6. **Shuffle Player Decks.** Spider-Man deck = remaining Spider-Man cards + Justice (yellow) + Basic (gray) cards from
   that starter pack; Captain Marvel deck = remaining Captain Marvel cards + Aggression (red) + Basic cards from that
   starter pack. (This tutorial Captain Marvel deck differs from the Captain Marvel/Leadership starter list below.)
7. **Collect Tokens and Status Cards.**
8. **Select Villain.** Villain deck Rhino (I) on top of Rhino (II). Main scheme deck is one card, The Break-In!,
   put into play on its 1A side.
9. **Set the Villain's Hit Points.** Printed value × number of players: Rhino (I) is 14 per player (14 / 28).
10. **Resolve Scheme Setup.** Resolve "Setup" on the main scheme; read 1A and flip to 1B.
11. **Create and Shuffle Encounter Deck.** Rhino encounter deck = all Rhino cards + all Standard encounter cards + all
    Bomb Scare cards, plus the obligations of the heroes in play (Eviction Notice / Family Emergency). Nemesis cards stay
    set aside until called for by an encounter card.
12. **Draw Cards.** Draw up to hand size (Peter Parker 6, Carol Danvers 6).
13. **Resolve Mulligans.** Discard any number, draw back up to hand size; discarded cards are not shuffled back.
14. **Resolve Character Setup Abilities.** (None for Spider-Man or Captain Marvel.)

## Nemesis sets (LTP p.17)

- **Spider-Man**: Vulture, Highway Robbery, The Vulture's Plans, Sweeping Swoop (x2)
- **Captain Marvel**: Yon-Rogg, The Psyche-Magnitron, Kree Manipulator (x2), Yon-Rogg's Treason
- **Iron Man**: Whiplash, Imminent Overload, Electric Whip Attack (x2), Electromagnetic Backlash
- **She-Hulk**: Titania, Personal Challenge, Genetically Enhanced, Titania's Fury (x2)
- **Black Panther**: Killmonger, Usurp the Throne, Heart-Shaped Herb, Ritual Combat (x2)

LTP rule for nemesis side schemes: when a card instructs a player to put a nemesis side scheme into play, the nemesis
side scheme of the player that drew the card is placed in the play area, their nemesis minion is put into play engaged
with that player, and the other encounter cards of that nemesis set go to the encounter discard pile.

## Core scenarios (LTP p.23)

Recommended order: Rhino first, then Klaw, then Ultron.

### Rhino

- **Villain deck**: Rhino (I), Rhino (II). Remove Rhino (I) and add Rhino (III) for expert mode.
- **Main scheme deck**: The Break-In!
- **Rhino encounter set**: Armored Rhino Suit, Breakin' & Takin', Charge (x2), Crowd Control, Enhanced Ivory Horn,
  Hard to Keep Down (x2), Hydra Mercenary (x2), "I'm Tough!" (x2), Sandman, Shocker, Stampede (x3)
- **Standard encounter set**: Advance (x2), Assault (x2), Caught Off Guard, Gang-Up, Shadow of the Past
- **Bomb Scare encounter set (modular, difficulty 1)**: Bomb Scare, Hydra Bomber (x2), Explosion, False Alarm (x2)

### Klaw

- **Villain deck**: Klaw (I), Klaw (II). Remove Klaw (I) and add Klaw (III) for expert mode.
- **Main scheme deck**: Underground Distribution, Secret Rendezvous
- **Klaw encounter set**: Armored Guard (x3), Defense Network, Illegal Arms Factory, Klaw's Vengeance (x2),
  Solid-Sound Body, Sonic Boom (x2), Sound Manipulation (x2), The "Immortal" Klaw, Weapons Runner (x2)
- **Standard encounter set**: Advance (x2), Assault (x2), Caught Off Guard, Gang-Up, Shadow of the Past
- **Masters of Evil encounter set (modular, difficulty 2)**: Masters of Mayhem (x2), Melter, Radioactive Man,
  The Masters of Evil, Tiger Shark, Whirlwind

### Ultron

- **Villain deck**: Ultron (I), Ultron (II). Remove Ultron (I) and add Ultron (III) for expert mode.
- **Main scheme deck**: The Crimson Cowl, Assault on NORAD, Countdown to Oblivion
- **Ultron encounter set**: Advanced Ultron Drone (x3), Android Efficiency (x3), Drone Factory, Invasive AI,
  Program Transmitter, Rage of Ultron (x2), Repair Sequence (x2), Swarm Attack (x2), Ultron Drones,
  Ultron's Imperative, Upgraded Drones (x2)
- **Standard encounter set**: Advance (x2), Assault (x2), Caught Off Guard, Gang-Up, Shadow of the Past
- **Under Attack encounter set (modular, difficulty 3)**: Concussion Blasters, Concussive Blast (x2), Under Attack,
  Vibranium Chassis

### Other core modular sets

- **Legions of Hydra (modular, difficulty 4)**: Hydra Soldier (x3), Legions of Hydra (x2), Madame Hydra
- **The Doomsday Chair (modular, difficulty 5)**: Biomechanical Upgrades (x3), MODOK, The Doomsday Chair (x2)

Each scenario's listed modular set (Bomb Scare / Masters of Evil / Under Attack) can be removed from it and/or added to
other scenarios.

### Expert mode

Use the expert mode villain stages listed for the scenario and add the expert encounter set (in addition to the
standard set). **Expert encounter set**: Exhaustion, Masterplan, Under Fire.

### Scenario customization rules (LTP p.23)

- Use the recommended decklist for the first few plays.
- Modular sets have a difficulty level (1-5) as a guide to how much difficulty they add.
- To customize, remove the scenario's modular set and replace it with another modular set. Using more than one modular
  set is allowed but dilutes the encounter deck.
- Players may choose the modular set, or pick one at random and shuffle it in without looking at it.

## LTP vs. RR v1.8 differences an engine should follow the RR on

- **Setup**: LTP has 14 steps; RR Appendix II has 16. The RR creates the encounter deck first, then adds
  "Put Setup Cards Into Play", resolves main scheme 1A "Setup" / 1B "When Revealed" and villain "Setup"/"When Revealed"
  abilities after the encounter deck exists (LTP resolves scheme setup before building the deck), and adds
  "Campaign Setup". Use RR.
- **Enemy attack**: LTP p.16 lists give boost → declare defender → flip boost → deal damage; RR v1.8 adds the
  "Calculate damage" step and the post-attack trigger ordering. Minions only get boost cards if villainous (RR).
- **Surge**: LTP quick reference says the player "draws and reveals an additional card immediately after resolving the
  original card"; RR v1.8 Surge deals a facedown encounter card to that player instead (resolved later in the queue).
- **Deckbuilding**: LTP p.22 says "No more than one copy (by title) of each unique card"; RR Appendix I uses the
  uniqueness "match" rules and adds the team-up replacement and permanent-card exceptions.
- **Status cards**: LTP quick reference phrases confused/stunned/tough as replacement rules; RR defines them as
  Forced Interrupt abilities with priority over other Forced Interrupts.
- **Golden rule**: LTP p.9 only says card text beats "this document"; RR also ranks RR over LTP and adds the Grim Rule.
