# Magik (Age of Apocalypse)

Source: official Age of Apocalypse rulebook (MC45), p.22, https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/a2/bd/a2bd6f15-688b-4485-affa-f5a14d750d3a/mc45_age_of_apocalypse_rulebook.pdf. Rules Reference v1.8 p.64 supplies the Magik FAQ, transcribed in [faq-errata.md](faq-errata.md#magik-30a). The rulebook introduces no Magik-specific keyword or additional hero setup.

## Top card and playing from the deck

In hero form, play with the top card of Magik's player deck faceup. Once per phase she may play that card as if it were in her hand, reducing its resource cost by 1. This applies to player and villain phases independently, and all normal play restrictions and non-resource costs still apply. Resource cards cannot be played or used from the deck as payment through this permission.

The current top card changes immediately when the played card is moved to the table during initiation, before paying its resource cost. The new top card therefore determines Magik's Crown, Soulsword, Mystical Armor, Soul Strike, Exorcism, and Magic Barrier conditions as those effects resolve. If the deck empties, the ordinary immediate player-deck reset applies.

The official FAQ settles all of these interactions:

- An ability that plays a card from hand, such as Team-Building Exercise, can play the current top card once per phase.
- The played card counts as played from hand for responses such as Pixie.
- An ability that puts a card into play from hand, such as Mutant Protectors, cannot use this permission.

Illyana Rasputin's once-per-phase interrupt puts a Spell from her discard pile on top of her deck when she changes to hero form. It occurs before the form change completes, not as an after-change response.

## Card rules

- Colossus is played, paying his cost, during an enemy attack's interrupt window. He becomes the defender without exhausting; Toughness applies normally. This does not permit playing an additional copy of a unique character already in play.
- Limbo exchanges one actual hand card with the actual deck's top card. Its action and villain-phase-begin response both exhaust it.
- Magik's equipment grants benefits to the named Magik hero, even if another player controls the upgrade; its top-card condition reads the controlling player's deck. Soulsword gives piercing to basic attacks; it does not give piercing to event attacks.
- Scrying looks at up to the top three available cards and applies draw, discard, return in that order. Looking does not permit searching through a reshuffled deck to make up missing cards.
- Stepping Disc can retrieve a Magik signature card other than Stepping Disc. It cannot retrieve an aspect/basic Spell merely because it is included in her deck.
- Goldballs' bonus lasts for the interrupted attack only, not later attacks or thwarts.
- Full-Body Charge compares remaining hit points to half of the hero's starting hit points, with a strict less-than condition.
- Damage costs on Blood Rage and Bloodgem are paid through ordinary damage interrupts; if the required damage is not taken, the cost is not paid.
- Stepford Cuckoos cancels the revealed treachery's effects, discards it, and makes that same player reveal another encounter card. It uses three psi counters and exhausts for each use.
- Ruler of Limbo has fixed initial threat 3 and one amplify icon. Its restriction prevents removing threat while Belasco is in play. A find instruction can find Limbo in any eligible zone, including in play; removed-from-game cards and the victory display are excluded by RR Find. Limbo is attached facedown and returns under its owner's control when the scheme is defeated. Other facedown cards attached by Belasco are handled by normal attached-card leaves-play rules.

The rulebook's Magik/Aggression deck contains 40 player cards:

| Set | Cards |
|---|---|
| Magik (15) | Colossus, Limbo, Magik's Crown, Soulsword, Mystical Armor, Scrying, Stepping Disc ×3, Exorcism ×2, Soul Strike ×2, Magic Barrier ×2 |

Obligation: Darkchilde. Nemesis: Belasco, Ruler of Limbo, S'ym, Witchfire, Battle for Limbo, one each.
