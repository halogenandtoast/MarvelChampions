# Silk

Sources: [official rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc52_rulesheet-web.pdf), [FFG mirror](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/4b/82/4b82f3be-dc6d-4372-b37f-763e6d5308be/mc52_rulesheet-web.pdf), Rules Reference v1.8 and local FAQ/errata.

## Featured rules

**Tuck.** Tucked cards are faceup under their host, out of play, and are not attachments. If the host leaves play, discard its tucked cards. Silk's cards remain under the same physical identity when she changes form. If the identity holds more than four cards, its player chooses cards to discard until four remain.

**Encounter sets.** Silk compares the encounter-set icon on a tucked card to that on the target enemy or scheme. Encounter-set identity is independent of card type: a treachery can match a minion or scheme from its set. Cards lacking an encounter set do not match each other merely because both fields are absent.

**Requirement.** A card with Requirement must be paid for using all printed required resource types. The additional resource is part of its normal cost, not an additional cost.

**Villainous and Victory.** Atlas receives a boost card for each activation. Growth counters increase his maximum hit points continuously. Grow Invulnerable checks its loss condition while it is in play, including immediately after Atlas gains a counter.

## Errata

RR v1.8, Appendix V, changes Eidetic Memory's final sentence to refer to the identity rather than Silk. It works in alter-ego form. The swap is between cards of the same encounter set; their card types need not match.

## Rules decisions (autonomously researched, 2026-11-02)

- Silk Sense can tuck only the actual card in the encounter discard pile, after defeat or treachery resolution. Victory and other zone replacements can make that card unavailable.
- A revealed swapped treachery resolves normally and can subsequently trigger Silk Sense. Swapping does not cancel its text by itself.
- Hunting the Spider-Bride is an encounter effect, so Silk Sense Overload does not redirect its tuck. The Forced Response on the tucked treachery applies from its explicit out-of-play position.
- Discarding a tucked card as a cost still triggers Hunting the Spider-Bride's damage if its discard was caused by a player-card effect.
- Stop Hitting Yourself uses the DEF for the attack just defended, including bonuses that expire with that attack; it does not recompute unmodified current DEF.
- Ready for a Fight changes the player using it to hero form when needed, then replaces that enemy's scheme with an attack against that player. An identity already in hero form skips the form change and still resolves the replacement attack. Investigative Journalism cancels the scheme activation and then confuses the enemy.
- Redemption's host-defeat versus discard distinction is also applicable to Victory cards in these packs; see the RR v1.8 Victory X definition.

- [Alex Werner, 2025-11-14](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) clarified Silk Sense Overload: discarding it at two tucked cards is optional; removal from the game at three is mandatory.

Organic Webbing's readying and Aerial gain are separate effects. A ready Silk may pay its cost to gain Aerial; the readying does as much as possible. Spider-Man 2099's response after using a basic power also includes a basic defense.
