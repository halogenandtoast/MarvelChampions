# Maria Hill (MC50)

Source: [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc50_rulebook-web.pdf), pp. 3–7 and FAQ; Rules Reference v1.8; [published FFG rulings](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/).

All-purpose counters adopt the receiving card's counter type. A card without a defined counter type can still receive them. Adding counters is not limited to the printed starting uses. Reassignment can move a counter between any two S.H.I.E.L.D. supports, not only Maria's own supports; it is limited once per round. Her ally-trait modifier applies only while her hero face is active.

Deck construction permits either no off-aspect S.H.I.E.L.D. supports or full playsets of exactly three different off-aspect S.H.I.E.L.D. support titles. The maximum number is mandatory when using this exception (RR v1.8 FAQ). The starter deck includes The Bellerophon, The Douglass and The Pericles under this exception. The expansion contains two copies of Super Spies, but each starter deck contains only one.

Organizational Support generates the printed resources of up to three eligible exhausted allies/supports in addition to its own mental resource. Each exhausted card must share at least one trait with the current identity. Printed icons retain their types. Special Funding's optional response places its counter after the paid-for support enters play.

Life Model Decoy prevents attack damage rather than canceling the attack. The Bellerophon's damage is not an attack. The Douglass explicitly ignores crisis icons. A support discarded for The Hard Call is a cost, and its printed cost is snapshotted through its removed entity. Front Organization replaces encounter-card discards, not costs, defeats from accumulated damage, or ordinary player-card discards.

## Autonomous rulings, 2026-11-02

The owner requested decisions from the rules and published FAQ without approval questions. Use the official sources above. No owner-invented rules are recorded. Off-aspect playsets follow the published FAQ. All-Points Bulletin may choose different targets for different supports. After-entry and after-spending responses retain the physical card identity.
