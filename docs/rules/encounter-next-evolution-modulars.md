# Next Evolution modular encounter sets

Military Grade, Nasty Boys, Extreme Measures, and Mutant Insurrection use the printed text in the Next Evolution MarvelCDB data. Shared timing follows Rules Reference v1.8.

Titanium Exoskeleton limits the total damage taken from one attack, including an attack with several damage instances. Its remaining allowance resets at the next identity or ally attack, and ability damage does not consume that allowance.

Inhibitor Collar blanks an attached identity, not a minion that receives it through an unusual attachment effect. Any player can use its removal action. Taking damage as a cost requires actually taking the full damage, so a tough identity cannot pay that option by discarding tough. The official May 22 / June 17, 2025 clarification confirms that a collar attached to Zola's Mutate gives the ATK penalty without blanking it and can be removed by any player: https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/ .

Dragoness, Forearm, and Reaper count printed resource icons in hand. A wild icon does not become the requested resource merely because that resource is being counted. Cards with two printed icons contribute two. Dragoness snapshots the count for one activation and clears its modifier after that activation.

Strobe offers the stun option if at least one controlled character can receive a stun card, including a character with an unfilled steady pair. An entirely stunned or stalwart group cannot choose an ineffective stun option. Samurai similarly offers only damage or a qualifying controlled-card discard that can change the game state. A controlled player side scheme with a sufficient printed cost can pay Samurai's discard choice; discard it to its owner without resolving its defeat ability.

Extreme Measures reacts to entering play, including cards put into play without being played; the actual controller takes indirect damage equal to the card's printed cost. Zero-cost cards cause no damage. Mutant Insurrection grants toughness as an enters-play keyword; it does not immediately toughen existing minions.

Official ruling index checked for newer guidance: https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/ .
