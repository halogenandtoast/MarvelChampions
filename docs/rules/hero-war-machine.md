# War Machine Hero Pack (MC23)

Source: [official War Machine rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/53/e7/53e7fe04-5a82-4239-8ee9-9f65e6a6248d/mc23_war_machine_rulesheet.pdf), two PDF pages. The first page contains the rules and play advice; the second is artwork. The summaries below are non-normative; the linked RR entries are the current rules.

## Rulesheet keywords

The pack introduces **Alliance**: other players can contribute toward the costs of an alliance card. Only the player playing it resolves its effect. See [Alliance](glossary-a-c.md#alliance), RR v1.8 p.6.

**Hinder X** adds X threat as the card enters play. The current RR uses enters-play wording; the original insert uses revealed wording. See [Hinder X](glossary-d-h.md#hinder-x).

**Piercing** removes the attacked target's tough status cards before attack damage. **Ranged** bypasses retaliate. See [Piercing](glossary-p-r.md#piercing) and [Ranged](glossary-p-r.md#ranged).

**Team-Up** names two characters. Deck inclusion requires the identity to match one; playing it requires both named characters in play as heroes or allies. See [Team-Up](glossary-s-z.md#team-up).

## Official FAQ and errata

The rulesheet contains no Q&A. The current Rules Reference has these pack entries, transcribed in [faq-errata.md](faq-errata.md):

- Gauntlet Gun (#5): its resource ability may only be triggered while paying for a War Machine event; generating ammo alone is insufficient.
- James Rhodes (#1B): his action has a limit of once per phase.

## Starter deck

Identity: War Machine / James Rhodes. Aspect: Leadership.

Include the 15 signature cards (23002–23011, printed quantities), 19 Leadership cards (23012–23021, printed quantities), Mockingbird (23022), Quincarrier (23023), **one** Two Against the World (23024), Energy (23025), Genius (23026), and Strength (23027). This is 40 cards. The second supplied Team-Up is excluded by its maximum of one per deck. Cards 23032–23035 are customization cards.

## Implementation checks

MarvelCDB's Captain Marvel ally has THW 2 with one consequential damage and ATK 3 with two consequential damage. Her response counts printed energy resource icons, so a discarded Energy contributes two icons.

Deadly Light Show has fixed base threat 3, one crisis icon, and Hinder 1 per player. Its defeated effect deals nonattack damage to each identity through the normal interruptible damage procedure.

Ammo counters use the identity's counters. Munitions Bunker's ammo does not use the Uses keyword and an empty bunker remains in play.

## Rulings

No project-owner rulings were needed. Resource generation may supply ammo needed for the card being paid for; the full ammo cost must be available when payment finishes.

As One!'s chosen exhaustion pair must leave a meaningful attack effect; a zero-total-ATK pair cannot pay when no damage can result. A stunned identity may still pay to attempt the attack and remove stun (RR “Cost”, “Target”, “Stunned”).
