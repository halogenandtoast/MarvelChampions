# Rocket Raccoon (The Galaxy's Most Wanted)

<!-- The Galaxy's Most Wanted rulebook (campaign expansion, MC16), starter decks p20 and FAQ p21 -->

Rocket Raccoon comes in The Galaxy's Most Wanted campaign expansion with his hero set
(16029–16039), his obligation Crisis on Halfworld (16053) and his nemesis set (16054–16057:
Vendetta, Blackjack O'Hare, Blackjack's Bazooka, Planetary Invasion ×2).

## Amplify icon (rulebook p18, new)

> The amplify icon increases the number of boost icons on boost cards. When a boost card is
> turned faceup during an enemy activation, add one additional boost icon to that card for
> each amplify icon in play.

Vendetta has one amplify icon.

## Frequently Asked Questions (rulebook p21)

**Q: If the last charge counter is removed from one of Rocket Raccoon's upgrades, is that
upgrade discarded?** A: No. When Rocket Raccoon's upgrades run out of charge counters, they
remain in play. This is because Rocket Raccoon's upgrades do not have the "uses" keyword, and
that keyword itself is what discards cards that run out of counters.

**Q: If Rocket Raccoon reveals Shadow of the Past, will the amplify icon from the Vendetta side
scheme apply to Blackjack O'Hare's quickstrike attack?** A: No. Each sentence of Shadow of the
Past is resolved one at a time, meaning Blackjack O'Hare's quickstrike attack will occur before
Vendetta enters play.

## Card text checked against the printed cards

- Blackjack's Bazooka gives the attached enemy +2 ATK (printed in its stat box; MarvelCDB's
  text omits it) and reads "Attach to Blackjack O'Hare. If you cannot, attach to the villain."
- Vendetta's starting threat is 2 per player (printed with the per-player icon), no text, one
  amplify icon.
- Salvage provides a mental and a wild resource.

## Readings from the rules (not asked)

- Charge counters are the upgrades' counters, kept and shown like uses. Battery Pack, Particle
  Cannon and Rocket Launcher enter play with 2, Rocket's Pistol with 3; none is discarded at 0
  (FAQ). Hand Cannon prints "Uses (3 charge counters)" and is discarded with its last one.
- Hand Cannon's +2 ATK and overkill are a lasting effect for that attack, so the attack keeps
  them when its last charge counter, removed as the cost, discards it (RR Uses, Lasting
  Effects).
- Salvage: the cards spent with it are in your discard pile by the time it resolves ("after you
  spend this card"), so a Tech upgrade spent alongside it may be the one put back.
- Bug and Groot heal only damage they have: with none, their responses are not offered.
- Excess damage (RR Excess Damage, Overkill) is damage dealt beyond the enemy's remaining hit
  points; abilities that count it count what overkill would. A villain's excess is past the
  hit points of its current stage.

## Open rulings (implemented as read; not confirmed by the project owner)

- "Murdered You!" ("After you deal excess damage to an enemy, draw 1 card") answers any damage
  Rocket deals — by attack or not (Rocket Launcher) — with his identity or a card he controls,
  not his allies' (RR "You"). Once per instance, no limit.
- Follow Through: "your hero's attack" is a hero attack's damage (a basic attack, an (attack)
  event or ability) and the damage its overkill deals to the villain (RR Overkill: damage from
  an attack). Each copy may answer each instance once, so several copies add up. The excess it
  adds counts for the overkill dealt past a minion and for everything counting that excess
  (Into the Fray, Moment of Triumph, "Murdered You!").
- Schadenfreude: each enemy you deal damage to is a separate "time" — Rocket Launcher hitting
  the villain and two minions heals 2 three times. Damage prevented is still dealt (RR Prevent:
  prevention reduces the damage taken, not the damage dealt), so damage a tough status card
  prevents heals too; damage by your allies is not "you".
- Crisis on Halfworld: with upgrades tied for highest cost you choose which to discard. "Exhaust
  your alter-ego" is offered only to a ready alter-ego (RR Choose (Option)).
- Battery Pack needs a charge counter on itself and another Tech upgrade you control to move it
  to; any Tech upgrade may receive one.
- Booster Boots: discarding the top card of your deck is part of the cost, so with an empty
  deck it cannot be used.
- Rocket Launcher is not an attack (no "(attack)" label): its damage is an ability's, to the
  villain and each minion engaged with the chosen player as one effect.
- Vendetta enters play with its threat; its amplify icon adds one boost icon to each boost card
  turned faceup for an enemy's activation while it is in play (RR Amplify Icon).
