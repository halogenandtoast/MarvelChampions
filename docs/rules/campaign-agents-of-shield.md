# Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. campaign (MC50)

Source: [official rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc50_rulebook-web.pdf), pp. 4–6, 9, 11, 13, 15, 18–19, 24; Rules Reference v1.8; [FFG rulings, November 21, 2025](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/).

Play Black Widow, Batroc, M.O.D.O.K., Thunderbolts and Baron Zemo in order. Identities remain fixed; decks/aspects may change. Campaign setup runs after normal scenario setup and before drawing opening hands; earned evidence setup runs after mulligans.

Prepare evidence with the stored game RNG: separately shuffle three means, three motives and three opportunities, hide one of each, then shuffle the six others into the S.H.I.E.L.D. envelope. Persist both envelopes across scenarios, but expose only earned evidence through the public campaign API. The mapping of all 27 combinations to board members is the printed campaign log on p.24, not an arithmetic guess.

Board secrets persist; a board member that flipped to an attachment remains betrayed for the campaign. The flip keeps its counters. A victory with a zero-secret board environment grants one evidence card. Record remaining minions plus side schemes after scenario one, surviving rescued captives after two, Adaptoid environments after three, and surviving Thunderbolts after four. Those results change Alert Level, Holding Cell locks and the final encounter setup.

Each evidence setup permits an optional collection addition paid by a secret counter, and an optional deck search paid by one main-scheme threat. Newly added cards bypass deck-building copy limits, including a fourth copy (published FFG ruling). Different players must choose different titles for the same evidence setup; existing deck copies do not disqualify a choice.

The accusation chooses a still-possible means/motive/opportunity combination and its associated board member. Each wrong guess, including the board member, adds one secret to every board member; accusing the wrong member additionally adds three secrets to that member. Reveal the actual mole and attach its reverse side. The final scheme gains one threat per player for each secret on board-member attachments.

Expert play carries remaining hit points between scenarios, capped at printed HP. Before each later scenario, each player may add one secret to a board environment to heal their REC. An eliminated player must use this option to rejoin. Losing the final expert scenario loses the campaign. Retrying the final standard scenario resets the evidence and awards one evidence card for each earlier qualifying victory.

## Autonomous decisions, 2026-11-02

The owner explicitly requested no approval questions. All rules decisions above follow the rulebook, RR v1.8 and published designer rulings. Hidden information remains hidden from spectators and other players until evidence is earned or the mole is exposed.

Expert recovery resolves after the starting-hand and evidence setup steps, with the restored board already in play. Accusation penalties resolve in order: distribute the wrong-guess secrets, finish any resulting betrayals, then place the additional three secrets on the accused member in its current form. The final setup excludes Jolt’s encounter set when adding surviving Thunderbolts.
