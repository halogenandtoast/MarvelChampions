# Luke Cage

Source: [FFG Luke Cage rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc62_rulesheet-web.pdf), checked 2026-11-02. Card text: MarvelCDB pack `luke_cage`. See RR1.8 and `faq-errata.md` for ordinary timing.

## Rulesheet rules

**Unpreventable damage:** damage of this kind cannot be reduced or prevented. A tough status card neither stops it nor is removed by it. This covers constant damage reduction as well as interrupts.

**Piercing:** an attack with piercing discards all tough status cards on its target before dealing damage. Luke's constant ability removes piercing from attacks against him in both forms; it does not prevent a card ability explicitly discarding tough status cards.

**Team-Up:** the chosen identity must match one of the two named characters to include the card in its deck. Both named characters must be in play to play it; a named character may be represented by an identity or ally.

**Prerequisite:** playing the card requires its player's identity to be in the stated form or have the stated trait.

## Implementation rulings (sources checked 2026-11-02)

The owner authorized published-source rules decisions without approval requests.

- Luke can have any number of tough cards in either form. His hero-side forced response deals one unpreventable damage after each individual tough card discarded; it does not trigger while his alter ego is faceup.
- Unpreventable damage leaves every remaining tough card intact and cannot be reduced by Metal Bracer.
- Knuckle Sandwich's optional tough discard happens after its attack, and returning the event occurs after it reaches the discard pile. That discard opens normal tough-discard response windows.
- Burstein Process tests Luke's number of tough cards as its effect resolves: none gives two; one or more gives one.
- Harlem's Hero determines the number of readies after giving its tough card; choose characters separately as each ready resolves.
- Cottonmouth's additional attacks initially target the previous attack's character. Players may defend direct attacks against allies normally (RR1.8, Attacks Against Allies). His once-per-phase limit prevents recursive extra attacks.
- Valkyrie's replacement heals its protected ally to one remaining hit point and replaces defeat; it does not bring a defeated ally back into play.

Recent-ruling search checked [FFG's RR1.8 update](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/mission-updates/) and current indexed rulings on 2026-11-02. No later official pack-specific correction was found. The supplied FFG rulesheet explicitly settles unpreventable damage.
