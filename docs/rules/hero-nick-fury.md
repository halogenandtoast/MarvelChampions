# Nick Fury (MC50)

Source: [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc50_rulebook-web.pdf), pp. 3–7 and FAQ; Rules Reference v1.8.

Assault/Stealth is one permanent, double-sided suit-form upgrade. Suit forms supplement hero/alter-ego forms; changing a suit does not consume the normal once-per-turn identity change, but counts as changing form. All suit threat survives either kind of form change. Setup puts Assault into play after starting-hand/mulligan setup.

Break Cover resolves before the attack's damage. Assault's optional interrupt can remove up to three suit threat to increase that attack's damage by the amount removed. The suit change is forced, and the damage increase is optional.

Stealth replaces an attack against Nick in hero form with a new scheme activation. A confused status replaces the resulting scheme, so no threat is collected. A stunned enemy never reaches the attack interrupt. When Nick defends another player's attack, Stealth does not retroactively replace that initiated attack. One point of the scheme's threat is redirected to the suit only if the suit had at most five threat before placement.

Informant changes a minion's scheme to threat removal. The printed FAQ preserves Stealth's one diverted suit threat, with the remaining scheme amount removed from its scheme instead. Covert Surveillance and Maria Hill's ally ability use the amount actually removed, not their nominal thwart amount. Concentrated Fire uses the defeated enemy's printed SCH. Fury's Flying Car and Organic Webbing-style readies do not restore the normal identity form change.

## Autonomous rulings, 2026-11-02

The owner requested rules/FAQ research without approval questions. The rulebook and RR FAQ govern Stealth's confusion and cross-player defense interactions. EM Shield prevents damage and is a defense ability; it does not cancel the attack's other effects. Eyepatch Camera redirects zero to three of the threat actually pending for the main scheme.

Secret Agent moves threat; it does not thwart. It neither opens a thwart window nor receives bonuses to threat removed by thwarting. A source barred from losing threat cannot supply the move, and a crisis icon still prevents moving main-scheme threat. Covert Surveillance transfers only threat actually removed. Nick Fury, Sr. may give tough to any S.H.I.E.L.D. character, including an enemy with that trait.

The [published FFG ruling of November 21, 2025](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) says Show of Empathy changes a moved token’s destination to a minion. By the same rule, Secret Agent may choose that scheme, but the token goes to the minion instead of the suit.
