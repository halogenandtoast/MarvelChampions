## Summary (non-normative)

```
Villain: Collector (A1), or (B1) for expert. +X SCH/+X ATK, X = the main scheme's stage. Would be defeated: remove
  3 per player threat from the main scheme and flip to Wounded (A2/B2) instead. Wounded: infinite hit points (cannot
  be defeated, still takes damage); as the round ends, flip back and reset the hit point dial.
Stages: 1 The Missing Milano, 2 Lost in the Museum, 3 The Great Escape. The players advance by removing the LAST
  threat from a stage; a stage completed (threshold reached) loses. 3B empty of threat: the players win.
1A: Library Labyrinth into play; the Ship Command set set aside (the Milano too). 2A: the Milano back, under the
  first player. 3A: Library Labyrinth flips to Museum Ship, 1 acceleration token, the rest of Ship Command shuffled in.
```

## Scenario
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.12 -->

***So... things didn't go as planned. The good: you broke into the Collector's museum; you beat the Collector; you
know the Power Stone was here. The bad: an alarm went off as soon as you arrived, alerting the Collector to your
presence; after you beat the Collector, he used his Elder God magic to trap you in his collection; also, someone else
seems to have stolen the Power Stone.***

***Kind of a mixed bag here. On the bright side, whoever took the Power Stone cut the museum's power as a
distraction, giving you enough time to get out of captivity. Maybe, just maybe, you can leverage this luck and
escape the museum before the Collector realizes what's going on.***

**Villain Deck**: Collector (A1)

*Remove Collector (A1) and add Collector (B1) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: The Missing Milano (1A/1B), Lost in the Museum (2A/2B), The Great Escape (3A/3B)

**Encounter Deck**: Escape the Museum, Galactic Artifacts, Menagerie Medley, Ship Command, and Standard encounter
sets.

*The Menagerie Medley modular set can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when using
scenario customization rules.*

## Infinite Hit Points (New)
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.12 -->

In this scenario, the villain has infinite hit points (∞). A character with infinite hit points cannot be defeated by
taking damage. However, damage may still be dealt to that character through attacks and card abilities.

## Campaign Instructions
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.12 -->

### Setup

- Reveal the "There Is No Escape" (16180A) side scheme (use the reverse side for expert mode).
- Each player must search their deck, discard pile, and hand for each of their cards recorded in the "Cards In The
  Collection" section of the campaign log, then remove those cards from the game. Each player draws up to their hand
  size.
- Shuffle the Badoon Headhunter (16183) minion into the encounter deck. Then, if the number of marks in the
  "Headhunter Defeated?" section of the campaign log is equal to or greater than:
  - (1) Shuffle the On the Hunt (16184) treachery into the encounter deck.
  - (2) Shuffle the Dead to Rights (16185) treachery into the encounter deck.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign
  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may subtract 1 unit from their respective "Unspent Units" box in the campaign
  log to heal their identity to its printed hit point value.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: In player order, each player must discard cards from the top of the encounter deck until
  they discard an attachment, then reveal that card.

### Victory

In the campaign log:

- Record units in each player's respective "Unspent Units" box *(add these to units you already have)*:
  - Record 1 unit for each player.
  - Record a number of units for each player (to a maximum of 3 units) equal to the victory values on encounter cards
    in the victory display.
  - For every 2 Galactic Artifacts side schemes in the victory display, record 1 unit for each player.
- Record the title of each Galactic Artifacts side scheme in the victory display.
- If Badoon Headhunter is in the victory display, mark the box beside "Scenario #3 - Escape the Museum" in the
  "Headhunter Defeated?" section.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **Infinite hit points** is the modifier `InfiniteHitPoints`, which the Wounded sides give themselves. Damage dealt
  to such a villain is dealt (the windows after damage open, and the damage is told), but it takes nothing off his
  hit point dial and never defeats him; the board shows ∞ (`infiniteHitPoints` in the villain's view) and no damage.
- **"Would be defeated … instead"**: a villain whose hit points are gone is first told `VillainWouldBeDefeated`; by
  default that defeats him. Collector (A1, B1) answers it instead: the window after his defeat is cancelled, so
  nothing that answers "after you defeat" sees one, 3[per hero] threat is removed from the main scheme (by the
  villain, not a thwart), and he flips to his Wounded side keeping his status cards and attachments.
- **The round ends**: the Wounded side's forced interrupt flips him back, then his hit point dial is set to the A1 or
  B1 side's printed hit points.
- **Stage number**: X is the stage of Escape the Museum's main scheme in play (The Missing Milano 1, Lost in the
  Museum 2, The Great Escape 3).
- **Advancing**: a stage's threat removed to none, by thwarting or not, advances to the next (3B: the players win).
  Threat reaching the stage's threshold is its completion: the players lose. Initial threat is placed before the A
  side's When Revealed resolves, as for every main scheme in the engine.
- **The Milano**: the Ship Command set is in the encounter deck, so its Setup card, the Milano, enters play before
  1A's Setup (RR Appendix II, step 11). 1A's Setup then sets the whole set aside, taking the Milano out of play to the
  set-aside area though it is permanent: setting aside is not leaving play by discard or defeat (`SetAside` of a
  card in play removes it). 2A puts it into play under the first player, and it follows the first player token from
  then on. 3A shuffles the set's other six cards into the encounter deck.
- **Library Labyrinth** is put into play by 1A, so it is not shuffled into the encounter deck. Its Hero Action deals
  the encounter card first, then removes 5 threat from the main scheme (not a thwart: patrol does not stop it).
- **Museum Ship**: "among players" indirect damage is divided as the group chooses among all friendly characters in
  play (RR Indirect Damage); the first player, who makes the group's decisions, chooses the option and assigns the
  damage a point at a time. "Exhaust the Milano" is open only with the Milano in play and ready.
- **"I Have You Now!"**: Collector is the villain. One already tough is given no second tough status card.
- **Impossible Geometry**: already confused, the player chooses an ally, upgrade or support they control to discard;
  a permanent card (the Milano) cannot leave play, so it is no choice. With none, nothing is discarded.

## Open rulings

- **The Milano at setup.** The rulebook's Setup keyword puts the Milano into play before the main scheme's Setup sets
  the Ship Command set aside; the engine follows that order, setting the permanent Milano aside from play (setting
  aside is not discarding). The observable result is the same as never putting it into play.
- **Collector's interrupt removing the last threat.** When his "would be defeated" interrupt removes the last threat
  from 1B or 2B, the stage advances (its own forced interrupt) before he flips; the next stage's When Revealed
  resolves before the flip.
