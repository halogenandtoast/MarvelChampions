# Civil War leaders and signature attachments

The six leaders use stages I–II on normal and III–IV on expert. Advancing to the final stage deals each player on that board an encounter card and prevents further damage to that leader for the current phase. In competitive play, final defeat records that board's result and keeps the leader available through the other team's reply.

The native handlers cover Iron Man's Tech-based SCH, Captain Marvel's defeat counters, Captain America's Shield response, Spider-Woman's Finesse attack bonus, She-Hulk's form-change damage, and Vision's mass-form flip after the threat step. Signature attachments include Energy Channel, Cap's Shield, Finesse, Superhuman Strength, Dense, and Intangible. Other leader encounter cards and scenario registration are separate work.

Retaliate joins the attack's Forced Response occurrence. Players can order it alongside an encounter response. Temporary retaliate granted by Gauntlets of Hercules lasts through the relevant attack's completion. Defense response windows and delayed defense damage share that occurrence, so optional defense responses do not precede forced retaliation. Riposte can be ordered alongside an enemy’s Forced Response. The Shield does not return when the attack deals no damage.

ATK-based event damage is recorded separately from basic attacks and flat damage. This allows the Shield to respond to Counter-Punch without treating every attack event as a use of ATK. For As One!, the engine applies that requirement by checking whether the attacking hero contributed ATK through its exhaustion payment. Basic attack damage from overkill retains its attribution.

## Rulings checked

[FFG rulings collected by Hall of Heroes](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/), checked November 3, 2026:

- December 17, 2025: ATK-based attack abilities can transfer Cap's Shield; flat damage abilities cannot.
- January 11, 2026: overkill using ATK can transfer the Shield. Captain Marvel can gain two counters by defeating an ally and identity with one overkill attack. Opposing Civil War factions' modular sets cannot be mixed in cooperative play.
- January 26 and June 2, 2026: Energy Channel always adds its counter, and clears four or more counters even when the attacker has left play. Its attempted counterattack then has no target.

The official [Rules Reference update](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/mission-updates/) explains retaliate's Forced Response timing. The local v1.8 text is in `glossary-p-r.md`, “Retaliate X”.

## Iron Man's encounter attachments

Powered Gauntlets, Rocket Boots, Mark V Helmet, Arc Reactor, and Mark V Armor are implemented. Their Tech traits contribute to Iron Man's capped SCH bonus; printed boost abilities reveal and attach the relevant cards. Resource payments after a basic attack discard the four removable equipment cards.

Mark V Armor's printed “would … instead” wording is handled as a damage replacement, despite its Forced Response heading. Damage that survives prevention is placed entirely on the Armor, which is discarded at five or more damage without spilling excess onto Iron Man. Tough resolves first. This follows the replacement timing described in the local Rules Reference; no specific ruling changing that heading was found.

Competitive attachment searches stay on the current board unless they explicitly name an attachment or its host. This allows effects targeting the friendly leader across the table without exposing unrelated opposing attachments to ordinary searches.

The focused Civil War suite passes 98 examples, including eleven Iron Man equipment and setup cases.

## Additional leader equipment

Captain Marvel's Helmet, Captain America's Helmet, Super-Soldier Serum, Focused Rage, Solar Gem, Vision's Cape, and Density Control are implemented. Helmet removal requires three resources of the same type. Vision's removable equipment flips the existing mass-form attachment, preserving its identity and host. Density Control changes the form before the activation and remains attached if the requested form is already showing. Mass-form flips are narrated in the game log.

The fifteen equipment cases cover costs and removal, boosts, Serum overkill, Focused Rage's scheme activation, and Density Control's attack, scheme, and unchanged-form branches.

## Defensive equipment

Cosmic Flight, Contaminant Immunity, and Mass Increase complete the leaders' attachment cards. Cosmic Flight responds only to attack damage above three after prevention, stays attached when tough prevents the attack, and leaves a second copy in play after the first reduces the damage to three. Damage interrupt windows refresh from the pending damage so subsequent interrupts see the updated amount.

Villains now have an interrupt window before gaining a possible status. Contaminant Immunity replaces the pending stun or confusion with its own discard and a tough status; it does not replace an impossible or redundant status. The narrator reports the status after that replacement opportunity.

Mass Increase responds to hero, ally, and leader attacks while Dense is attached. It stuns the attacking character and prevents the attack's remaining damage, including other targets of one attack event. A lasting effect survives the attachment's departure and expires when that attack ends. A save/load test resumes Melee between its two targets with this prevention still active.

The focused Civil War suite passes 129 examples, including cross-board leader attacks and both mass forms. The full unit suite passes 8,053 examples, and the two-seed Rhino, Klaw, and Kang smoke selection passes all 25 games. The local Rules Reference's Interrupt, Replacement Effect, Damage, and Tough entries govern these effects; a search for newer card-specific rulings found none changing their printed instructions.

## Iron Man and Captain Marvel treacheries

Repulsor Blast and Supersonic Punch are implemented in both identity forms, including counting individual printed energy/wild resources and Iron Man's Tech-dependent confusion or piercing. Photonic Blast, Crisis Interdiction, and Energy Absorption handle their form-dependent effects, energy counters, every scheme on the local board, and healing from the updated counter total. Energy Absorption's boost also adds its counter.

Twelve behavior tests cover these five cards; the focused Civil War suite now passes 141 examples. Other leaders' treacheries, leader side schemes, main schemes, modular sets, and scenario registration remain separate work.

Shield Block and Shield Toss reattach the existing Shield, preserving its physical identity and detaching its previous host. Shield Toss damages all controlled characters together when the Shield is already on Captain America. Pheromones applies both statuses and its additional skill counter, with Finesse capped at three for every counter-placement source. Its boost confuses only when its attempted stun did not add a status card. One-Two Punch activates She-Hulk before giving her tough. Ground Stomp exhausts ready controlled characters, surging only when none were exhausted; its boost offers one ready character. Surge deals a facedown encounter card under v1.8.

Fourteen additional cases cover these five cards and their boosts; 155 focused Civil War examples pass.
