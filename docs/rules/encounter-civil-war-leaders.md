# Civil War leaders and signature attachments

The six leaders use stages I–II on normal and III–IV on expert. Advancing to the final stage deals each player on that board an encounter card and prevents further damage to that leader for the current phase. In competitive play, final defeat records that board's result and keeps the leader available through the other team's reply.

The native handlers cover Iron Man's Tech-based SCH, Captain Marvel's defeat counters, Captain America's Shield response, Spider-Woman's Finesse attack bonus, She-Hulk's form-change damage, and Vision's mass-form flip after the threat step. Signature attachments include Energy Channel, Cap's Shield, Finesse, Superhuman Strength, Dense, and Intangible. Other leader encounter cards and scenario registration are separate work.

Retaliate joins the attack's Forced Response occurrence. Players can order it alongside an encounter response. Temporary retaliate granted by Gauntlets of Hercules lasts through the relevant attack's completion. Defense response windows and delayed defense damage share that occurrence, so optional defense responses do not precede forced retaliation. Riposte can be ordered alongside an enemy’s Forced Response. The Shield does not return when the attack deals no damage.

ATK-based event damage is recorded separately from basic attacks and flat damage. This allows the Shield to respond to Counter-Punch without treating every attack event as a use of ATK. For As One!, the engine applies that requirement by checking whether the attacking hero contributed ATK through its exhaustion payment. Basic attack damage from overkill retains its attribution.

## Rulings checked

[FFG rulings collected by Hall of Heroes](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/), checked November 3, 2026:

- December 17, 2025: ATK-based attack abilities can transfer Cap's Shield; flat damage abilities cannot.
- January 11, 2026: overkill using ATK can transfer the Shield. Captain Marvel can gain two counters by defeating an ally and identity with one overkill attack. Opposing Civil War factions' modular sets cannot be mixed in cooperative play.
- January 26 and June 2, 2026: Energy Channel always adds its counter, and clears four or more counters even when the attacker has left play. Its attempted counterattack then has no target.

The official [Rules Reference update](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/mission-updates/) explains retaliate's Forced Response timing. The local v1.8 text is in `glossary-p-r.md`, “Retaliate X”.

## Verification

The complete unit suite passes: 8,011 examples. A two-seed smoke run with Spider-Man and Black Panther against Rhino, Klaw, and Kang passes all 25 selected games, including standard/expert variants. Leader tests cover both stage progressions, phase-limited immunity, Shield timing, ATK events and overkill, dead-attacker Energy Channel handling, Finesse, both mass forms, Superhuman Strength, and save/load during a response choice.
