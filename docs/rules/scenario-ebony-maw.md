## Summary (non-normative)

```
Villain deck: Ebony Maw (I), (II); expert (II), (III). Main schemes: Attack on Knowhere (1A/1B), The Power
  Stone (2A/2B). Encounter deck: Ebony Maw, Black Order, Armies of Titan, Standard (+ Expert).
Spell environments (Fireball, Manipulation, Pacification, Rubblestorm, 2 each) go into the play area of the player
  who reveals them, with 2-4 invocation counters. When the last counter is removed: discard it, then its effect hits
  that player. Each has surge when revealed; put into play (1B, 2A, Maw II/III, Channeling Trance), it does not surge.
Ebony Maw, each stage: when he activates against you, remove a counter from each Spell in your play area first.
  A stunned attack / confused scheme removes the status card instead, and the interrupt does not happen.
1B, 2A, Maw II and III When Revealed: each player discards from the encounter deck until a Spell, and puts it into
  play in their own area (1B then shuffles the discard pile back; 2A shuffles it back first).
2B completed: the players lose. Maw's last stage defeated: the players win.
```

## New rules
<!-- MTS rulebook p.2 -->

**Amplify Icon**

The amplify icon increases the number of boost icons on boost cards. When a boost card is turned faceup during an
enemy activation, add one additional boost icon to that card for each amplify icon in play.

*For a list of important keywords, see page 13.*

## Important keywords
<!-- MTS rulebook p.13 -->

**Hinder X** — When a card with the hinder X keyword is revealed, place X threat on that card.

**Incite X** — When a card with the incite X keyword is revealed, place X threat on the main scheme.

**Permanent** — A card with the permanent keyword cannot leave play.

**Piercing** — An attack with the piercing keyword discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack
before dealing damage.

**Ranged** — An attack with the ranged keyword ignores the retaliate keyword.

**Setup** — A card with the setup keyword begins the game in play.

**Stalwart** — A character with the stalwart keyword cannot be stunned or confused.

**Villainous** — When a minion with the villainous keyword activates, give it a facedown boost card from the top of
the encounter deck. When you resolve that minion's activation, turn the boost card faceup and apply its boost icons
to the minion's stats for the activation. If the boost card has a boost ability, resolve its effects. Discard the
boost card after the activation.

**Victory X** — When a card with the victory X keyword is defeated, place it in the victory display instead of its
owner's discard pile. The victory display is a named, out-of-play game area shared by all players. Cards in the
victory display follow the standard rules for out-of-play cards.

## Scenario
<!-- MTS rulebook p.6 -->

**SCENARIO #1 - EBONY MAW**

*The Mad Titan, Thanos, is on a quest to collect all six Infinity Stones and join them together in the Infinity
Gauntlet. If he succeeds, Thanos will have the power to control the universe — or destroy it.*

*His fanatical followers, the Black Order, have pledged their lives to the Titan's cause, and they scour the
universe for the only two stones that their master still needs to complete the Gauntlet: the Power Stone and the
Reality Stone.*

*One of those followers, Ebony Maw, has discovered that the Power Stone is being kept in a secure vault on
Knowhere. Now, the Black Order's sorcerer is leading an army of Thanos in a direct assault on the space station.
Cosmo and the rest of the Knowhere Corps are doing what they can to defend the station, but they won't last long
against Maw without your help!*

**Villain Deck**: Ebony Maw (I), Ebony Maw (II)

*Remove Ebony Maw (I) and add Ebony Maw (III) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: Attack on Knowhere, The Power Stone

**Encounter Deck**: Ebony Maw, Black Order, Armies of Titan, and Standard sets. (The Standard set can be found in
the *Marvel Champions* core set.)

*The Black Order and Armies of Titan sets can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when
using the scenario customization rules.*

## Spell environment cards
<!-- MTS rulebook p.6 -->

Ebony Maw is a servant of Thanos and a powerful sorcerer. To represent his deadly magic in the game, Ebony Maw's
set contains eight ***Spell*** environment cards. When a player reveals a ***Spell*** environment, they place that
card in front of them in their play area.

Each of these ***Spell*** environments enters play with a specified number of "invocation counters" on them. When
Ebony Maw activates against a player, his ability text triggers: "**Forced Interrupt**: When Ebony Maw activates
against you, remove an invocation counter from each ***Spell*** in your play area." Because this is an interrupt
ability, it resolves immediately when Ebony Maw activates and before that activation is resolved.

For example, Ebony Maw activates against Spider-Man, who has two ***Spell*** environment cards in his play area:
Rubblestorm and Fireball. Rubblestorm has three invocation counters on it, and Fireball has one. Ebony Maw's
ability removes one invocation counter from each environment card, reducing Rubblestorm's invocation counters to two
and Fireball's to zero. This causes Fireball's ability to trigger: "**Forced Response**: After the last invocation
counter is removed from Fireball, discard it → Deal 4 damage to your identity." Spider-Man immediately takes four
damage; then Ebony Maw continues resolving his activation.

## Rules clarification
<!-- MTS rulebook p.7 -->

**Q.** The "**When Revealed**" text on stage 1B tells players to put a ***Spell*** environment into their play area.
Does this trigger its surge keyword?
**A.** No. The surge keyword is only resolved when the card it appears on is revealed. If the card is simply "put
into play," it is not revealed.

**Q.** If Ebony Maw is stunned or confused, does his **Forced Interrupt** still trigger when he activates against
me?
**A.** No. If Ebony Maw is prevented from attacking or scheming by a status card, he removes the status card instead
of resolving his activation.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **A Spell environment is in the play area of the player who revealed it, or put it into play**, not the villain's
  (the RR's Environment entry is overridden by this scenario's page). It is still an environment in play for
  everything that counts environments ("each environment in play"), shown beside that player's identity with its
  invocation counters. "Your" on it is that player. When that player is eliminated, it is discarded with the other
  encounter cards in their play area (RR Player Elimination step 3).
- **Invocation counters** are the environment's counters; "enters play with" places them whether it was revealed or
  put into play. "After the last invocation counter is removed": removed by any effect, from one or more to none.
  "Discard it →" is the cost: the card goes to the encounter discard pile, then the effect resolves.
- **Ebony Maw's Forced Interrupt**: his attack against you (the villain phase, Assault, any card) or his scheme in
  your alter-ego's activation. A stunned attack or confused scheme is not resolved, its status card removed
  instead, and the interrupt does not happen (rulebook p.7). It resolves before the activation's boost card is
  dealt. The Spells keeping a counter lose theirs at once; of those losing their last, the first player chooses the
  order when there are two or more (RR Simultaneous Resolution), each resolving its response before the next loses
  its counter (the rulebook's example). Only Spell environments carry invocation counters, so a
  player's own Spell cards are untouched.
- **"Discards cards from the top of the encounter deck until they discard a Spell card and puts that card into
  play"**: player by player from the first player, each looking at the deck as the previous player left it. The
  Spell is put into play, not revealed: no surge. A deck that runs out first fulfils it (RR Encounter Deck): that
  player gets no Spell, and the deck is reset with an acceleration token as usual. Landing Craft and Channeling
  Trance discard the same way.
- **Expert setup**: 1B's When Revealed and Ebony Maw (II)'s each resolve, so each player begins with two Spells
  (1B's, then Maw's; 1B shuffles the discard pile back in between).
- **Agent of Thanos**: X is the Spell environments in your play area as it resolves; it gains surge when X is 0
  (as Stall Tactics: the threat or damage the card would place, not what an interrupt then prevents).
- **Abjuration** stops all damage to Ebony Maw (`CannotTakeDamage`), before his tough status card would: a tough Maw
  attacked under Abjuration keeps his tough card. "2 or more damage from a single attack": the damage of a hero's or
  ally's attack (overkill included) that Abjuration stopped; damage that is not an attack's (an event's, Rubblestorm
  cannot hit him) is prevented without discarding it.
- **Restrained**: "friendly character with the highest ATK" is any player's hero or ally (an alter-ego has no ATK),
  ties chosen by the player revealing it; with no hero or ally in play it is discarded. Only the player controlling
  the attached hero or ally may use its Hero Action, in hero form (RR Attachment: "Only the player who controls the
  card to which that attachment is attached can trigger abilities or pay costs on that attachment"). On an ally, it
  is discarded when the ally leaves play by any means (defeated, returned to hand, removed from the game).
- **Blood to Spare**: each minion engaged with a player activates against them (attacks a hero, schemes against an
  alter-ego), player by player; then the players with no minion engaged, counted after those activations, each
  search for a Black Order minion. A unique one already in play is not put into play again.
- **The villain** of Black Order Infantry is the active villain.

## Open rulings

- **Abjuration and a tough status card**: whether the tough card or Abjuration's prevention applies first is not
  stated. The engine applies Abjuration first (a constant ability, so the damage is never dealt and the tough card
  is not discarded), as it already does for every "cannot take damage" effect.
- **Agent of Thanos "If you take no damage / place no threat"**: read as X = 0 (the precedent of Stall Tactics),
  not as damage or threat an interrupt then prevents.
- **Several Spells reaching zero at once** (Ebony Maw, Channeling Trance): settled below (the first player orders
  them).
- **Expert setup order**: 1B's When Revealed resolves before Ebony Maw (II)'s (the engine's setup order for every
  scenario), so 1B's shuffle happens between them.
- **No Spell left in the encounter deck** (all eight in play): "discard until" empties the deck, which is reset
  with an acceleration token; that player gets no Spell (RR Encounter Deck).

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-28)

- **Simultaneous forced abilities**: two or more Spells losing their last invocation counter at once (Ebony Maw's
  interrupt, Channeling Trance) respond in the order the first player chooses (RR Simultaneous Resolution); with one,
  nothing is asked.
- **Abjuration and tough**: Abjuration prevents the damage first; a tough Ebony Maw keeps his tough status card.
- **Abjuration's "single attack"**: a hero's or ally's attack, or the overkill excess of one past a minion, counts;
  damage that is not an attack's is prevented without discarding it.
- **Agent of Thanos**: "no damage / no threat" is X = 0 (Stall Tactics), not damage or threat later prevented.
- **Expert setup**: 1B's When Revealed, then Ebony Maw (II)'s: each player begins with two Spells.
- **Restrained**: any player's hero or ally with the highest ATK, ties the revealing player's choice, none: discarded.
  Its Hero Action is the attached character's controller's (RR Attachment), not any player's.
- **"Each Spell card in your play area"**: Spell environments only; a player's own Spell cards carry no counters.
- **No Spell left in the encounter deck**: the deck is discarded (fulfilled, reset with an acceleration token) and that
  player gets none.
- **Blood to Spare**: who is engaged with a minion is counted after the activations.
- **An eliminated player's Spells** are discarded (RR Player Elimination step 3).
