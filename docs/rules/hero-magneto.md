## Rules insert — implementation summary

<!-- MC49 insert p.1; non-verbatim summary -->

Player side schemes enter with their starting threat and share the table's player-side-scheme limit. Defeated schemes with Victory enter the victory display. Linked allies begin set aside when their parent card is included in a deck; they are outside deck-size limits. New Recruits brings its linked allies into the game.

Steady requires two matching stun/confuse statuses to replace an activation, removing both when replaced. Villainous minions receive and resolve boost cards during activations. Victory cards enter the victory display when defeated.

## Current errata

Magnetic Missile discards the wrapped minion as an effect, followed by a “Then” clause, rather than as a cost. Exodus uses its total ATK for the attack just resolved. The reprinted Deft Focus is Basic.

## Source-based rulings selected under owner authorization

The owner authorized autonomous rules research on 2026-11-01; these are documented source decisions, not owner-supplied rulings.

- Magnetic Pull must find a Magnetic card to count as resolved for Armor and Cape (FFG, 2024-11-08, answer 33). An unsuccessful attempt still counts as using the ability for Old Grievances.
- “You Got This!” reads the ally's printed power after it is discarded, excluding its former in-play modifiers (FFG, 2025-01-24, answer 68).
- Mysterio's nested illusion interrupts can rebuild the player deck before Magnetic Pull resumes; discarding then continues from the rebuilt deck (FFG, 2025-01-24, answer 69).
- Armor checks printed resource icons on the cards actually discarded; a printed wild is not simultaneously the other three icons.
- Magnetic Bubble absorbs the entire instance that reaches its threshold; excess damage does not spill onto Magneto.
- Wrapped in Metal suppresses printed text and activations; its non-Elite attachment restriction is checked when attached.

## Starter deck

Magneto/Leadership: cards 49002–49026, at the printed quantities, total 40. The four Linked New allies are set aside by New Recruits and do not belong in the deck. Hellfire is the optional modular encounter set.
