# Standard III — Age of Apocalypse

Source: official MC45 rulebook, pp.2–3; cards 45075–45080 in the local MarvelCDB dump.
Rules Reference v1.8 applies, including Find, nemesis sets, activation timing, and costs.

Standard III replaces the core Standard set; it is not shuffled alongside Standard or Standard II.
Pursued by the Past begins in play through Setup. It is permanent, and its reverse is not a separate encounter-deck copy.

Pursuit counters are placed for the player resolving the instruction. After placement reaches at least the starting player count plus three, remove every pursuit counter. If that player's designated nemesis minion is in play, it activates against that player. Otherwise, turn the environment over, find and reveal that physical nemesis minion, reveal the nemesis side scheme still set aside, shuffle the remaining set-aside nemesis cards into the encounter deck, and turn the environment back.

Find searches eligible known zones, including held cards and the Collection. It excludes the victory display, cards removed from the game, and facedown dealt encounter cards. A found physical card moves; a duplicate is never generated.

Dark Designs, Sinister Strike, and Nowhere Is Safe check whether pursuit counters remain only after the placement and its threshold response have resolved. Evil Alliance activates each nemesis minion in play, including one belonging to an eliminated player; with none, it places three pursuit counters. The star effects on Dark Designs, Evil Alliance, and Nowhere Is Safe place their counter after the current activation finishes.

Drawing Near remains in its owner's play area when revealed. Its turn-start forced response counts printed icons on the discarded card. Removing it is an alter-ego action paid by discarding an identity-specific hand card.
