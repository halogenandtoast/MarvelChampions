# Kree Fanatic (print-and-play Ronan modular set)

Source: official [seven-card print-and-play set](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/12/42/1242eccb-60ab-4a30-a099-a76f702cdbb5/ronan_modular_set.pdf).
Collector numbers are 90001–90005 in MarvelCDB; the two treacheries have two
copies each. This is separate from The Galaxy’s Most Wanted Ronan scenario.

- Ronan has 9 hit points per player who started the scenario, Toughness and stun
  immunity. The immunity does not prevent confusion.
- At the beginning of the villain phase, he engages the hero with the fewest
  remaining hit points. Alter-egos are not candidates; with no heroes, he stays
  engaged where he is. The first player resolves a tie.
- Judge, Jury, Executioner starts with 3 threat and has an acceleration icon. Its
  response follows a friendly character’s defeat by an enemy attack, including
  an ally. Damage from unrelated abilities does not trigger it.
- The Accused increases the attack being initiated against its identity. Changing
  the defender does not change the identity against whom the attack began. It
  is discarded after the Kree Fanatic Ronan minion is defeated.
- Bring the Hammer Down activates Ronan against his engaged player in that
  player’s current form. If he is absent it surges. Its boost effect deals the
  first player one encounter card if that activation defeats a character.
- You Dare Oppose Me? discards the top five encounter cards, dealing each Kree
  Fanatic card discarded this way to its player facedown. The other discarded
  cards stay in the encounter discard pile. Its boost gives an attack overkill.
- Ronan’s boost puts him into play engaged with the resolving player. Judge,
  Jury, Executioner’s boost puts the scheme into play. The Accused’s boost
  attaches it to that player’s identity.

Apply the Rules Reference v1.8 uniqueness rules when this modular is used with a
Ronan villain: a unique encounter character with the same title cannot enter play.
