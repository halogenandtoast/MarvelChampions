## Rulesheet rules

<!-- MC30 rulesheet p.1 -->

Patrol prevents a player engaged with a patrol minion from thwarting the main scheme.
Piercing discards the attack target's tough status cards before damage. Requirement
requires spending every listed resource when paying the card's cost. Stalwart prevents
stun and confusion. Villainous gives a minion a facedown boost card for each activation;
its boost icons and boost ability resolve for that activation, then it is discarded.
The current Rules Reference entries govern these existing keywords.

Gesture effects (giving a high five, reading flavor text aloud, speaking in a voice,
sticking out a tongue, or wiggling) are optional and impose no gameplay cost.

## Toon counters

Spider-Ham can spend each toon counter as a wild resource while his hero side is
faceup. Counters remain on the identity when it changes form, but Peter Porker has
no permission to spend them as resources. Spider-Nonsense responds once to each
instance of actual damage, regardless of its amount or source. Fully prevented
damage does not trigger it. Cartoon Power responds to making a basic recovery.

A counter spent as a resource cannot also pay an ability's remove-counter cost.
Wild resources can be declared as another resource type during payment (RR Wild
Resource); this can satisfy Even the Odds' energy Requirement.

## Thwarting and consequential damage

Lady Spider and Overwatch use threat actually removed, bounded by the threat on
the scheme. Their copied removal is an ability effect, not another thwart.
Overwatch interrupts the qualifying removal; Lady Spider responds after her thwart.
SP//dr returns only when consequential damage exceeds her remaining hit points;
exactly lethal consequential damage does not qualify. Lady Spider has two
consequential damage icons for both basic powers; SP//dr has two for THW and one
for ATK.

## Warrior of the Great Web FAQ

<!-- RR v1.8 p.63 -->

The attached character's title must contain the word “Spider” spelled that way.
SP//dr's title does not qualify. The attachment grants Web-Warrior and gains its
attack bonus when a Web-Warrior ally leaves play; the bonus lasts until phase end.

## Starter deck and encounter cards

The forty-card starter deck contains the fifteen signature cards, all nineteen
Justice cards (30012–30019 at their printed quantities), Scarlet Spider, SP//dr,
Team-Building Exercise ×3, and Web of Life and Destiny. Warrior of the Great Web
is an additional deck-building card. The obligation and five nemesis cards are
separate from the player deck.

The nine-card Inheritors modular set contains Hunting the Spider-Totems and Bora,
Brix, Daemos, Jennix, Karn, Morlun, Solus and Verna. Hunting the Spider-Totems
starts at a fixed six threat. Nefarious Trap starts at a fixed three threat and
has a hazard icon.
