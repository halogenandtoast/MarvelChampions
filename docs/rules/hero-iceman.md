# Iceman hero pack

Source: [FFG Iceman rules insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/93/f8/93f8dca2-fed8-4e66-88c7-11d89539d1f0/mc46_iceman_rules_insert-web.pdf).

## Frostbite

Create six Frostbite upgrades set aside at the start of the game. They do not count toward Iceman's deck size. Abilities attach physical copies from that finite supply. Copies return to the supply after their enemy finishes activating or leaves play. Multiple copies stack their SCH and ATK reductions, with the resulting stat never below zero. Stun/confuse replacing an activation does not remove Frostbite, since the enemy has not activated.

## Player side schemes

A player may play a player side scheme during their turn by paying its cost. It enters with its printed starting threat (including the per-player multiplier). It otherwise follows side-scheme rules. One or two starting players allow one player side scheme in play; three or four allow two. If the limit is exceeded, discard a player side scheme selected by the players. Victory keeps Keep Up the Pressure in the victory display after defeat.

## Starter deck

The 15 signature cards (46003–46011) plus printed quantities of 46012–46023 form the 40-card Aggression starter. Frostbite upgrades are separate. Add Hot-Headed to the encounter deck and set aside the Iceman nemesis set. Sauron is a separate modular encounter set.

## Rulings researched under the owner's delegation (2026-11-01)

The owner explicitly delegated decisions to the rules reference, FAQ and recent official rulings rather than requesting approval while absent. The following are paraphrases of official answers archived in [Latest FFG Rulings, post-RRG 1.5](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-5/), checked 2026-11-01; consult RR v1.8 and local FAQ/errata first if later updates conflict.

- **Ice Wall** (Alex, 30 May 2024, answer 199): when the wall breaks, the new Frostbite must remain until the enemy's next activation. Attach after the current activation finishes and its existing Frostbites have returned to the supply.
- **Arctic Attack** (Alex, 6 June 2024, answer 204): the first option's damage and attachment are simultaneous, with Frostbite attached before enemy defeat. Hot-Headed therefore triggers even if this damage defeats the enemy.
- **Surprise Move** (Alex, 13 and 20 June 2024, answers 209/217): Freeze and Surprise Move can resolve in that order during the same basic-attack interrupt occurrence. The enemy need not have an upgrade before Freeze attaches Frostbite.
- **Ice Blast** (Alex, 13 June 2024, answer 213): the final sentence damages every frostbitten enemy, including enemies engaged with other players. Multiple Frostbites do not multiply this damage.
- **Copied Ice events** (Alex, 20 June 2024, answer 216): Rogue may attach a set-aside Frostbite when resolving a copied Chill Out!; the supply is not restricted to Iceman being the resolving player.

Other timing decisions follow the RR: Frozen Solid replaces a genuine activation rather than a stun/confuse replacement; Shark-Girl counts player upgrades only; Suppressing Fire answers only its controller's hero attack; attack-trait event damage bonuses do not turn unlabeled damage into an attack.

The [post-RRG 1.6 official-ruling archive](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/) was also checked through its latest December 2025 entry. Its September 2024 clarification permits The Eye of Sauron to affect different characters for separate wild icons. Its October 2025 Suit Up clarification permits finding Snow Clone alongside an upgrade that attaches to an ally generally; Snow Clone's constant attachment prohibition still applies once in play. No later Frostbite or Ice Wall answer was found there. Cryokinetic Perception's response to Freeze resolves inside the interrupted basic-defense window (Alex, 16 May 2024, answer 186).
