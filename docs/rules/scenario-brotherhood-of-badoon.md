## Scenario #1 - Brotherhood of Badoon
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.8 -->

*The Brotherhood of Badoon has mounted a surprise attack against Earth! You and the other Guardians of the Galaxy rush
to Earth's aid, teaming up with the mighty Avengers in the process to beat back the Badoon. However, the Brotherhood of
Badoon's leader, Drang, is a skilled tactician with a fleet of powerful ships and an overwhelming number of soldiers at
his side. This won't be an easy fight.*

**Villain Deck**: Drang (I), Drang (II)

*Remove Drang (I) and add Drang (III) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: Terrestrial Invasion (1A/1B), Protect the Planet (2A/2B)

**Encounter Deck**: Brotherhood of Badoon, Band of Badoon, Ship Command, and Standard encounter sets. *(The Standard
set can be found in the Marvel Champions core set.)*

*The Band of Badoon modular set can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when using scenario
customization rules.*

### Campaign Instructions
<!-- TGMW rulebook p.8 -->

**Setup:**

- Each player records their identity in the campaign log found on pages 22 and 23 of this rulebook. *(Players cannot
  switch identities during a campaign.)*
- Reveal the Badoon Blitz (178A) side scheme (use the reverse side for expert mode).
- Shuffle the Badoon Headhunter (183) minion into the encounter deck.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: In player order, each player must discard cards from the top of the encounter deck until
  they discard a minion, then put that minion into play engaged with them.

**Victory:**

In the campaign log:

- Record units in each player's respective "Unspent Units" box:
  - Record 1 unit for each player.
  - Record a number of units for each player (to a maximum of 3 units) equal to the victory values on encounter cards
    in the victory display.
  - Record 1 unit for each player if there are no minions in play.
  - Record 1 unit for each player if the main scheme is on stage 1B (Terrestrial Invasion).
- If Badoon Headhunter is in the victory display, mark the box beside "Scenario #1 - Brotherhood of Badoon" in the
  "Headhunter Defeated?" section.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **Setup.** The Milano comes into play by its own setup keyword, for the first player, before 1A's Setup (see
  [scenario-galaxys-most-wanted.md](scenario-galaxys-most-wanted.md)); 1A's Setup puts the Badoon Ship into play, so
  none is shuffled into the encounter deck. In expert, Drang (II) is the first villain, and his When Revealed resolves
  at setup (RR When Revealed Abilities): Drang's Spear is found and attached to him.
- **Charge Up** is a step of the Badoon Ship's own: the counter is placed, then the count looked at. Its indirect
  damage is each player's to assign (RR Indirect Damage). With no Badoon Ship in play, Charge Up does nothing.
- **"After Drang schemes"** answers each scheme of his, the villain phase's or a card's; confused, he does not scheme.
  **"After Drang activates"** (III) answers his schemes and his attacks.
- **Removing 3 threat** with the Milano is an encounter card's ability, not a thwart: patrol does not keep it off the
  main scheme, nor a crisis icon (RR Patrol, Crisis Icon: "abilities on encounter cards are not affected"). Exhausting
  the Milano is the cost, so the action is offered only with the Milano ready, and only to the first player in their
  turn.
- **Protect the Planet's choice**: "deal 3 damage to a minion" is offered only with a minion in play (RR Choose).
- **Drang (II)'s facedown boost card** is his, turned faceup with his next activation's own.
- **Drang (III)'s When Revealed**: "each time a minion is discarded this way": the cards are discarded one at a time,
  and a minion discarded is put into play engaged with the player engaged with the fewest minions (the minions put in
  before it counted) before the next card is discarded. Put into play, it engages (Badoon Assassin attacks, Badoon
  Grunt deals a card, from the top of the deck the discard goes on with). If the discard empties the encounter deck,
  it is fulfilled and does not go on with the new deck (RR Encounter Deck).
- **Drang's Spear** attaches to the villain, Drang being the only one; he has stalwart while it is attached, and a
  stun or confuse is not given to him.
- **Badoon Engineer** "activates against you": its attack or its scheme, it being engaged with you. Stunned or
  confused, it does not activate, and there is no Charge Up.
- **Badoon Assassin** attacks only a hero: engaging a player in alter-ego form, it does not. Its +2 ATK is for that
  attack.
- **"+2 boost icons for this activation"** (Lieutenant, Sentry, Warlord) are added to the activating enemy, villain or
  minion, as the boost resolves; an amplify icon adds its one to the card's count, not to these.
- **Badoon Grunt's boost** puts it into play engaged with the boosted player, and it engages: its Forced Response
  follows.

## Campaign hooks

The campaign agent reads the Victory step from queries already there: the main scheme's stage by `selectOne
MainScheme` and `getMainSchemeCode` ("16061" is stage 1, Terrestrial Invasion); minions in play by `selectAny
AnyMinion`; the victory display by `getVictoryDisplay` and `getVictoryPoints`.

## Open rulings

- **Ties for "the player who is engaged with the fewest minions"** (Drang III): the first player chooses among the
  tied players, as for other ties.
- **The Milano's "remove 3 threat"** is read as removing threat, not thwarting (so patrol and the crisis icon do not
  stop it on the main scheme). The foundation's Cannonade, with the same text, thwarts; the two differ only for
  "threat cannot be removed by thwarting" and responses to thwarting.
- **Drang (II)'s facedown boost card** given outside an activation is kept for his next activation (as Hired Gun's
  "give the villain a facedown boost card").
