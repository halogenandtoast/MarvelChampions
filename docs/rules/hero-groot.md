# Groot (The Galaxy's Most Wanted, MC16)

Groot's hero set, his obligation and nemesis set, the pack's Protection and basic cards.

## New Keyword: Team-Up

The team-up keyword names two characters. To include a card with the team-up keyword in your deck, your chosen identity must match one of the named characters. Additionally, a card with the team-up keyword cannot be played unless both of the named characters (hero or ally) are in play. (RR Team-Up.)

## Starter Deck: Groot / Protection

A flora colossus from Planet X, Groot's unique physiology allows him to sprout vines and branches from himself. This process of germination is represented by growth counters, which make Groot more difficult to take down while simultaneously fueling the versatility of his special upgrades.

- **Groot cards**: Fruition x2, "I Am Groot" x2, "I. AM. GROOT!" x2, Root Stomp x3, "We Are Groot", Fertile Ground, Entangling Vines, Lashing Vines, Vine Shield, Vine Spikes
- **Protection cards**: Starhawk, Desperate Defense x3, Fighting Fit x3, The Power of Protection x2, Dauntless x3, Hard to Ignore x3, Indomitable x2
- **Basic cards**: Rocket Raccoon, Flora and Fauna, Energy, Genius, Strength, Deft Focus x3
- **Obligation**: Wilt
- **Nemesis set**: Blazing Inferno, Furnax, Fan the Flames x3

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can I play an ally card that shares a title with a hero already in play (such as playing the Groot ally while my friend is playing the Groot hero)?** A. No. The players as a group are permitted to have only one copy of each unique card (by title) in play.

**Q. How does Groot's Flora Colossus ability work if he takes more damage than he has growth counters?** A. Groot's Flora Colossus ability will do as much as it can. So, for instance, if Groot has 3 growth counters but takes 5 damage, 3 growth counters will be removed from Groot, preventing 3 of that damage. The remaining 2 damage will then be dealt to Groot's hit points.

**Q. If Groot has a tough status card and takes damage, will growth counters be removed from him?** A. No. As status card abilities have timing priority over all other conflicting card abilities, the tough status card will prevent the damage before Groot's Flora Colossus ability is able to trigger.

## Readings from the rules (no owner ruling asked)

- Growth counters are on Groot's identity card and stay on it whichever face is up. "To a maximum of 10": counters past 10 are not placed.
- Flora Colossus answers each instance of damage. Indirect damage is first assigned, then resolved simultaneously (RR Indirect Damage): the points assigned to Groot are dealt to him as one instance, and Flora Colossus prevents a point of it for each counter removed.
- Lashing Vines' "after Groot uses a basic power" answers his basic attack, basic thwart and defense; recovering is in alter-ego form, where a Hero Response cannot be used.
- "We Are Groot": the player picks how many counters to remove (1 to 4, no more than he has, no more than there are friendly characters), then that many different friendly characters (any player's identity or ally) each get a tough status card.
- Wilt's first option needs a ready alter-ego (RR Choose (Option)); its second removes as many counters as he has, up to 3, and gains surge only if none were removed.
- Fighting Fit and Dauntless: "starting hit points" is the identity's printed hit points (RR Hit Points); remaining hit points are the current maximum less damage.
- Starhawk: his Interrupt answers damage he would take (as other interrupts left it) exactly equal to his remaining hit points; returned to hand, he takes none. A tough status card prevents the damage first, so it does not answer then.
- Deft Focus: the next Superpower card its player plays this turn costs 1 less; the reduction ends with the turn.
- Furnax "activates": after he attacks or schemes.
- Blazing Inferno's base threat is 3 per player (no fixed-threat icon, MarvelCDB).

## Open rulings

- Flora and Fauna's "Groot" is any friendly character titled Groot: the Groot identity (in either form: counters placed, him readied) or the Groot ally (16047), which is only readied; the engine keeps no growth counters on an ally, as no card reads them there.
- Flora and Fauna's "a Rocket Raccoon upgrade" is a hero-specific upgrade (no aspect, not basic) controlled by a player whose identity is Rocket Raccoon; its charge counters are the upgrade's uses, placed without ever discarding it (FAQ: Rocket's upgrades stay in play at 0 charge counters).
