# Jubilee Hero Pack (MC47)

Sources: [FFG announcement and card previews](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2024/3/14/shopping-spree/), [official rules insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/68/7e/687e6308-1160-42c6-bade-3469b6b53d11/mc47_jubilee_rules_insert-web.pdf), local Rules Reference v1.8 and [FFG designer rulings, post 1.6](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/). Checked 2026-11-01.

## Rules insert

Player side schemes enter play with their printed starting threat. Their table limit is one with one or two starting players, two with three or four. Exceeding this limit requires the first player to discard schemes until within the limit. Generation X has Victory 0; Shopping Spree does not have victory and goes to its owner's discard pile when defeated. The insert's generic statement that all included player side schemes have victory is contradicted by Shopping Spree's printed card.

Alliance lets other players contribute payment; the player playing the card remains its resolver. Piercing discards tough before attack damage. Team-up requires both named characters in play and the deck's identity matching one name. Victory cards go to the victory display on defeat. The starter deck is Jubilee / Justice, forty cards.

## Rules decisions (owner authorized source-based decisions, 2026-11-01)

- RR v1.8, Wild Resource: declare each generated wild separately as energy, mental, physical, or wild. Only resources actually spent count; surplus generation is not an additional resource type. Plasmoid Energy's three versions retain different printed pairs.
- Three Steps Ahead resolves each choose/remove-two instance sequentially. A crisis scheme defeated by one instance stops barring later main-scheme choices. Repeating the same target is permitted. The designer's November 23, 2024 rulings 46 and 49 supersede earlier simultaneous rulings (7 and 19); RR v1.8's For Each entry now agrees.
- Grande Finale similarly resolves separate chosen two-damage instances, with fresh legal targets after each one. Jubilee's Coat/Sunglasses instead scale one instance by the number of distinct paid types.
- Blinding Flash chooses distinct enemies. The October 7 and November 19, 2024 designer rulings confirm this.
- Lost Child uses the ally's printed THW as base SCH, then applies its printed minus-one SCH modifier. The October 11, 2024 designer ruling 21 explicitly confirms the modifier applies. Existing Beguiled conversion/restoration handles the remaining transformation rules.
- Shopping Spree prevents removal performed by a hero or ally, including their event thwarts; its own alter-ego action and Disguise's upgrade action use their card as source. The August 8, 2024 ruling 6 distinguishes a scheme's own abilities from identity abilities. Item search puts the card into play and does not play it.
- Grounded's additional form-change cost only applies during the affected player's turn. Its response removes it after a Jubilee event is played.
- Husk pays up to three resources. Each matching icon grants its listed effect once, regardless of repeated resources. Her ready occurs after the basic use. Synch can assist ATK, THW, DEF, or REC, only for that use.
- Nanny's attack response checks the attacked player's allies; an ally defender does not erase the attacked-player association.
- Arcade is immune to damage while any Trap! scheme is in play. Elaborate Trap resolves every such scheme's When Defeated ability using its revealing player without actually defeating the schemes. Hinder adds one threat per starting player in addition to each trap's base threat.

Husk's interrupt does not trigger when she exhausts to defend: RR v1.8 Basic Power (p.10) requires use of a statistic, and Defend, Defense (p.15) distinguishes a hero's basic DEF use from an ally exhausting to defend. Husk has no DEF statistic. Cell Phone's bonus expires when a stunned/confused basic use is replaced by removing its status, including an ally's replaced use.
