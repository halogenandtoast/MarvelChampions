# The Hood

Implementation notes (non-normative). Card text is in `frontend/public/data/cards.json`, codes 24001–24013.

Source: [The Hood rules insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/89/66/89666652-cfcb-47c1-822f-a7a377a69401/mc24_the_hood_rules_insert.pdf), pp. 2–5; [MarvelCDB](https://marvelcdb.com/set/hood).

Choose seven modular encounter sets, from this pack or other products, and set them aside. Recruit one randomly during setup. Recruitment adds one complete remaining set, chosen randomly, to the encounter deck. The insert suggests difficulty bands, rather than one required collection of sets.

Steady allows a second stun and confuse card. One card does not cancel an activation; two do, and both are discarded when they cancel it. See the canonical Steady entry in `glossary-s-z.md` (Rules Reference v1.8 p. 41).

The scenario uses Standard, with Expert and the later villain stages in expert mode. Standard II and Expert II are optional replacements.

Foul Play discards once (up to the current stage's discard count); it does not continue through a reshuffled deck. Only cards outside The Hood encounter set are dealt. Stage II deals the first eligible card; stage III deals every eligible card. A player's obligation qualifies.
