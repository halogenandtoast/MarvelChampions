## Summary (non-normative)

```
Campaign: five scenarios in order (Ebony Maw, Tower Defense, Thanos, Hela, Loki); a loss replays the scenario.
Same identity all campaign; aspects and decks may change between scenarios.
The log: each player's identity (and remaining hit points, expert); nine checkboxes, the "campaign pool":
  1 Cosmo, Security Breach; 2 Shawarma, Black Swan, Avengers Tower Damaged; 3 System Shock, The Infinity
  Stones 1B completed; 4 Norn Stone, Odin.
Each scenario puts its own campaign side scheme into play (double-sided: defeated, it flips into a reward or a
  threat), and the pool brings cards into later scenarios.
Campaign-only cards (180-193) cannot be in a deck outside this campaign.
Expert: remaining hit points recorded on a win; scenarios 2-5 start at them; a player may place an acceleration
  token on the main scheme to heal to full; a defeated player skips the victory steps and rejoins by placing a
  token. Loki lost in an expert campaign loses the campaign.
```

## Campaign mode rules
<!-- MTS rulebook p.4 -->

Each of the scenarios in this expansion can be played individually as a standalone adventure, or together with the
other scenarios as part of one epic campaign. Campaign mode combines all five scenarios in *The Mad Titan's Shadow*
expansion into one epic experience where the outcome of each game impacts the next scenario.

To complete the campaign, the players must win all five scenarios in numerical order, starting with scenario #1 –
Ebony Maw and ending with scenario #5 – Loki.

To begin a campaign, the players start by choosing their heroes. Each player must use their chosen identity for the
entire campaign, but they are free to change aspects and alter the contents of their deck following the deck
customization rules in the *Marvel Champions* Rules Reference between scenarios.

To play a scenario in campaign mode, set up the scenario as per the normal rules of the game. Then, follow that
scenario's setup instructions in the order in which they are listed in the Campaign Instructions.

When the game ends, if the players won, follow that scenario's victory instructions in the order in which they are
listed in the Campaign Instructions. If the players lost, they may reset the scenario and try again with no penalty.

### Expert campaign

For players who want an even more challenging campaign experience, *The Mad Titan's Shadow* expansion comes with
everything you need to play an expert campaign. The rules for this alternate play format are found on page 25.

- Some setup and victory instructions are preceded by **Expert Campaign Only**. Players should ignore these
  instructions unless they are playing an expert campaign.

### Campaign log

The campaign log found on the back cover of this rulebook tracks the progress of the entire campaign. At the end of
each scenario, the players record their results by entering the required information in the campaign log.

A printable copy of the campaign log can be found on our support page at:
www.fantasyflightgames.com/marvel-champions

### Campaign only cards

*(Image: Shawarma (183), "Resource. Max 1 per deck.", encounter set line "Basic / Campaign (4/23)", called out
"This card is Basic but also Campaign Only.")*

Cards 180–193 were created specifically for use in *The Mad Titan's Shadow* campaign. These cards cannot be included
in any deck unless playing *The Mad Titan's Shadow* campaign and the players were directed to add them to a deck by
the Campaign Instructions.

## Scenario #1 - Ebony Maw
<!-- MTS rulebook p.7 -->

### Campaign Instructions

**Setup:**

- Each player records their identity in the campaign log found on the back cover of this rulebook. Players cannot
  switch identities during a campaign.
- Put the "Secure the Landing Pad" side scheme into play.
- Shuffle the "Security Breach" side scheme into the encounter deck.

**Victory:**

- If Secure the Landing Pad was defeated, add Cosmo to the campaign pool.
- If Attack on Knowhere 1B was completed, add Security Breach to the campaign pool.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points in the campaign log.

## Scenario #2 - Tower Defense
<!-- MTS rulebook p.13 -->

### Campaign Instructions

**Setup:**

- Put the "Save the Shawarma Place" side scheme into play.
- If Security Breach is in the campaign pool, shuffle it into the encounter deck.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign
  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may place one acceleration token on one of the main schemes to heal their
  identity to its full hit point value.

**Victory:**

- If Save the Shawarma Place was defeated, add Shawarma to the campaign pool.
- If Black Swan is NOT in the victory display, add her to the campaign pool.
- If Avengers Tower has the ***Damaged*** trait, record that in the campaign log.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points in the campaign log.

## Scenario #3 - Thanos
<!-- MTS rulebook p.17 -->

### Campaign Instructions

**Setup:**

- Put the "Hack Sanctuary's Computer" side scheme into play.
- If Cosmo is in the campaign pool, put him into play under the first player's control.
- If Security Breach is in the campaign pool, shuffle it into the encounter deck.

  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may place one acceleration token on the main scheme to heal their identity to
  its full hit point value.
- If Avengers Tower had the ***Damaged*** trait, deal three damage to each identity.

*(The last bullet is printed after the shaded Expert Campaign Only box, unshaded: it applies in every campaign.)*

**Victory:**

- If Defensive Protocols is NOT in the victory display, add System Shock to the campaign pool.
- If The Infinity Stones 1B was completed, record that in the campaign log.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points in the campaign log.

### Campaign Instructions

## Expert campaign rules
<!-- MTS rulebook p.25 -->

For players who want a more challenging campaign experience, *The Mad Titan's Shadow* expansion comes with everything
you need to play an expert campaign.

### Persistent damage

While playing *The Mad Titan's Shadow* campaign at the expert level, each player must record their remaining hit
points in the campaign log after they win a game. This determines each player's starting hit points for the next
scenario. If a player's remaining hit point value is higher than their base hit point value, record their base hit
points in the campaign log instead.

The setup instructions for each scenario offer each player the opportunity to restore their identity to their full
hit point value by placing an acceleration token on the main scheme.

If a player is defeated during a scenario that their teammates go on to win, the defeated player does not
participate in any of the victory steps for that scenario. However, they can rejoin their teammates for the next
scenario by placing an acceleration token on the main scheme to restore their identity to full hit points.

## How the engine reads it

- The campaign is `Marvel.Campaign` with `kind = MadTitansShadow`; its log's section is `titan` (the nine
  checkboxes); each player's identity and remaining hit points are the common `players`. The Campaign Instructions
  are `Marvel.Campaign.MadTitansShadow`.
- The Setup bullets run as RR Appendix II step 13 has them: after the scenario's own setup, before starting hands.
- "Was defeated" (Secure the Landing Pad, Save the Shawarma Place, Find the Norn Stones): the side scheme's A face is
  no longer in play when the scenario is won. Put into play by the instructions, it leaves play only by being
  defeated (and flipping).
- "1B was completed": the scenario's second main scheme (The Power Stone 21075, Balance the Scales 21115) is in play
  when it is won.
- "Avengers Tower has the Damaged trait": an Avengers Tower environment with the trait is in play when it is won.
- A flipped side scheme's other face enters play as that card would be put into play: a side scheme with its
  starting threat and hinder, an ally under the first player's control, a minion engaged with the first player, an
  attachment attached as it says.
- Expert healing: a player recorded with hit points below full is asked whether to place an acceleration token on a
  main scheme (their choice of main scheme when there are two) to heal to full; one recorded with 0 (defeated as the
  last scenario was won) places one and is healed, unasked. A defeated player's remaining hit points are recorded as
  0; the pool's checkboxes are the group's, not a player's.
- Campaign-only cards: a deck brought to any table with a card of the `mts_campaign` set in it is refused.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-28)

The project owner's ruling, then the readings adopted with the campaign:

- **System Shock drawn from a deck** stays in its player's hand (its text only works there), rather than going into
  the play area as RR *Obligation* has an obligation drawn from a player deck. It cannot be chosen to discard from
  there (to hand size, by a card's "discard a card", or paying a cost that discards cards from hand); a random
  discard (Security Breach's random card) still takes it.
- **First player at campaign setup**: in a campaign game the first player is chosen before the scenario is set up
  (RR Appendix II step 3), so Cosmo, Black Swan and Odin go to the one chosen; outside a campaign the question stays
  where it was.
- **A flipped side scheme's other side** enters play as that card would be put into play: a side scheme with its
  starting threat and hinder (Defensive Protocols, Retrieve Odin's Armor), an ally under the first player's control
  (Cosmo), a minion engaged with the first player (Black Swan), an attachment attached as it says (Jormungand). The
  side scheme itself is removed from the game, so it reaches no victory display.
- **"Was defeated"** (Secure the Landing Pad, Save the Shawarma Place, Find the Norn Stones): the A side is no longer
  in play when the scenario is won.
- **"1B was completed"**: the scenario's second main scheme (The Power Stone 21075, Balance the Scales 21115) is in
  play when it is won.
- **"Discards the top half of their deck"**: rounded down, counted after Shawarma and System Shock are shuffled in.
- **Tower Defense's expert heal** ("one of the main schemes"): the player chooses which; a player recorded at 0
  hit points places the token (choosing the scheme only there) and is healed, unasked.
- **Scenario #3's tower damage** comes after the expert heal (printed order), for every campaign, and is not an
  attack (it is dealt through the "would take damage" path).
- **Open the Dungeons**: each player in player order chooses among the campaign set's Captive allies still set
  aside; a Captive whose title one of their unique cards in play already has is not offered.
- **Jormungand** attaches to the Loki in play, raising his hit points (and their maximum) by 4 per player as it
  attaches; it is removed from the game as that Loki is defeated, his dial untouched, so the next Loki does not
  keep it.
- **Cosmo and Odin King** are owned by whoever controls them; leaving play they are removed from the game.
- **Black Swan "engages the first player"** is constant, as Garm's is: she moves to each new first player as the
  token passes, and her "after Black Swan engages you" discard answers each move.
- **Norn Stone in scenario #5** is put into play by the instructions, not added to decks.
- **Campaign-only cards** (the `mts_campaign` set) are refused in any deck brought to a table: the campaign adds
  them itself as its instructions say.
