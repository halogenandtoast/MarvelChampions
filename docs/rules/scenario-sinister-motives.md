# Sinister Motives: Scenario Implementation Reference

This is a non-normative implementation summary. Consult the [official English rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf) and current [Rules Reference corrections](faq-errata.md) for authoritative rules. The local MarvelCDB card data below is retained as an implementation reference; printed cards and errata take precedence. Owner decisions and printed-card corrections remain at the end.

## Keywords and shared mechanics

[Rulebook p. 3](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=3). Use current Rules Reference definitions when timing differs from the original printing.

- Hinder adds its specified threat to the entering side scheme; Incite adds threat to the main scheme during revelation.
- Patrol blocks the engaged player's thwarts against the main scheme. Permanent prevents a card leaving play.
- Piercing removes the target's tough cards before attack damage. Stalwart prohibits stun and confusion.
- Steady permits two cards of each of those statuses. Only two matching cards replace an activation; remove both when that replacement occurs.
- Requirement demands payment of each named resource type. Setup cards start in play.
- Team-Up restricts deck inclusion to a named identity and requires both named characters in play to use the card. Identity-specific cards, obligations, and nemesis sets remain exclusive to their associated identity.
- Villainous gives an activating minion a boost card, including its boost ability, for that activation; discard it afterward.
- Victory sends a defeated card to the shared, out-of-play victory display instead of a discard pile. Victory values may be signed.
- Each amplify icon adds a boost icon to every boost card resolved for an enemy activation.

## Venom: identity boost cards

[Rulebook p. 11](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=11), [FAQ p. 21](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=21).

Cards placed as identity boosts come facedown from the encounter deck and remain hidden until moved by the main scheme's activation interrupt. Keep them separate from facedown encounter cards. Bell Tower converts attack damage into chimes; this deals no damage and creates no excess-damage result, including when the conversion passes the ringing threshold. Apply the current erratum limiting Venom's vengeance trigger to player and controlled-ally attacks.

## Mysterio: encounter cards in player zones

[Rulebook p. 13](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=13), [FAQ p. 21](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=21).

Encounter cards may enter player decks, hands, and discard piles. Deck additions are facedown and discard additions faceup. Their different backs need not be concealed or re-sleeved; players may anticipate their position. For a simultaneous draw, finish drawing the whole group before moving drawn encounter cards to that player's facedown encounter area, in their chosen order. Remove encounter cards from player zones after the scenario.

## Sinister Six: active villain and activation order

[Rulebook p. 15](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=15), [FAQ p. 21](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=21).

The six villains are Doctor Octopus, Electro, Hobgoblin, Kraven, Scorpion, and Vulture, with activation orders 1–6 respectively. Setup randomly puts one more villain into play than the player count and gives the lowest-order entrant the active counter; others remain set aside.

Only the active villain takes the ordinary villain-phase activation. An unqualified encounter reference to the villain selects the active villain; a qualified reference, such as highest ATK or most remaining hit points, compares eligible villains independently of the counter. Moving to the next villain advances to the next higher order and wraps to the lowest. With one villain, the counter stays there. Defeating its holder moves the counter onward; with none left, set it aside. Track each villain's remaining hit points separately.

A status that replaces an activation does not advance the counter. The main scheme's Ambush handles a requested activation when no villain is present, then continues it. Under current errata, a missing counter with villains present goes to the lowest-order villain before continuing. Overkill against the villain selects the active villain. Light at the End provides the scenario's escape victory condition; defeating the final villain alone does not win. Apply the owner ruling below when flipping Light at the End.

## Venom Goblin: glider and three main schemes

[Rulebook p. 17](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=17), [FAQ p. 21](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc27_sinister_motives_rules_v5-compressed.pdf#page=21).

Start the glider on Midtown Manhattan. Unqualified encounter references to the main scheme, enemy scheme activations, and acceleration icons use the glider's scheme. Acceleration-token placement also goes there, including player-generated placement. Qualified encounter instructions compare all eligible schemes; the first player chooses among ties for least or most threat.

Player cards referring to the main scheme may select any main scheme. Patrol and crisis restrictions apply only to the glider's scheme. During villain-phase acceleration, each scheme receives its own printed amount plus tokens located on that scheme; acceleration icons apply at the glider. Tokens stay where placed when the glider moves normally.

Completing a main scheme flips that physical card to its environment face. Resolve that face's counter/token migration to the least-threat remaining main scheme. Two actual symbiote environments cause a loss. Festering Mass supplies the symbiote-environment condition only while no other such environment exists. Current errata specifies that an effect counting acceleration tokens asks the player to choose a main scheme and counts its tokens.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-30)

- Venom III: “first attack this turn” means the first attack made by a player or an ally against any enemy. An attack against a minion, an attack prevented by tough, or an attack whose damage is converted into Bell Tower counters still occupies this first-attack position. Vengeance/Retribution itself requires an attack against Venom that damages him. A status card that replaces an attack does not make an attack.

### Owner ruling: Light at the End flip (2026-10-30)

When Escape flips to Chase, apply Chase's printed 5 starting threat and Hinder 10 per player, in addition to threat already present after resolving Ambush. Thus a clear board flips to 15 threat in solo standard or 17 in solo expert when Ambush puts a villain into play; two players use 25 or 27 respectively. Other threat placed during the intervening effects remains.

### Printed card correction: Improvised Weapons

Improvised Weapons (27164) has one printed amplify icon, omitted from the MarvelCDB data. Verified against the [printed card image](https://marvelcdb.com/bundles/cards/27164.png) on 2026-10-30.

## Life-Size Decoy and controlled allies

Life-Size Decoy's “The engaged player cannot thwart side schemes” prevents that player from using any cards they control to thwart side schemes, including allies. The Rules Reference's [Patrol definition](glossary-p-r.md#patrol) gives the equivalent wording “The engaged player cannot thwart the main scheme” and explicitly applies it to cards that player controls. This restriction names the player, so the identity-specific interpretation of “you” does not narrow it. Other players may thwart those schemes, and removing threat through an ability without the thwart label remains legal.
