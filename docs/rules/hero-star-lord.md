## New Keyword: Hinder X

When a card with the hinder X keyword is revealed, place X threat on that card.

## New Keyword: Piercing

An attack with the piercing keyword discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack before dealing damage.

## New Keyword: Ranged

An attack with the ranged keyword ignores the retaliate keyword.

## FAQ (RR v1.8 p.62)

**Star-Lord (#1A) — Q: If I play an ally that does not have the printed Guardian trait while in hero form, can I trigger the response on Knowhere (#22)?** A: Yes. Star-Lord's constant ability grants each ally his player controls the Guardian trait. Constant abilities have priority over triggered abilities like Knowhere's "**Response**" ability, so the newly-played ally will have the Guardian trait before Knowhere triggers.

**Mister Knife (#26) — Q: When Mister Knife is revealed by Shadow of the Past, does Shadow of the Past gain surge if it was the first treachery revealed by the Star-Lord player that phase?** A: No. Mister Knife was not in play when Shadow of the Past was revealed, so his ability does not cause Shadow of the Past to gain surge.

**Dive Bomb (#28) — Q: Can I play Dive Bomb if I am engaged with a minion with the guard keyword? If so, how would it resolve?** A: Yes. Abilities are resolved one sentence at a time, so "[d]eal 7 damage to an enemy" would resolve first. If that damage defeated the guard minion, the second effect, "[d]eal 1 damage to each other enemy" would deal 1 damage to the villain. If the first sentence did not defeat the guard minion, the second sentence would deal 1 damage to each enemy other than the villain and the enemy dealt damage by the first sentence.

## Errata (RR v1.8 p.67)

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-27)

- Star-Lord's "What could go wrong?" is offered when you play a card from your hand that costs more than 0, once per round, before its cost is paid; a card it would make affordable counts as playable.
- Blaze of Glory's end-of-phase damage is dealt to each Guardian character in play (any player's) when the phase ends.
- Mister Knife counts every treachery the engaged player reveals in the villain phase: if one was revealed before he was in play, none later that phase gains surge. The treachery that brings him out (Shadow of the Past) is that first treachery, and gains nothing (FAQ).
- Adam Warlock: a discarded card with more than one printed resource resolves each line it matches, in printed order.

## Readings from the rules (not asked)

- The pack's starter deck is Leadership: the hero set, Leadership and basic cards #2–#23. Dive Bomb, Agile Flight, Ever Vigilant and Enhanced Awareness (#28–#31) are not in it.
- "Star-Lord" on Jet Boots and Star-Lord's Helmet is the hero: in alter-ego form Peter Quill is not Aerial and Jet Boots' Hero Interrupt cannot be used.
- "Facedown encounter cards in front of you" are the encounter cards dealt to you and not yet revealed.
- A card ability that changes Star-Lord's form (Bad Boy, Banishment) does not count against his one voluntary change (RR Form).
- Nova Prime and Star-Lord's interrupt answer cards played from hand only, not from a discard pile or held by an upgrade.
- Budding Crime Syndicate enters play with its 2 starting threat plus 2 [per hero] from hinder.
- Hinder X is a constant: the threat is placed as the card enters play, before its When Revealed.
