# Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. scenarios and encounter sets

Source: [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. rulebook, MC50](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc50_rulebook-web.pdf), especially pp. 3, 9, 11, 13, 15, 18–19, and the FAQ. Consult the Rules Reference v1.8 for the underlying attack, replacement, find, flip, status, and victory rules.

## Find and tuck

Find searches the relevant in-play, set-aside, player-deck, encounter-deck and discard areas. MC50 explicitly excludes the victory display, removed-from-game cards and facedown encounter cards already dealt to players. Do not search unnecessary areas when the card is located. An existing Thunderbolt changes engagement without being revealed again. Only Down but Not Out explicitly retrieves a Thunderbolt from victory.

Tucked cards are faceup and out of play; discard tucked cards when their host leaves play unless a specific instruction moves them elsewhere.

## Vulnerable

When a vulnerable character becomes stunned or confused, immediately discard it. This is not a defeat, including when the status and lethal damage are simultaneous. Tough status cards do not cause vulnerable to discard a character. A replaced or prevented status is not gained.

## Black Widow

Her Preparation interrupt belongs to the character's attack, including an attack-labeled ability and an ally attack. A stunned status replaces an attack before her interrupt. Remove threat from the main scheme before discarding the encounter card; without threat to remove, no card is discarded. Crisis does not prevent this ability's removal. Preparation abilities replace boost abilities on the cards in this encounter set; do not resolve a Preparation merely because the card is revealed as a boost.

The card's printed Preparation is resolved, or the preparations granted by Night Vision Goggles and Automated Defenses apply if it has no printed preparation. Effects that occur after the attack wait for that attack's completion. Black Widow's Gauntlet is an optional hero response, after retaliate, when no Preparation resolved.

## Batroc

Batroc has fixed hit points, not hit points per player. Reducing his dial to zero replaces his defeat with resetting his dial and removing six threat. This does not defeat him for other effects.

Advance by clearing each main scheme. Clearing stage 2 puts a set-aside captive into play exhausted under a chosen player's control. Players then choose between advancing and refilling stage 2 with three threat per player. Stage 3 directs attacks to rescued captives. Clearing stage 3 wins; filling any scheme or losing the last captive at stage 3 loses.

Alert Level is a double-sided environment, with threat tracked on the physical card. At four threat per player, its Low side clears its threat and flips. Its High side increases Batroc's SCH and ATK; reaching that threshold again loses. A character defeated by consequential damage does not raise alert. In expert mode, setup and entering stage 3 add two threat per player to Alert Level.

## M.O.D.O.K.

Shuffle the four Holding Cells into a separate stack. Only its top card is in play; entering play puts two lock counters per player on it. Removing its last lock counter flips that physical card to its rescued ally and exposes the next cell. When a rescued Inhuman leaves play, it returns to the bottom of that stack on its Holding Cell side. If the stack was empty, that card immediately becomes the exposed cell and receives its entering-play counters.

M.O.D.O.K.'s hit points are fixed. His replaced defeat removes two locks and resets his printed hit points while a cell is present. With no cell in play, reducing him to zero wins. A replacement is not a defeat. His interrupt precedes an Adaptoid's When Defeated when overkill damages both simultaneously.

The main scheme's completion recruits a random remaining Adaptoid upgrade environment. If this leaves no set-aside upgrade, players lose. Otherwise it clears the scheme and shuffles discarded Adaptoids into the encounter deck.

Hostage Situation's attached rescued ally remains in play, under no player's control. It is excluded from ally limits and controlled-character effects. The player who defeats the scheme gains control of it.

## Thunderbolts

Use one qualifying Elite/Thunderbolt modular per player, plus one additional qualifying modular. Each player reveals one of the set-aside elite minions; the additional minion is in play attached to Thunderbolt Backup. It can be targeted and damaged, retains tokens/status/attachments, and does not activate while unengaged.

Attacking a Thunderbolt causes it to engage the attacking player. At round end, attach the Thunderbolt with the most damage to the backup environment. The first player breaks ties, including a tie at zero damage. If it replaces another minion, the previous backup engages the player the newly attached minion left. Heal one damage per player from the backup, or two per player and give tough in expert mode.

Citizen V cannot be defeated until the victory display contains enough Thunderbolt minions. During step two of the villain phase, an engaged Thunderbolt replaces his activation with healing. Stun/confuse statuses take priority over this replacement.

## Baron Zemo and the Executive Board

Standalone setup prepares evidence and puts two secret counters on each Board member. Campaign setup instead restores the campaign's counters. A Board member flips into a permanent attachment at four secrets, or three in expert. Flipping preserves its counters; those retained counters are not newly placed. Three Board attachments lose the game.

The main scheme's player-phase-end response may add two secrets to a clear Board environment to gain evidence (two cards standalone, one in campaign), then may advance to an accusation. Compare means, motive, opportunity, and accused member against the hidden evidence. Apply the printed penalties for each wrong guess, with the extra three secrets for accusing the wrong member, before advancing to the final stage.

The final stage flips Zemo to his Unmasked side and resets his hit points, attaches his sword, flips the mole, and places player-scaled threat for all secrets on Board attachments. Before becoming Unmasked, a reduced-to-zero Zemo instead resets his fixed hit points and removes three secrets; this is not a defeat.

Reluctant Foe uses a hero from the collection whose title matches no character in play. Its SCH is printed THW, its ATK and hit points are printed hero values, and its text is replaced by villainous and the printed When Defeated removal instruction. DEF is irrelevant. Defeating it removes both physical cards from the game.

## Research decisions (2026-11-02)

The owner authorized decisions from the rules, FAQ and recent FFG rulings without approval requests.

* Monica Rappaccini (50126): MarvelCDB incorrectly includes printed villainous. The [printed card image](https://marvelcdb.com/bundles/cards/50126.jpg) has Victory −1 and Vulnerable only; villainous is gained only while Scientist Supreme is in the victory display.
* [FFG ruling, 2026-01-17](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-7/): if A.I.M. Grunt redirects Arm Block, its damage attacks the Grunt while its prevention still applies to Black Widow's original enemy attack. Retarget the player's attack without changing the event's original triggering enemy.
* The same rulings collection clarifies that an Alert Level flip is not a reveal from the encounter deck, so the Black Widow ally cannot cancel that flip. The September 14, 2026 clarification distinguishes explicit reveals from other zones from cards first dealt to a player.
* Use printed card stats, keywords, and icons over an inconsistent database record. Negative Victory values stay negative.

* FFG September 12, 2025 (Alex), collected at https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings-post-rrg-1-6/: Might Makes Right does not remove secrets when stunned cancels Zemo’s attack: there was no attack. A completed attack must actually exist, and no character may have taken damage from it.

* FFG July 11, 2025 (Alex), same ruling collection: S.H.I.E.L.D. Agent’s secret placement interrupts its defeat, including simultaneous damage. Its defeat ability uses the When-defeated window, before removal.
