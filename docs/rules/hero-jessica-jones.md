# Jessica Jones

Source: [FFG Jessica Jones rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc61_rulesheet-web.pdf), checked 2026-11-02. Card text: MarvelCDB pack `jj`. Rules Reference 1.8 and `faq-errata.md` govern ordinary timing.

## New rules

**Prerequisite (form or trait):** the identity playing the card must have the specified form or trait.

**Starting:** each deck can contain at most one Starting card. Before drawing an opening hand, its owner may find it in their deck and add it as the first card of that hand. It counts against hand size and can be discarded during the mulligan. This is optional and is earlier than setup abilities.

**Exactly defeat:** an enemy is exactly defeated when an attack defeats it without dealing excess damage. This includes an attack dealing precisely its remaining hit points, after damage modifiers and prevention; merely discarding a minion is not attacking and defeating it.

Featured existing rules: Alliance; alternate player card backs; Forced Action; Hinder; Permanent; Piercing; player side schemes; Team-Up; Victory.

## Implementation rulings (sources checked 2026-11-02)

The owner authorized resolving rules from published sources without approval requests. These are applications of printed text and RR1.8, not owner-created rulings.

- Gather Evidence responds to basic ATK, THW, or DEF. After using DEF triggers after the defended attack resolves (RR1.8 Defend); taking damage does not invalidate the trigger. Triggered attack/thwart/defense abilities are not basic powers.
- Incognito Mode changes form through the ordinary form-change system, then prevents returning to alter ego for the current phase. It does not consume the ordinary once-per-turn change-form opportunity.
- Alias Investigations first places its evidence, then evaluates whether sufficient evidence can pay to defeat the villain's current stage. Defeating a stage is not damage and respects effects that prevent defeat.
- 4K Digital Camcorder discards its chosen non-Elite minion; it does not defeat it. A negative calculated evidence cost is treated as zero under RR numerical rules.
- Unbreakable Bond heals at most three damage distributed among the named characters currently in play. Team-Up can be satisfied by an identity or ally; the identity must be in hero form to count.
- Purple Man's stars define his base ATK and SCH before additive modifiers (RR1.8, Base Value / Modifiers / Non-Numerical Variable). His own derived star is excluded from the comparison to avoid circular self-definition; all other characters' current values count. A defeat snapshot preserves his defined base SCH for Shakedown rather than treating the printed star as zero.
- Suggestion's forced action can be resolved during the player phase and must be resolved before that phase ends; playing a matching printed resource card pays all normal costs. Wild resources are not printed energy/mental/physical icons.

Recent-ruling search: [FFG rules update](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/mission-updates/) confirms the current RR1.8 timing update. No later official card-specific erratum for this pack was found in the search on 2026-11-02. The printed rulesheet remains authoritative.
