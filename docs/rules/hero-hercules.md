## Rulesheet: Labor and Gift decks

Before play, separately shuffle the three Labor cards and the three Gift cards into facedown decks. They are separate from Hercules's forty-card starter deck and the encounter deck. Their alternate backs identify cards that cannot enter ordinary player decks, hands, or discard piles.

In alter ego, New Labors of Hercules reveals the top Labor if no Labor is in play. In hero form, completing a Labor can trigger Atonement: put the top Gift into play, ready Hercules, and optionally change to alter ego. Each Gift's response draws four cards when it enters play.

Find searches eligible in-game areas, including set-aside and in-play cards, excluding facedown encounter cards, the victory display, and cards removed from the game.

## Rules and rulings applied, 2026-11-02

The user authorized consulting official rules and recent rulings without asking for decisions.

- **Labor FAQ:** RR v1.8 Appendix IV directs an unsuccessful Labor to be discarded. Every Labor leaving play other than entering the victory display returns to the bottom of the Labor deck.
- **Protect Humanity:** if Hercules controls no ally, its forced interrupt has no effect and the original attack proceeds. When an ally becomes the target, Hercules may defend; the Labor loses a counter only if he defended and the attack completed.
- Hercules's event attacks and thwarts count as his attacks and thwarts. Ally attacks and thwarts remain those allies' uses of their powers.
- **Defeat the Hydra:** use at least six printed hit points, excluding Elite minions. Added hit points do not satisfy that printed threshold. Damage restrictions still permit Hercules's labeled event attacks.
- **Embody Pathos:** the selected encounter side scheme must not already be in play. Treat its per-player symbols as one during its reveal, including initial threat and Hinder. Add the Labor's six threat afterward. Hercules's event thwarts remain eligible.
- **Appeal to Athena:** Gift cards retain their traits and permanent bonuses, but count as zero when an ability counts Gifts controlled.
- **Prince of Power:** excess damage dealt and excess damage taken are distinct. Damage prevention does not reduce the former value used for its healing. Its response follows defeat, and its exhaustion cost limits available uses.
- **Temporary:** Lion of Olympus is discarded at the end of the round.
- **Find an existing minion:** RR v1.8, Find (p. 19), requires preserving its attached cards and tokens. It resolves reveal effects and engages the revealing player if necessary, but does not enter play again. Defeat the Hydra subsequently heals that preserved host's damage explicitly.
