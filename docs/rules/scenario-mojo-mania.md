# Mojo Mania: standalone scenarios

Implementation notes (non-normative). Card text is in `frontend/public/data/cards.json`, codes 39001a–39071.

Source: [official MojoMania rules insert](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc39_mojomania_rules_insert-compressed.pdf), pp. 2–3, 7–8, 11–12, 16, 18; [MarvelCDB](https://marvelcdb.com/set/mojo). Campaign instructions are separate from standalone setup.

## Genre sets and Longshot

Crime, Fantasy, Horror, Sci-Fi, Sitcom, and Western are modular encounter sets. Each contains one Show environment representing a Setting. Revealing a Setting discards the previous Setting. Its conditional surge applies only when revealed from the encounter deck, not from Spiral's show deck or Mojo's set-aside cards (FAQ p. 18).

Longshot is an optional one-card modular set with an encounter back. He does not count toward a scenario's required number of modular sets. Revealing him puts him into play under the revealing player's control and surges; this effect cannot be canceled. As a boost card he has no boost icons. Longshot is not automatically included in standalone setup.

## MaGog

Use MaGog's A side in standard mode and B side in expert mode. There are no successive villain stages. Include one modular set; the printed recommendation is a random genre from this product. Put both crowd environments into play with their Booing Crowd sides faceup.

A lethal hit does not defeat MaGog: reset his hit point dial to 10 per starting player, award The Challengers 3 ratings per player (2 on B), and deal each player an encounter card. Attachments and status cards remain. His reset does not trigger abilities that require actually defeating an enemy.

Completing Melee in the Mojo-seum also replaces completion: remove all its threat and award The Champion 2 ratings per player. No excess threat is retained.

At 5 ratings per starting player a crowd flips while retaining its counters. The Champion's flip draws each player a card; The Challengers' flip puts Surprise Contender into play engaged with the first player, or gives it tough if it is already in play. At 10 ratings per player the Champion wins (players lose), or the Challengers win (players win). Defeating MaGog and completing the main scheme do not independently end this scenario. Eliminating all players still loses.

Jolt of Adrenaline and Surge of Aggression remain attached until MaGog's hit point reset; each awards an additional rating per player before being discarded. Surprise Contender has 7 hit points per starting player, is villainous, and awards 2 ratings per player when defeated.

## Spiral

Include three genre sets. Begin with Spiral's Escaped face, The Search for Spiral in play, and one randomly selected Show in play. Remove the other two Shows from the encounter deck and shuffle them with Cornered! to form the three-card show deck. The search scheme and Cornered! are not shuffled into the encounter deck.

The show deck has no discard pile and player card effects cannot manipulate it. When a Show would be discarded, place it on the bottom of this deck instead. Revealing the next Show replaces the current Setting. Cornered! flips Spiral to her Cornered face, reveals the next show-deck card, and is then shuffled back into the show deck; its effect cannot be canceled.

While Escaped, Spiral cannot take damage or be stunned, the main scheme cannot have threat removed, and attempts to make her attack become schemes. While Cornered, each completed activation places a teleport counter on her. On reaching the stage's per-player threshold, remove all teleport counters and flip her back to Escaped. These thresholds are 3, 2, and 3 per starting player at stages I, II, and III respectively.

Flipping preserves Spiral's damage, attachments, status cards, boost cards, and other associated elements. Resolve any When Revealed ability on the new face. When a stage is defeated, the next stage enters on the same face (Cornered or Escaped) as the defeated one, with its new hit points and no teleport counters. In standard mode the stages are I–II; in expert mode II–III.

The Search for Spiral is permanent. Removing its final threat reveals the top show-deck card for the player responsible and replaces its threat with 3 per starting player. Its damage-paid action is not a labeled thwart. Spiral's Swords gives ATK equal to its remaining sword counters and its paid removal action is available only while Spiral is Cornered.

## Mojo

Choose genre sets equal to the starting player count plus one and set them aside. MojoMania reveals one random chosen set's Show and shuffles its other cards into the encounter deck. Wheel of Genres starts Spinning. When the encounter deck resets, lose if no chosen sets remain; otherwise flip the Wheel to Stopped. At the encounter-card dealing step, a stopped Wheel reveals a randomly selected remaining Show, places that set's shuffled remaining cards on top of the encounter deck, deals the first player two additional encounter cards, then flips back.

Threat may be placed on characters in this scenario. When a character flips or leaves play, move its threat to the main scheme. Mojo's end-of-turn effect applies only when that player's turn ends in hero form. Cards discarded from the encounter deck that are outside the Mojo encounter set each add one threat to that hero.

## FAQ timing

- Discarding Stinger Tail with an attack removes its retaliate before retaliate would resolve.
- Stinger Tail and Undercover Mojo have the same trigger; the first player orders their forced interrupts.
- Wild Wild Mojo increases consequential damage when at least one damage is taken. Overkill first increases damage to the initial character, then increases the excess damage reaching the second character separately.
