# Nova Hero Pack (MC28)

Sources: [official MC28 rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc28_nova_rulesheet.pdf),
p.1 (p.2 is blank); [printed starter-deck divider](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/deck1.jpeg);
local MarvelCDB data, codes 28001–28032; Rules Reference v1.8.
The following is an implementation summary. Card text is in `frontend/public/data/cards.json`.

## Stacking tough status cards

<!-- Nova rulesheet p.1; RR v1.8 "Tough", p.44 -->

When damage would be dealt to a character with several tough status cards, one of
those cards prevents that instance of damage. The other tough cards remain.
Armadillo explicitly permits an unlimited number of tough status cards.
Piercing removes all tough status cards before dealing attack damage; it does not
remove them if the attack would deal no damage (RR v1.8 "Piercing", p.32).

## Requirement (resources)

<!-- Nova rulesheet p.1; RR v1.8 "Requirement (Resources)", p.37 -->

Playing a Requirement card requires spending every resource listed by its keyword
while paying its cost. Cost reductions do not waive this requirement. Such a card
cannot be played ignoring its resource cost, because that would prevent paying the
required resources. No Quarter requires one physical resource; Honed Technique
requires two mental resources.

## Wild resources

<!-- RR v1.8 "Wild Resource", p.48 -->

A generated wild resource may be declared energy, mental, physical or wild. Each
wild resource generated by a doubling effect may be declared separately. A printed
wild icon being counted outside payment remains wild and cannot be treated as
another resource type.

Lightspeed Flight and Pot Shot double wild resources generated to pay for them,
including a wild resource generated by Supernova Helmet. A doubled wild resource
may satisfy a Requirement by being declared as its required type. Forcefield
Projection and Sam Alexander's ability require a resource spent as wild for their
bonus effects. Moon Girl and Honed Technique check resources spent as mental.

## Ms. Marvel errata

<!-- RR v1.8 Appendix V, "Nova Hero Pack", p.68 -->

Ms. Marvel's Hero Response returns the played event from the discard pile. The
current errata explicitly adds that zone, so it does not retrieve an event from
some other zone. Her exhaust and one damage are costs.

## Printed statistics

The printed Nova identity has THW 1, ATK 1, DEF 2, 10 hit points and hand size 5.
Sam Alexander has REC 3, 10 hit points and hand size 6. Armored Assault starts with
3 threat per player and has no encounter icon. These agree with the local card data.

## Timing and attribution

One by One is one labeled attack with two conditional damage instances (RR
"Attack (Player Ability Type)"). Its second instance requires an actual defeat;
replacing the first enemy's defeat with healing does not satisfy that condition.
No Quarter instead checks excess damage already dealt, which is not undone when
Biomechanical Upgrades replaces the minion's defeat with healing (RR "Excess
Damage" and "Replacement Effect").

Unleash Nova Force names the Nova side of the identity. Its lasting effect remains
until the round ends, but does not trigger while Sam Alexander is faceup (RR
"Identity"). Events and qualifying upgrades extend the identity; allies and
supports do not (RR "You, Your").

## Starter deck

The printed divider lists fifteen signature cards: Ms. Marvel, Forcefield Projection
×2, Lightspeed Flight ×3, Pot Shot ×3, Unleash Nova Force ×2, Connection to the
Worldmind ×2, Jesse Alexander and Supernova Helmet.

Its twenty-five other player cards are The Locust, Chase Them Down ×2, Pitchback ×3,
No Quarter ×3, One by One ×3, The Power of Aggression ×2, Fluid Motion ×3, Honed
Technique ×3, Moon Girl, Everyday Hero ×3 and Champions Mobile Bunker.

Weight of the World is the obligation. The five-card nemesis set consists of
“Bring the War!”, Warbringer, War Delivery ×2 and “The War's Been Brought”.
Neither is part of the forty-card player deck. Yaw and Roll and Height Advantage
are additional deck-building cards.

## Armadillo modular encounter set

The six-card modular set contains Armored Assault, Armadillo, “Rollin', Rollin'”,
Tough and Tumble and Tough It Out ×2. It is separate from Nova's nemesis set and
may be included when constructing a scenario's encounter deck.
