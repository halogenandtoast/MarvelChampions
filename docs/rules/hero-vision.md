## Additional forms

The rulesheet introduces additional forms granted by form upgrades. Vision's mass form is independent of hero/alter-ego form. Changing mass form triggers effects that respond to changing form, but does not use the voluntary hero/alter-ego flip. See RR v1.8, [Form](glossary-d-h.md).

Vision's permanent mass-form upgrade begins Intangible through his alter-ego Setup ability. Its reverse is Dense; the local MarvelCDB record stores that side under `backName` and `backText`. Dense grants +2 ATK and +2 DEF only in hero form, and offers a response to draw one card after changing to that mass form. Blanking this upgrade does not change which mass form Vision occupies.

## Alliance, piercing and stalwart

The insert also introduces Alliance, piercing, and stalwart. Alliance allows other players to contribute costs when someone plays the card, while the player who played it resolves it. Piercing discards the attack target's tough statuses before damage. Stalwart prevents a character becoming stunned or confused. See the canonical [Alliance](glossary-a-c.md), [Piercing](glossary-p-r.md) and [Stalwart](glossary-s-z.md) entries.

## Errata

[RR v1.8, Machine Man](faq-errata.md): the stat increase applies only to the current attack or thwart, rather than later uses. This correction is already present in the local MarvelCDB text.

## Starter deck

The printed signature, Protection and basic cards at positions 2–27 total 41 physical cards: 40 ordinary deck cards plus the permanent mass-form upgrade. Permanent is set aside during the normal setup sequence and the identity's Setup puts it into play, Intangible faceup. Vision's opening hand size is therefore the printed 5 before that Setup, with the increased alter-ego hand size applying after it.

## Resource costs

A cost saying “up to” requires at least one game element (RR “Cost”). Machine Man's optional interrupt spends one to three resources; declining it leaves his basic power unchanged. Resource-sensitive effects count the resources actually allocated to the final cost, excluding excess generated resources.
