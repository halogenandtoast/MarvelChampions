# X-23 Hero Pack

Sources: [official X-23 insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/32/e8/32e82755-7b86-4ba0-9201-aa32b52faaa8/mc43_x-23_rules_insert-web.pdf), two PDF pages (second page artwork); local MarvelCDB pack `x23`, cards 43001–43040; Rules Reference v1.8.

## Rules introduced by the insert

Player side schemes are played from hand, paying their cost and placing their starting threat. They follow side-scheme rules. The shared limit is one with one or two starting players and two with three or four. If exceeded, the first player selects schemes to discard until within that limit. Victory schemes enter the victory display when defeated. See [Player Side Scheme](glossary-p-r.md) and [Victory Display](glossary-s-z.md).

Assault replaces THW with ATK during a basic thwart against that scheme. An ally uses its ATK consequential damage too. The action remains a thwart, so attack responses do not trigger. RR v1.8 also explains that a modifier to the basic power can increase the ATK used for this thwart. See [Assault](glossary-a-c.md).

Linked cards are excluded from decks and deck size. When a deck contains their named parent, set aside the product's complement of linked cards during setup. Each deck containing the parent contributes its own complement. A player gaining a linked card becomes its owner. Specialized Training has four linked upgrades: Combat Specialist, Defense Specialist, Front Line Specialist and Surveillance Specialist. See [Linked](glossary-i-o.md).

Piercing discards the target's tough status before attack damage. Victory X moves a defeated card to the victory display. See [Piercing](glossary-p-r.md) and [Victory](glossary-s-z.md).

## Current FAQ and errata

- Honey Badger (43003) cannot trigger her response if the damage defeats her: she has left play before its response window. [RR FAQ](faq-errata.md).
- Front Line Specialist (43036) grants +4 hit points to the identity, including alter-ego form. [RR errata](faq-errata.md).
- Damage in costs uses the ordinary damage interruption and prevention rules; the cost must actually be paid. Living Weapon can respond to damage paid as a cost, limited once per phase. See [Costs](glossary-a-c.md), [Damage](glossary-d-h.md) and [timing](timing.md).

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-11-01)

Use the published rules and errata. In particular, apply the Honey Badger FAQ and Front Line Specialist correction above. No project-specific departures requested.

## Starter deck

X-23/Laura Kinney, the pack's signature cards 43002–43012 and Aggression/basic cards 43013–43027 at their supplied quantities. X-23's Claws is permanent and does not count toward deck size. Set aside all four Specialization upgrades when Specialized Training is included.

## Timing and interaction notes

- Specialized Training resolves each player separately, in the order chosen by the first player. Unique Specialization upgrades already in play cannot be chosen again. See [Each Player](timing.md).
- Sisterly Bond adds the matching power actually used: ATK for Assault or a chosen ATK thwart, and THW for an attack changed to use THW. Its bonus lasts only for that use, including an aborted attack. See [Basic Power](glossary-a-c.md).
- A Victory attachment enters the victory display when its host is defeated. Cybermods can replace a host's discard while the attachment is still present; ordinary discarding of a host does not defeat its attachments. See [Victory X](glossary-s-z.md).
- Living Weapon requires X-23 to be able to ready. A blocked ready cannot consume the once-per-phase limit. Defense Specialist responds to an actual basic defense, including one whose attacker is defeated by retaliate, and not to a defense event alone. See [Initiating Abilities](timing.md) and [Defend](glossary-d-h.md).

Puncture Wound refers to Honey Badger by name, so her attacks still count when a card such as Beguiled treats her as a minion. For example, Moondragon can make that minion attack another enemy during the player turn. Changing her card type does not change her name. See [Referential Abilities](timing.md).
