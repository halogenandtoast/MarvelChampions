## Summary (non-normative)

```
Villain deck: five different Loki (I) cards, one at random in play, four set aside. No stage II/III, no expert villain.
Main scheme: All Hail King Loki (1A/1B), 12[per player] threat, 1[per player] base, 1[per player] acceleration.
Encounter deck: Loki, Infinity Gauntlet, Enchantress, Frost Giants, Standard (+ Expert).
1A Setup: Lokis set aside; War in Asgard into play; shuffle; 1 random Loki revealed; top Infinity Stone revealed.
Loki defeated: he goes to the victory display (Victory 1) and a random set-aside Loki enters play with everything that
  was on him. Lokis in the victory display = the victory condition (1 rookie, 2 standard, 3 expert, 4 heroic, chosen
  before the game): the players win. 1B completed: the players lose.
"Swap Loki with a random set-aside Loki": a set-aside version replaces him; he does not leave or enter play and is
  not revealed; attachments, status cards (and, by RR "Swap", his hit points) stay; the old one is set aside again.
Each side scheme of the Loki set: Hinder 1[per hero]; When Defeated: reveal the top Infinity Stone, swap Loki.
```

## Scenario
<!-- MTS rulebook p.24 -->

**SCENARIO #5 - LOKI**

*The heroes have defeated Hela and rescued Odin from the dungeons of Hel, but Loki still sits on the throne of Asgard
with the Infinity Gauntlet. Heimdall can take you to the Golden Realm, and the All-Father can offer you some
protection from Loki's magic, but it is up to you to defeat the trickster god and secure the Infinity Stones.*

**Villain Deck**: Loki (five different versions)

**Main Scheme Deck**: All Hail King Loki

**Encounter Deck**: Loki, The Infinity Gauntlet, Enchantress, Frost Giants, and Standard sets. (The Standard set can be
found in the *Marvel Champions* core set.)

*The Enchantress and Frost Giants sets can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when using
the scenario customization rules. The Infinity Gauntlet set may be used in other scenarios, but it is required when
playing Loki.*

### The God of Mischief
<!-- MTS rulebook p.24 -->

Loki is the Asgardian god of mischief. To represent the trickster god's sorcerous powers, Loki does not have ordinary
villain stages. Instead, Loki has five different stage-I villain cards. When setting up Loki's scenario, choose one
Loki villain card at random, reveal it and put it into play. Set the remaining four versions of Loki aside, out of
play. When the players defeat Loki, instead of revealing the next villain stage, the players reveal one of the
set-aside versions of Loki at random.

### Swapping Loki
<!-- MTS rulebook p.24 -->

When a card effect instructs the players to "swap Loki with a random set-aside Loki," they should replace the Loki
card that is in play with one of the remaining set-aside versions of Loki. This does not cause Loki to leave play,
enter play, or be revealed. Attachments and status cards on Loki should be transferred to the new version of Loki that
was swapped in. The Loki card that was swapped out should be set-aside with the other remaining set-aside versions of
Loki.

### Winning the Game & Modular Difficulty
<!-- MTS rulebook p.24 -->

To win the game, the players must defeat a certain number of Loki villains. Each version has Victory 1 to indicate
that it is added to the victory display when it is defeated. When Loki is defeated, that triggers the **Forced
Interrupt** on stage 1B: "When Loki is defeated, advance to a random set-aside Loki villain." When a new version of
Loki enters play, transfer all attachments, status cards, counters, and tokens that were on the previous version of
Loki to the one that enters play.

The victory condition for the scenario is written in bold on stage 1B: "**If the number of Lokis in the victory
display is equal to the victory condition, the players win the game.**" The number of different versions of Loki that
must be defeated is determined by the players before the game begins. Below is the suggested number of Loki villains
that must be in the victory display for each mode of play:

- **Rookie Mode** – One version of Loki
- **Standard Mode** – Two versions of Loki
- **Expert Mode** – Three versions of Loki
- **Heroic Mode** – Four versions of Loki

### Rules Clarification
<!-- MTS rulebook p.24 -->

**Q**. When the Loki villain card is swapped out for one that has the stalwart keyword, what happens to any stunned or
confused status cards that were on Loki?
**A**. When Loki gains the stalwart keyword, discard any stunned or confused status cards from him.

## Victory X
<!-- MTS rulebook p.13 -->

When a card with the victory X keyword is defeated, place it in the victory display instead of its owner's discard
pile. The victory display is a named, out-of-play game area shared by all players. Cards in the victory display follow
the standard rules for out-of-play cards.

## Campaign Instructions
<!-- MTS rulebook p.25 -->

**SETUP:**

- Put the "Open the Dungeons" side scheme into play.
- Shuffle the "Summoned Back" treachery into the encounter deck.
- If Shawarma is in the campaign pool, each player shuffles one copy of Shawarma into their deck.
- If System Shock is in the campaign pool, each player shuffles one copy of System Shock into their deck.
- If The Infinity Stones 1B was completed, each player discards the top half of their deck.
- If Norn Stone is in the campaign pool, each player puts one copy of the Norn Stone upgrade into play on its
  **Setup** side.
- If Odin is in the campaign pool, put Odin into play on his ***King*** side.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign
  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may place one acceleration token on the main scheme to heal their identity to
  its full hit point value.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: If the players lose this game, Loki exerts his rule over all the universe and the players
  lose the campaign.

**VICTORY:**

- Loki is defeated and the players win the campaign! Turn the page to read the conclusion.

## Rules Reference entries these rely on (RR v1.8, quoted)

**"Swap"** (RR p.42), the bullet for a card in play swapped with an out-of-play card: "Share a title, neither card is
considered to enter or leave play. Tokens, attached cards, tucked cards, and status cards on the previously in-play
card are transferred to the other card and the other card maintains the state *(ready or exhausted)* of the previously
in-play card. If the swapped card has an associated hit point dial, that dial remains at the same value."

**Stalwart** (RR p.40): "If a character gains the stalwart keyword while they have a stunned and/or confused status
card, each stunned and/or confused status card is removed from that character."

**When Defeated Abilities** (RR p.48): "The '**When Defeated**' timing trigger is equivalent to the following trigger:
'**Forced Interrupt**: When this card is defeated...'" **Victory X** (RR p.46): for a character, "**When Defeated**: Add
this card to the victory display."

**Unique Icon** (RR p.45): "A non-villain card in an out-of-play state that matches a card in play cannot enter play.
[...] A non-villain encounter card, it is discarded and any effects of it entering play are ignored." (Thor's nemesis
Loki minion while a Loki villain is in play.)

FAQ/errata: the RR's "Rulebook Pg. 19, Swapping Avatars of Loki, Paragraph 1" errata ([faq-errata.md](faq-errata.md),
under *Trickster Takeover Scenario Pack*) is for the Trickster Takeover pack's Avatar of Loki villains and Fading
Figment, not this scenario. There is no MTS FAQ entry for the Loki set or the Enchantress set.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **One Loki in play, four set aside.** The scenario picks the random Loki as it starts, before the encounter cards
  with the setup keyword go into play, so the Infinity Gauntlet ("Attach to the villain", Setup) has a villain to
  attach to; no Loki has a When Revealed ability, so 1A's "reveal 1 set-aside Loki" coming earlier changes nothing.
- **The victory condition** is chosen by the first player as the scenario starts (1–4: rookie, standard, expert,
  heroic), whatever the game's difficulty.
- **Loki defeated**: the defeated version goes to the victory display; if the Lokis there now equal the victory
  condition, the players win at once. Otherwise a random set-aside Loki takes his place, with his attachments, upgrades,
  status cards, boost cards and an activation under way (as a next stage does), at its own full hit points; then the
  defeated version's When Defeated resolves (discard until a side scheme, the active player revealing it). Jormungand
  is removed from the game first, without its hit points coming off the already-defeated Loki.
- **Swap**: a random set-aside Loki takes his place with everything on him and **his hit point dial as it was** (RR
  "Swap": the cards share a title); the swapped-out version goes back to the set-aside versions. It is not leaving or
  entering play and opens no reveal window; it opens the "after Loki is swapped" window Loki's Cape answers. A Loki
  with stalwart sheds his stunned and confused status cards (the engine's stalwart sweep after each message).
- "**Attach to Loki**" is the villain, as "attach to the villain" (`attachToTheVillain`), never Thor's nemesis Loki
  minion, which cannot be in play with a Loki villain anyway (RR Unique Icon).
- **Loki (21160)** "cannot take damage while a side scheme is in play": any side scheme, the campaign's Open the
  Dungeons included. His "would take damage" interrupts (Master of Illusions) do not open while he cannot take damage.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-28)

- **The victory condition** is chosen in game by the first player as the scenario starts, 1 to 4 Lokis (rookie,
  standard, expert, heroic), whatever the game's difficulty; the board shows the Lokis in the victory display against
  it.
- **"Would take damage" windows** open only for damage the villain can take: none while he cannot take damage (21160
  with a side scheme in play), none a tough status card prevents. So Master of Illusions does not trigger then (an
  engine-wide fix, not Loki's alone).

Adopted readings (recommended, not ruled otherwise):

- **Order at Loki's defeat** (1B's Forced Interrupt, Jormungand's, Victory 1 and When Defeated are all "when
  defeated" interrupts): his "when defeated" window first (Jormungand), then the victory display and win check, the
  next Loki, then the side scheme discarded for and revealed.
- **Swap keeps his damage** (RR "Swap": the hit point dial stays), though the rulebook names only attachments and
  status cards. Advancing to a set-aside Loki sets the new one's hit points to its own (20[per hero]).
- **Who reveals** the side scheme (Loki's When Defeated) and the Infinity Stone (a side scheme's When Defeated): the
  active player.
- **A swap with no set-aside Loki** does nothing (RR "Swap" cannot be completed).
- **Master of Illusions** answers damage from an attack, its overkill included (RR Overkill: "considered damage from
  an attack"); with a treachery discarded, every instance of damage that attack deals Loki is prevented until the
  attack ends (the window after the attack).
- **"Loki's attacks gain piercing" [star]** on 21164 is the piercing keyword on his card.
- **MarvelCDB typos**: Loki's Staff's boost "Attack this card to Loki" is "Attach"; Infinite Mischief's "it's" is
  "its".
- The Enchantress set's readings (Beguiled, Enchantress's search, Seduced) are written up with its implementation
  (shared with Hela's Fallen Warrior).

### Enchantress (implemented with Hela)

- **Enchantress** searches the encounter deck, its discard pile and the set-aside area for Seduced and puts it into
  play, attached to the revealing player's identity as its own text says (it is attached, not revealed); none found,
  nothing happens; the deck is shuffled.
- **Beguiled**: of every player's allies, the one with the highest printed cost (a tie: the revealing player
  chooses) that can have encounter cards attached (not Odin); none, it gains surge and is discarded. The ally does
  not leave or enter play: it is a minion engaged with its controller, its damage and status cards kept, the cards
  attached to it discarded, its printed traits kept and Enthralled gained, its keywords and abilities blanked, its SCH
  its printed THW. Defeated, the card goes to its owner's discard pile. Beguiled leaving play first, the card is its
  owner's ally again, its damage kept (as Hela's Fallen Warrior).
- **Seduced** keeps the identity from basic attacks, the stunned player's attempt to remove the stun included, and
  from playing Attack events; its allies still attack.
