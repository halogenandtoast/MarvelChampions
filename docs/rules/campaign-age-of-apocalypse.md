# Age of Apocalypse campaign and side missions

Reference: official [MC45 rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mc45_age_of_apocalypse_rulebook.pdf), pp. 4–6, 9, 12, 14, 16, 19–20, and the campaign log on p. 24. The locally transcribed Rules Reference v1.8 and its errata take precedence. In particular, Mission Team's discount lasts **this phase**.

## Campaign progression

Play Unus, Four Horsemen, Apocalypse, Dark Beast, and En Sabah Nur in that order. Players keep the same identities throughout, but may adjust their legal decks and aspects between games. After a loss, retry the same scenario without recording victory benefits. Campaign-only cards cannot be included in ordinary decks.

Before each of the first four scenarios, shuffle the Age of Apocalypse encounter set into the encounter deck. Randomly select an unused mission from Liberate the Seattle Core, Evacuate Survivors, Sabotage the Sea Wall, and Find Lost Mutants. Follow its setup column in the campaign log. Randomly select an undefeated Overseer, and put it in the mission area with the selected mission. The first player controls the Mission Team support, on its Mission side. Each player searches their deck for an ally to add to hand before drawing their opening hand: that ally counts toward the opening hand size.

After victory, mark the starting mission used whether it succeeded or failed, and apply the appropriate campaign-log column. An Overseer is removed from the available pool only if its Overseer face was defeated. Defeating its Prelate reverse face in the Apocalypse scenario does not remove the Overseer from the campaign.

In the final scenario, use Protect the Professor rather than a random mission. Set up an available Overseer, Mission Team, and starting ally searches normally. Professor X cannot enter play during this game, including by effects that put allies directly into play. Winning the scenario wins the campaign only if Protect the Professor succeeded; otherwise the campaign is lost.

## Expert campaign

Record each surviving identity's remaining hit points after victory, capped at its base hit points. In the next game, restore that recorded amount rather than automatically healing fully. During setup each player may place three threat on the current mission to heal fully.

An eliminated player receives no victory rewards. They can rejoin in the next scenario by placing three threat on that mission and returning at full hit points. In expert campaign, the ally selected during setup must share a trait with the player's **hero**, rather than merely their current alter-ego.

## The mission area

The mission area is separate from every player's play area. Its cards are in play but no player controls them. They are immune to card abilities that do not explicitly refer to the mission area. In particular, normal damage effects and thwart abilities cannot target the mission's enemies or scheme, normal ally actions cannot use its allies, and its allies do not count against any player's ally limit. Mission enemies are not engaged with players and do not activate during the ordinary villain phase.

While an unfinished mission is present, a player playing an ally must select their own play area or the mission area. This decision precedes resource payment, because Mission Team can discount the next ally played to the mission by two this phase. That discount does not reduce an ally played under ordinary player control. It expires at phase end and is consumed by the next successful mission ally play. Effects that merely put an ally into play do not constitute playing it and do not create this destination choice.

Allies at the mission have their printed text blanked except for traits. Printed enters-play responses, toughness, and action abilities therefore do not operate there. Upgrades may nevertheless be attached to mission allies. The upgrades' applicable stat modifiers affect mission attempts. Desperate Measures adds a wild icon for mission resource matching **in addition to** the ally's original printed icon. It does not make the ally a resource-generating ability.

Physical ownership is preserved. A card leaving the mission normally goes to its owner's discard pile; mission success/failure instructions can instead shuffle or remove it. When an owner is eliminated, their mission cards are placed in their discard pile before their game zones are removed, including cards held facedown by an Overseer. Attached upgrades and facedown player cards held by an Overseer are also player cards at the mission.

## Mission attempts

Mission Team exhausts for either its phase-limited discount or a mission attempt. The player making the attempt discards one card from the top of their deck for each ally currently at the mission. Apply any Overseer's Mission Response after that discard and before assigning cards.

Assign discarded cards to different mission allies. An ally participates if any resource icon on its assigned card matches an icon on that ally. A wild icon on either card can match any icon on the other. Multiple resource icons on a card do not make it count as multiple assigned cards or allow it to be assigned to multiple allies. The discarded cards return to their discard pile after assignment, except cards a response attaches elsewhere.

Add the modified ATK of participating allies to form a damage pool. Assign that damage to mission enemies one enemy at a time, choosing the amount spent from the pool on each target. An Overseer cannot take damage while any other mission minion remains. After dealing damage, remove threat equal to the participating allies' modified THW. A mission at zero threat cannot be defeated while a mission minion remains; it can be completed after the last minion is defeated.

After an unsuccessful attempt resolves, put one attempt counter on the mission and deal one damage to each ally still there. Upon the fourth unsuccessful attempt, remove Mission Team and flip the mission to its Finished side. On success, shuffle all player cards at the mission into their owners' decks, flip Mission Team, and flip the mission. The Finished mission's response removes every remaining mission-area card, including the Finished mission itself, and applies its immediate success or failure result. The saved mission state retains the outcome for the campaign log.

A completed Mission Team remains under the first player's control and cannot be discarded. Its action exhausts it to let a chosen player draw one card. Both support faces follow the first player token.

## Overseers

Each Overseer has five hit points per player and victory 5. All are protected from damage while another mission minion remains.

| Overseer | Mission effect |
|---|---|
| Mister Sinister | Assigned cards cannot share any printed resource icon with another assigned card. The player may leave a discarded card unassigned when necessary, allowing a later card to use that icon. |
| The Shadow King | Each mental resource icon discarded places two threat on the mission. |
| Abyss | Each discarded card showing a wild icon is attached facedown to Abyss and cannot be assigned that attempt. |
| Sugar Man | Each physical icon discarded heals him for three. |
| Mikhail Rasputin | Choose one mission ally and deal it damage equal to the number of printed energy icons discarded, as one damage instance. Resolve this before assigning cards; an ally defeated by this response cannot participate. |

A printed double resource counts twice for the relevant Overseer response. Conditional resource generation does not count as a printed icon: The Power of the Mind has only one printed mental, The Power of Flight only one printed energy, and Band Together no printed icons. Wild icons do not imitate mental, physical, or energy for these responses; their wildcard permission applies to resource matching during assignment.

## Mission results

| Mission | Setup | Immediate success | Immediate failure | Campaign success | Campaign failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liberate the Seattle Core | Set Desperate Measures copies aside. | Each player receives one copy in hand. | Place two threat per player on the main scheme. | Each player may include one copy in future games, exempt from minimum deck size. | Remove those copies from the campaign. |
| Evacuate Survivors | Each player shuffles Panicked Refugees into their deck. | Each player searches deck and discard for a card to add to hand. | Deal each player an encounter card. | Remove Refugees from the campaign; each player may include one chosen upgrade of any aspect in future games, exempt from minimum deck size. | Refugees must be shuffled into each player's deck in future games. |
| Sabotage the Sea Wall | Shuffle North American Sea Wall into the encounter deck. | Remove that wall and let each player deal three damage to an enemy. | Find and reveal the wall. | Remove the wall from the campaign; each player may include one chosen support of any aspect in future games, exempt from minimum deck size. | Shuffle the wall into future encounter decks during setup. |
| Find Lost Mutants | Set the four campaign allies aside. | Each player takes a distinct available campaign ally into hand. | Each player discards a card. | Each player chooses a campaign ally they may include in future games, exempt from minimum deck size. | Remove all campaign allies from the campaign. |
| Protect the Professor | Final scenario only; Professor X cannot enter play. | Each player searches deck and discard for an ally to add to hand. | The players lose. | Completing this mission and winning the scenario saves Professor X and wins the campaign. | Winning the scenario without completing it still loses the campaign. |

## Other campaign cards

Agent of Apocalypse has guard. When revealed, choose to move it to the mission or let it activate against the revealing player. Its boost deals one damage to a mission ally and gives the activating enemy another boost card.

Worldwide Crisis offers three mission threat, or one identity damage followed by surge. Its boost places one mission threat and gives the enemy another boost card.

North American Sea Wall has two starting threat, hinder two per player, surge, and victory 2. Its constant effect protects the villain from damage until it leaves play. Its boost deals the actual card as a facedown encounter card.

Panicked Refugees reveals itself when it enters hand and draws a replacement card. Its owner may exhaust their alter-ego to remove the obligation from the game.

The four campaign allies have optional responses when they enter hand: Destiny removes two main-scheme threat; Blink deals two villain damage; Morph confuses the villain; X-Man gives the receiving identity tough. These responses occur from hand, rather than when the ally enters play, and may be declined. They are not offered if they cannot change the game state.

## Implementation notes and rulings

Mission state is stored with the scenario so save/reload and undo retain entity IDs, the selected mission and Overseer, assignments, attempts, and the current damage pool. The public board renders the same physical entities in a separate mission area. All randomness uses the game's stored generator.

No unresolved mission card ruling was identified in the official rulebook or RR v1.8. Campaign progression and permanent rewards are implemented by the campaign module; mission attempts and immediate card effects use the mission handler.
