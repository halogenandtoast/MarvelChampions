## Rules implementation summary

Colossus can hold two tough status cards in hero form. Each positive damage instance discards only one tough to prevent its entire damage. Piercing discards all tough status cards before dealing positive attack damage. Changing to alter-ego does not itself discard existing statuses.

Discarding a tough as a cost is distinct from having a tough prevent damage, but both satisfy Iron Will and Organic Steel. Each discarded status creates its own response opportunity. Organic Steel must exhaust and spend one of its two steel counters; its last use discards it.

Steel Fist uses the current erratum: its ability is an attack. It deals its damage first; its optional tough discard is an effect, followed by stun and confuse, rather than a play cost. Armor Up uses the erratum “would activate”: resolve the form change before selecting whether the villain attacks or schemes.

Team-Up requires both named characters in play. A Colossus identity and a Colossus ally cannot coexist under the unique rule, and the equivalent restriction applies to Shadowcat.

## Piercing and tough-discard responses

<!-- RR v1.8 Piercing / Tough; official designer ruling 138(4), Alex, 2022-09-16 -->

Resolve positive piercing attack damage in this order: discard all existing tough status cards; resolve responses to those discards; then apply the pending damage. Iron Will therefore draws before Colossus loses hit points, with one response opportunity per discarded tough.

Organic Steel can supply a new tough during those response windows. Piercing has already resolved and does not discard that replacement again. The replacement tough prevents the pending damage normally and is then discarded, creating a further tough-discard response opportunity. Organic Steel remains exhausted, so that second discard cannot use the same copy again.

Source: Alex's official designer answer, September 16, 2022, [Hall of Heroes ruling 138, question 4](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/latest-ffg-rulings/#138). Apply that answer's replacement-tough timing together with current RR v1.8. Its older statements about zero-damage attacks are superseded: current Piercing does not discard tough when the attack would deal zero damage.
