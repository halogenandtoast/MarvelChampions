## Summary (non-normative)

```
Villain: Hela (A1, Mystic) / (A2, Wounded) on one card; expert (B1)/(B2). Mystic: +1 SCH, +1 ATK and +2[pp] (B: +3[pp])
  hit points for each side scheme in the victory display. Wounded: ∞ hit points; after any side scheme is defeated,
  flip to Mystic. Flipping either way is like advancing a stage: hit points reset, status cards stay.
Main scheme: Odin's Torment (1A/1B; 18[pp], start 1[pp], +1[pp]). Hela would be defeated while Odin is attached here:
  discard her attachments and flip her to Wounded instead. Completed: the players lose.
Setup: Odin (Captive side) attached to the main scheme; Gnipahellir and Garm revealed; Gjallerbru, Skurge, Hall of
  Nastrond and Nidhogg set aside.
Chain: Gnipahellir defeated -> Gjallerbru + Skurge; Gjallerbru defeated -> Hall of Nastrond + Nidhogg; Hall of Nastrond
  defeated -> the first player takes control of Odin. Each guard minion engages the first player and locks its scheme.
Win: Hela defeated with Odin not attached to the main scheme (the players control the Odin ally).
Lose: the main scheme completed, or Odin (Captive side) leaving play.
```

## Scenario
<!-- MTS rulebook p.20 -->

***Against all odds, Thanos has been defeated and the Infinity Gauntlet wrested from his hand! The combined power of
the Guardians of the Galaxy and the Avengers was just enough to save the universe from the Mad Titan.***

***The heroes all breathe a huge sigh of relief, but they have to decide what to do with the Infinity Gauntlet.
Tensions rise among the super-powered friends as an impassioned debate breaks out. Before the argument can spiral
further out of control, the enigmatic Adam Warlock steps forward to offer his stewardship. His unique connection to
the Soul Stone makes him the ideal candidate to safeguard the Gauntlet. The gathered Avengers and Guardians agree to
this, and the Gauntlet is entrusted to Warlock's care.***

***Relieved to have the mattered settled, the exhausted heroes move to the lounge to eat and celebrate their victory.
After they leave, the stoic figure of Adam Warlock is replaced by the grinning God of Mischief. Loki used his magic
arts to impersonate Warlock and fool the heroes into giving him the Infinity Gauntlet!***

***The trickster uses the power of the Gauntlet to enact the final part of his plan: to usurp the Throne of Asgard
from Odin. He casts the All-Father down to Hel, consigning him to the cruel ministrations of Hela, goddess of death.
Only the stalwart watchman of Asgard, Heimdall, escapes to bring news of Loki's treachery to Avengers Tower. He warns
that only Odin has the power to defeat Loki and insists that the heroes journey into Hel to rescue the All-Father
from Hela's grasp.***

**Villain Deck**: Hela (A)

*Remove Hela (A) and add Hela (B) for expert mode.*

**Main Scheme Deck**: Odin's Torment

**Encounter Deck**: Hela, Legions of Hel, Frost Giants, and Standard sets. (The Standard set can be found in the
*Marvel Champions* core set.)

*The Legions of Hel and Frost Giants sets can be removed from this scenario and/or added to other scenarios when
using the scenario customization rules.*

## Infinite Hit Points
<!-- MTS rulebook p.20 -->

In this scenario, the villain has infinite hit points (∞). A character with infinite hit points cannot be defeated by
taking damage. However, damage may still be dealt to that character through attacks and card abilities.

## Journey Through Hel
<!-- MTS rulebook p.20 -->

Hela is the Asgardian goddess of death and that makes her nearly impossible to defeat. Without the aid of Odin the
All-Father of Asgard, the heroes would never be able to vanquish the goddess of Hel. Unfortunately for the heroes,
Odin begins the game as a ***Captive*** attached to the main scheme. If the players want to defeat Hela, they must
rescue Odin first.

To help bring Hela's formidable tenacity to the game, she is represented by a single double-sided villain card. She
begins the game on her ***Mystic*** side. When Hela would be defeated, the players must resolve the **Forced
Interrupt** on stage 1B, Odin's Torment: "When Hela would be defeated, if Odin is attached to this scheme, discard
each attachment from Hela and flip her to her ***Wounded*** side instead." Flipping Hela from her ***Mystic*** side to
her ***Wounded*** side and vice versa is resolved just like advancing to the next villain stage: her hit points are
reset and any status cards attached to Hela remain attached.

**If the players control the Odin ally when Hela is defeated, they win the game.**

## Keywords used here
<!-- MTS rulebook p.13 -->

**Hinder X**: When a card with the hinder X keyword is revealed, place X threat on that card.

**Victory X**: When a card with the victory X keyword is defeated, place it in the victory display instead of its
owner's discard pile. The victory display is a named, out-of-play game area shared by all players. Cards in the
victory display follow the standard rules for out-of-play cards.

## Campaign Instructions
<!-- MTS rulebook p.21 -->

### Setup

- Put the "Find the Norn Stones" side scheme into play.
- Shuffle the "Summoned Back" treachery into the encounter deck.
- If Shawarma is in the campaign pool, each player shuffles one copy of Shawarma into their deck.
- If System Shock is in the campaign pool, each player shuffles one copy of System Shock into their deck.
- If The Infinity Stones 1B was completed, each player discards the top half of their deck.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Set each player's hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign
  log for the previous scenario.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Each player may place one acceleration token on the main scheme to heal their identity to
  its full hit point value.

### Victory

- If Find the Norn Stones was defeated, add Norn Stone to the campaign pool.
- If Retrieve Odin's Armor is in the victory display, add Odin to the campaign pool.
- **Expert Campaign Only**: Record each identity's remaining hit points in the campaign log.

## How the engine reads these rules

- **One card, two faces.** Hela (A1/A2, or B1/B2) is one villain whose face flips. Either flip keeps her status
  cards, attachments, upgrades and stage, as advancing does, and then resets her hit point dial: to ∞ on the Wounded
  side (infinite hit points, as the Collector's Wounded sides), and on the Mystic side to her printed hit points plus
  +2[per hero] (B: +3[per hero]) for each side scheme then in the victory display.
- **"Gets +X hit points for each side scheme in the victory display"**: a side scheme reaching the victory display
  while Hela is Mystic raises her dial (and maximum) by 2[per hero] (B: 3[per hero]) at once (RR Hit Points). Her +1
  SCH and +1 ATK per side scheme are counted whenever she schemes or attacks. "Side schemes in the victory display" are
  the side scheme cards there, the campaign's Retrieve Odin's Armor among them; minions there (Garm, Skurge, Nidhogg)
  are not counted.
- **Odin's Torment's Forced Interrupt** is answered as Hela would be defeated, while Odin is attached to the main
  scheme: the window after her defeat is cancelled, each attachment on her is discarded (a permanent one stays), and
  she flips to Wounded.
- **Wounded's Forced Response** answers any side scheme defeated, of any set (Legions of Hel, Unnatural Storm, the
  campaign's, a nemesis set's), after its When Defeated ability and after it has gone to the victory display.
- **Odin attached** is a card held by the main scheme, faceup: he is no ally in play and nobody controls him (he is
  not exhausted by Unnatural Storm, cannot be chosen, damaged or defended with). Hall of Nastrond's When Defeated
  takes him from the main scheme and puts him into play as an ally under the first player's control, his owner too
  (RR Ownership and Control: a scenario-specific player card).
- **"The first player gains control of Odin"**: he goes to the first player's play area as the first player token
  passes or the first player is eliminated (RR Player Elimination: the token passes first, so Odin goes with it and
  does not leave play). He does not count against the ally limit, and no upgrade may be played on him (Captive side:
  "cannot have cards attached"); on the King side only encounter cards are kept off him.
- **"If Odin leaves play, the players lose"** (Captive side): defeated, discarded, returned to hand or removed from
  the game. The King side's Forced Interrupt instead removes him from the game as he would leave play.
- **The win**: Hela defeated (on her Mystic side, Odin no longer attached) is the players' win, as her stage is the
  villain deck's last.
- **Side scheme chain**: each When Defeated reveals the two set-aside cards, the side scheme first; their hinder is
  placed as each side scheme is revealed. "Deal each other player 1 facedown encounter card" skips the first player;
  Hall of Nastrond deals one to each player, the first player included, after Odin is put into play.
- **"Engages the first player"** (Garm, Skurge, Nidhogg): constant. Each enters play engaged with the first player and
  re-engages the first player whenever it would be engaged with anyone else, and whenever the first player token
  passes.
- **"Threat cannot be removed from <scheme>"**: while the minion is in play, no thwart and no other effect removes
  threat from that side scheme (as Klyntar Frenzy).
- **Per-player hit points on minions** (Garm 4, Skurge 5, Nidhogg 6, each [per hero]).
- **Hela's Crown**: the boost card it gives stays facedown on Hela for her next activation.
- **The Wastes of Niffleheim as a boost card**: its Boost ability adds as many boost icons to the activation as there
  are side schemes in the victory display.
- **Hela's Domain's boost** places its 2 threat if an ally is defeated by this activation's attack damage (once, for
  the attack).
- **Fallen Warrior**: discarding until an ally, the ally discarded is put into play as a minion engaged with the
  player, Fallen Warrior attached. The minion is the player's card (it goes to its owner's discard pile when it leaves
  play), with SCH equal to the ally's printed THW, ATK its printed ATK and hit points its printed hit points; its text
  box is blank (no abilities, no keywords: Toughness on an ally is not used), and it keeps its printed traits and
  gains ***Undead*** (traits are not part of the text box, RR Traits). It takes no consequential damage (a minion never
  does). With no ally in the deck before it empties, or with the ally matching a unique card in play (RR Unique: it
  cannot be put into play), Fallen Warrior is discarded. If Fallen Warrior leaves play while the minion stays, the card
  reverts to its owner as an ally in play, keeping its damage (RR Ownership and Control).
- **No Place for the Living**: a player with an upgrade or support may discard the highest-cost one (a tie: they
  choose which) or take damage equal to their upgrades and supports; with none, they take 0 damage. Each player in
  turn order, starting with the first player.
- **Unnatural Storm**: an identity in hero form or an ally that would be readied by a player card's ability is not;
  the ready phase and encounter cards still ready them.
- **Frozen**: the attached identity cannot ready, by any means (as All Tied Up).
- **Laufey**: stuns the character his attack damaged (a tough status card taking the damage is no damage).

## Campaign hooks (for the campaign agent)

- Odin's two faces are one ally: Retrieve Odin's Armor's "flip him to his King side" flips the ally in play.
- `sideSchemesInVictoryDisplay` counts the campaign side schemes there too.
- Find the Norn Stones reads Hela's ***Wounded*** trait: her current face's printed traits.
- The campaign Setup and Victory steps above are the campaign planner's.

## Open rulings

- **Odin attached: in play?** Read as a held card, no ally in play (see above). The card says "attached", which would
  put him in play; no ability of these sets needs him as a character while attached, and treating him as one would let
  Unnatural Storm exhaust him and the players defend with him.
- **"Engages the first player" as the token passes.** Read as constant: the minion follows the first player. The
  alternative (only as it enters play) is equally short to implement; the card's wording is a constant ability.
- **Hela's hit points as a side scheme reaches the victory display while she is Mystic.** Read per RR Hit Points:
  the dial goes up at once.
- **Fallen Warrior leaving play first.** Read per RR Ownership and Control as the card reverting to its owner, an
  ally in play. Discarding it instead would also be defensible; no card of these sets causes it.

## Rulings (from the project owner, 2026-10-28)

No ruling of the project owner's concerns these sets. The orchestrator's: "simultaneous forced abilities with the
same timing: the first player orders them" (RR Simultaneous Resolution) applies as everywhere. Adopted readings (the
recommendations above, not ruled otherwise):

- **Odin attached** is a card Odin's Torment holds faceup, no ally in play, controlled by no one; the board shows him
  on the main scheme.
- **"Engages the first player"** (Garm, Skurge, Nidhogg) is constant: engaged with anyone else, the minion engages
  the first player; it follows the first player token.
- **Hela's hit points** go up at once (dial and maximum) as a side scheme reaches the victory display while she is
  Mystic; each flip resets them, the Mystic side's with the victory display's bonus.
- **Attachments on Hela** stay as Wounded flips back to Mystic (RR Flip); only Odin's Torment's interrupt discards
  them, a permanent one excepted.
- **Wounded's Forced Response** answers any side scheme's defeat, of any set.
- **Odin and player elimination**: the token passes first, Odin goes to the new first player, not leaving play. A
  player over the ally limit discards an ally counted against it (Odin is not).
- **Fallen Warrior**: printed traits kept and Undead gained, keywords and abilities blanked; with no ally before the
  deck runs out, or the ally kept out by a matching unique card in play, it is discarded with nothing attached;
  leaving play while its minion stays, the card is its owner's ally again, its damage kept.
- **The Wastes of Niffleheim as a boost card** gains its boost icons as its icons are counted (the window to counting
  them), so an amplify icon and other counting effects see them as the card's.
- **Hela's Domain's boost** places its 2 threat once for the attack.
- **No Place for the Living** with nothing controlled: the damage option, at 0; a permanent card is not one to
  discard. Each player chooses in player order.
- **Game areas** (two-area play): Unnatural Storm exhausts and keeps from readying only the heroes and allies of its
  own area; Legions of Hel counts the Undead minions of its area; a guardian (Garm, Skurge, Nidhogg) locks its side
  scheme only from its area; Beguiled picks among the revealing player's area's allies.
- **"Discard each attachment from Hela"** (Odin's Torment): every card attached to her, a player's upgrade too.
- **King Odin leaving play** is removed from the game instead: he never reaches a discard pile or a hand.
- **An ally treated as a minion** keeps its status cards both ways; Beguiled's minion at its printed hit points with
  as much damage is defeated (RR Hit Points).
- **Unnatural Storm** stops a player card's readying of a hero (hero form) or an ally; the ready phase and encounter
  cards still ready them. **Frozen** stops all readying of its identity.
- **"Cannot have cards attached"** is checked for every encounter card attaching to an ally: one that would attach to
  Odin is discarded instead, and a card choosing a character to attach to (Restrained's "highest ATK") chooses among
  those it can attach to.
- **Frozen in Time** (Kang) with Unnatural Storm: a player card that cannot ready the hero is no "would ready", so it
  stays; the ready phase still makes it discard itself.
- **Flipping Odin** (the campaign's Retrieve Odin's Armor) flips the ally in play (RR Flip): the same card, his damage,
  status cards, counters, upgrades and attachments kept, the King side's text from then on.
